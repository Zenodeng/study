5.1 固体形变与弹簧伸长实验 (Forces acting on solids)

Stretching, Compressing, Bending, Elastic vs Plastic Deformation & Paper 6 Spring Apparatus
Core

5.1.1 形变方式与弹性 vs 塑性形变 Core

外力作用于固体时，可以改变物体的尺寸与形状（拉伸 stretching、压缩 compressing、弯曲 bending、扭转 twisting）。根据撤去外力后能否复原，分为两类：

  • 弹性形变（Elastic deformation）：撤去外力（load removed）后，物体能够完全恢复原来的长度与形状（如正常范围内的金属弹簧、橡皮筋）。
  • 塑性/非弹性形变（Plastic / inelastic deformation）：超过限度后即使撤去外力，物体也无法恢复原长，留有永久伸长量（permanent extension）（如被过度拉直的圆珠笔弹簧、橡皮泥、过度弯折的铜线）。

5.1.2 测定弹簧伸长量实验 (Paper 6 标准装置与防误差措施) Core

伸长量 Extension x = 总长度 l − 原长 l₀   |   砝码重力 F = mg (g = 9.8 N/kg)

Paper 6 五步标准操作与控制细节：
1. 用铁架台垂直固定米尺，并用直角三角尺（set square）核对尺身与桌面垂直（90°）；
2. 在弹簧底端水平粘贴一根细指针（horizontal pointer / pin），尽量贴近刻度线且读数时视线水平防视差（eye level perpendicular to scale）；
3. 先不挂砝码读出弹簧原长 l₀；
4. 逐个挂上钩码（每次换算重力 F = m × 9.8），待静止后读出新长度 l，算出 x = l − l₀；
5. 逐个卸下钩码复测，检验弹簧是否回到 l₀（判断是否进入塑性形变）。

⚠️ 致命失分点：Extension (伸长量 x) 绝不等于 Total Length (总长度 l)！

如果弹簧原长 l₀ = 12.0 cm，挂 4.0 N 时总长为 16.0 cm，此时伸长量只有 x = 16.0 − 12.0 = 4.0 cm！若题目问“挂 8.0 N 时弹簧的总长度是多少？”，伸长量翻倍为 8.0 cm，总长度 = 12.0 + 8.0 = 20.0 cm（绝不是 16.0 × 2 = 32.0 cm！）。

5.2 拉伸弹簧与三类材料载荷–伸长曲线 (Stretching springs)

Load–Extension Graphs, Limit of Proportionality, Elastic Limit, Rubber Band & Copper Wire
Core

5.2.1 金属弹簧、橡皮筋与铜丝的 F–x 图像对比 Core

实验材料 图像曲线特征 核心物理结论（考纲必记）
金属弹簧
(Metal spring)		 从原点 (0, 0) 出发的一条倾斜直线，到达某拐点后向上弯曲（F 在纵轴）或向右侧平缓弯曲（x 在纵轴）。 直线段严格遵守正比关系，直至到达比例极限（Limit of proportionality）；接近或略高处为弹性极限（Elastic limit），越过即产生永久形变。
橡皮筋
(Rubber band)		 全程呈 S 形非线性曲线，没有直线正比段；卸载曲线低于加载曲线（形成滞回环）。 不遵守胡克定律；加载做功多、卸载放回少，差值转化为橡皮筋内部的热能（升温）。
金属铜丝
(Copper wire)		 初始是一段极陡峭的短直线（极小拉力几乎难拉长），随后突然发生大幅塑性拉伸直至拉断。 延展性极强，一旦超过屈服点便发生不可逆的塑性颈缩伸长。
💡 考场盯死坐标轴！F 在纵轴还是 x 在纵轴？

CIE 试卷中两种画法都频繁出现：
• 若纵轴是 Load F，横轴是 Extension x：超过比例极限后，弹簧变软容易拉长，曲线会向下弯平（斜率变小）；
• 若纵轴是 Extension x，横轴是 Load F：超过比例极限后，同样拉力增幅带来更大伸长，曲线会向上陡弯（斜率变大）！

5.3 胡克定律与劲度系数 (Hooke's law & spring constant)

F = kx, Spring Constant Definition k = F/x, Graph Gradient & Series/Parallel Springs
Extended Only

本节公式 F = kx 与劲度系数计算属于 Extended 扩展考纲内容（0625 Supplement）。Core 考生只需掌握 5.1–5.2 图像定性规律。

5.3.1 胡克定律 (Hooke's Law) 与劲度系数 k Extended

F = kx   |   劲度系数 k = Fx   (单位: N/m 或 N/cm)

• 劲度系数定义（1 分关键词）：产生单位伸长量所需的力（force per unit extension）；k 越大代表弹簧越“硬”（stiffer）。
• 图像斜率法求 k：在 F–x 图像（F 为纵轴）直线段内，斜率 Gradient = F / x = k；若题目把 x 放在纵轴、F 放在横轴，则 k = 1 / Gradient！
• 串并联拓展直觉：两根相同弹簧串联挂重物 F 时，每根都承受拉力 F ⇒ 总伸长量翻倍为 2x（等效 k_eff = k/2）；并联挂重物 F 时，各分担一半力 F/2 ⇒ 伸长量减半为 x/2（等效 k_eff = 2k）。

📘 例题 5.3 · 胡克定律计算 k 与新载荷下弹簧总长 (g = 9.8 N/kg)

A spring has an unstretched length of 14.0 cm. When a mass of 0.40 kg is hung from it, the total length becomes 22.0 cm. (g = 9.8 N/kg)
(a) Calculate the spring constant k in N/cm and in N/m.
(b) Calculate the total length of the spring when a load of 4.41 N is hung (assuming the limit of proportionality is not exceeded).

解析 (Solution)：
(a) 先算载荷与伸长量：F = mg = 0.40 × 9.8 = 3.92 N；x = 22.0 − 14.0 = 8.0 cm。
k = 3.92 N ÷ 8.0 cm = 0.49 N/cm（换算为国际单位：0.49 N ÷ 0.01 m = 49 N/m）。
(b) 新伸长量：x₂ = F₂ / k = 4.41 ÷ 0.49 = 9.0 cm；
弹簧总长度：l₂ = l₀ + x₂ = 14.0 + 9.0 = 23.0 cm。

5.4 固体压强与应用 (Pressure p = F/A)

Force per Unit Area, Pascal (Pa = N/m²), Area Conversion cm² → m² & Everyday Applications
Core

5.4.1 压强公式、帕斯卡 (Pa) 与面积换算陷阱 Core

p = FA   |   1 Pa = 1 N/m²   |   1 kPa = 1000 Pa   |   1 cm² = 10⁻⁴ m²

压强（Pressure）定义为垂直作用在单位面积上的力（force per unit area acting normal to a surface）。
• 2023–2028 考纲重要变更提醒：水银气压计（Barometer）与 U 型管压强计（Manometer）的推导已从 2023 新纲移除，考场核心聚焦于固体压强 p = F/A 与液体压强公式 Δp = ρgΔh！

工程目标 面积调节策略 典型实例与考场标准表述 (Mark Scheme)
增大压强
(切割/刺穿/扎入)		 相同力 F 下，尽量减小接触面积 A 菜刀开刃、图钉尖端、缝衣针、冰刀、高跟鞋细跟 ⇒ 极小面积 A 产生极大压强 p = F/A 轻易切入物体。
减小压强
(防陷入松软地面)		 相同重力 F 下，尽量增大接触面积 A 雪地靴/滑雪板、坦克与挖掘机宽履带（caterpillar tracks）、沙漠骆驼宽掌、大厦宽阔地基 ⇒ 大面积分担重力，减小对松软雪/泥的压强以防下陷。
📘 例题 5.4 · 四足站立与 cm² 单位换算压强计算 (g = 9.8 N/kg)

(a) An elephant of mass 4000 kg stands on all four feet, each of contact area 0.098 m². Calculate the pressure on the ground (g = 9.8 N/kg).
(b) A dancer of mass 50 kg balances momentarily on one shoe heel of area 0.50 cm². Calculate the pressure exerted on the floor in Pa.

解析 (Solution)：
(a) 总重力 F = 4000 × 9.8 = 39 200 N；四只脚总接触面积 A = 4 × 0.098 = 0.392 m²；
p = 39 200 ÷ 0.392 = 100 000 Pa（即 100 kPa）。
(b) 重力 F = 50 × 9.8 = 490 N；务必先换算面积为 m²：A = 0.50 cm² = 0.50 × 10⁻⁴ m² = 5.0 × 10⁻⁵ m²；
p = 490 ÷ (5.0 × 10⁻⁵) = 9.8 × 10⁶ Pa（高达 9.8 兆帕，远大于大象！）。

5.5 液体压强与液压原理 (Calculating pressure in liquids)

Δp = ρgΔh (g = 9.8), Independence from Shape, Dams & Hydraulic Press Link
Extended: Δp = ρgΔh 定量计算

本节定量公式 Δp = ρgΔh 属于 Extended 扩展考纲内容（0625 Supplement）；Core 考生需定性理解液体压强随深度与密度增大。

5.5.1 液体压强公式 Δp = ρgΔh 与四大铁律 Extended

Δp = ρgΔh   (g = 9.8 m/s², ρ 单位 kg/m³, Δh 为从液面算起的垂直深度 m)

• 绝对总压强 vs 附加压强：Δp = ρgΔh 算的是液柱自身产生的压强增量；若题问水下绝对总压强（total pressure），必须加上液面上的大气压强：p_total = p_atm + ρgΔh！
• 四大铁律（选择简答必考）：
1. 只与垂直深度 Δh（从自由液面往下量，而不是离容器底的距离！）和液体密度 ρ、g 有关；
2. 与容器的形状、粗细、横截面积或装水总体积完全无关（连通器原理：不同形状的连通管装同种液体时液面等高）；
3. 同一深度处液体压强向各个方向大小完全相等（向下、向上、侧向均同为 ρgΔh）；
4. 水库大坝（Dam）上窄下宽：越深处液体压强越大（Δp ∝ Δh），水对坝底产生巨大侧推力，故底部必须修建得极其厚实。

🔬 拓展串联：液压千斤顶与刹车系统 (Hydraulic Press)

由于液体几乎不可压缩，封闭液体内施加的压强会大小不变地传递到各处：p = F₁ / A₁ = F₂ / A₂。在小面积主活塞 A₁ 施加较小的力 F₁，即可在大面积副活塞 A₂ 上产生成比例放大的巨大举升力 F₂ = F₁ × (A₂ / A₁)！

📘 例题 5.5 · 潜艇深海压强与舱门总压力计算 (g = 9.8 m/s²)

A submarine dives to a depth of 120 m in seawater of density 1025 kg/m³. Atmospheric pressure at the sea surface is 101 000 Pa, and g = 9.8 m/s².
(a) Calculate the pressure due to the seawater alone at 120 m.
(b) State the total pressure on the outside of the submarine hull.
(c) The pressure inside the submarine is maintained at 101 000 Pa. Calculate the net force acting on a circular hatch of area 0.80 m².

解析 (Solution)：
(a) 海水压强：Δp = ρ g Δh = 1025 × 9.8 × 120 = 1 205 400 Pa（≈ 1.21 MPa）。
(b) 外壳总绝对压强：p_total = 101 000 + 1 205 400 = 1 306 400 Pa（≈ 1.31 MPa）。
(c) 舱门内外压强差正好抵消大气压，净压强差 Δp = 1 205 400 Pa；
净压力：F = Δp × A = 1 205 400 × 0.80 = 964 320 N（≈ 9.64 × 10⁵ N）。

SME 1 · 弹簧伸长实验与胡克定律综合计算 (g = 9.8 N/kg)Core Extended [7 marks]
A spring has an unstretched length of 15.0 cm. When a mass of 0.30 kg is hung on it, the total length becomes 21.0 cm. (g = 9.8 N/kg)
(a) Calculate the weight of the 0.30 kg mass and the extension of the spring. [2]
(b) Ext Calculate the spring constant k in N/cm and in N/m. [2]
(c) Ext Calculate the total length of the spring when a load of 4.41 N is hung from it (limit of proportionality not exceeded). [2]
(d) Explain what happens if the spring is stretched far beyond its elastic limit and the load is then removed. [1]
原长 15.0 cm 弹簧挂 0.30 kg 后总长 21.0 cm，求载荷、劲度系数 k（双单位）、挂 4.41 N 时的总长及超弹性极限后的表现。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 7 Marks)

B1 (a) Load F = 0.30 × 9.8 = 2.94 N; B1 Extension x = 21.0 − 15.0 = 6.0 cm.

B1 (b) k = F / x = 2.94 ÷ 6.0 = 0.49 N/cm; B1 = 49 N/m (2.94 ÷ 0.060).

C1 (c) x₂ = 4.41 ÷ 0.49 = 9.0 cm; A1 Total length = 15.0 + 9.0 = 24.0 cm.

B1 (d) The spring undergoes plastic deformation / retains a permanent extension (does not return to 15.0 cm).

中文精析：第 (c) 问算出的 9.0 cm 只是伸长量，必须加上原长 15.0 cm 才能拿到总长度的 A1 分！

SME 2 · 长方体极值压强计算与宽履带应用 (g = 9.8 N/kg)Core [6 marks]
A rectangular concrete block of mass 300 kg has dimensions 1.5 m × 0.40 m × 0.25 m. (g = 9.8 N/kg)
(a) Calculate the weight of the concrete block. [1]
(b) Calculate the maximum pressure and the minimum pressure the block can exert when resting flat on horizontal ground. [4]
(c) Explain why heavy bulldozers are fitted with wide caterpillar tracks when working on soft mud. [1]
300 kg 水泥块尺寸 1.5 m × 0.40 m × 0.25 m，求其放在地面上产生的最大压强与最小压强，并解释履带减压原理。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 6 Marks)

B1 (a) W = 300 × 9.8 = 2940 N.

C1 (b) Maximum pressure uses smallest face area A_min = 0.40 × 0.25 = 0.10 m² ⇒ A1 p_max = 2940 ÷ 0.10 = 29 400 Pa.
C1 Minimum pressure uses largest face area A_max = 1.5 × 0.40 = 0.60 m² ⇒ A1 p_min = 2940 ÷ 0.60 = 4900 Pa.

B1 (c) Wide tracks increase the contact area, reducing the pressure (p = F/A) on the mud so the bulldozer does not sink.

Elastic: returns to original length/shape when load is removed. Plastic: keeps a permanent extension/deformation after load removal.
C03 · 5.2 Proportionality LimitCore
Define the limit of proportionality on a load–extension graph.
默写载荷–伸长曲线上“比例极限”的标准英文定义。
👆 点击查看参考答案

C04 · 5.2 Rubber vs SpringCore
Why does a rubber band fail to obey Hooke's law, and what happens during unloading?
为什么橡皮筋不遵守胡克定律？加载与卸载路径有何特点？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Its load–extension graph is non-linear (S-shaped curve), not a straight line through the origin. Its unloading curve lies below the loading curve (energy lost as thermal energy).
C05 · 5.3 Hooke's LawExtended
State Hooke's Law in words and as an equation.
写出胡克定律的文字表述与数学表达式。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Up to the limit of proportionality, extension is directly proportional to load: F = k x.
C06 · 5.3 Spring ConstantExtended
Define the spring constant k and state its SI unit.
定义劲度系数 k 并写出其国际单位。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Spring constant k is the force per unit extension (k = F / x). SI unit: N/m (often also expressed in N/cm).
C07 · 5.3 Gradient TrapExtended
How do you obtain k from (i) a Load vs Extension graph and (ii) an Extension vs Load graph?
当坐标轴对调时，如何分别从直线斜率求劲度系数 k？
👆 点击查看参考答案
(i) If Load F is on y-axis: k = gradient. (ii) If Extension x is on y-axis: k = 1 / gradient. Always check axes!
C08 · 5.3 Series & ParallelExtended
Two identical springs are joined first in series and then in parallel under the same load F. Compare extensions.
两根相同弹簧挂同一砝码时，串联与并联的总伸长量分别是单根的几倍？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Series: each feels full load F, so total extension = 2x. Parallel: each supports F/2, so extension = x / 2.
C09 · 5.4 Pressure DefCore
Define pressure, state its formula, and define the unit pascal (Pa).
写出压强定义、公式以及单位帕斯卡（Pa）的含义。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Pressure is force per unit area (p = F / A) acting normal to a surface. 1 Pa = 1 N/m².
C10 · 5.4 Area ConversionCore
How do you convert an area in cm² into m² before calculating pressure in Pa?
算帕斯卡（Pa）压强前，如何把 cm² 换算为 m²？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Since 1 m = 100 cm, 1 m² = 10 000 cm², so divide cm² by 10 000 (multiply by 10⁻⁴).
C11 · 5.4 Reducing PressureCore
Explain why snowshoes or wide tractor tyres prevent sinking into soft ground.
从公式角度解释雪地鞋与宽轮胎为何能防止陷入松软地面。
👆 点击查看参考答案
They provide a large contact area A for the same weight F, which reduces pressure (p = F / A) on the ground.
C12 · 5.4 Increasing PressureCore
Why does sharpening a knife make it cut much more easily with the same pushing force?
为什么磨快刀刃后用同样大的力能轻松切开物体？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Sharpening makes the contact area A tiny, producing a very large pressure (p = F / A) for the same applied force.
C13 · 5.5 Liquid PressureExtended
Write the equation for the change in pressure beneath the surface of a liquid, defining each symbol.
写出液体内部压强变化公式及每个符号代表的物理量与国际单位。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Δp = ρ g Δh, where ρ is liquid density (kg/m³), g = 9.8 m/s², and Δh is vertical depth below the surface (m).
C14 · 5.5 IndependenceExtended
Does liquid pressure at a given depth depend on container width or shape? What directions does it act in?
液体压强是否受容器形状宽窄影响？在同一深度压强指向哪个方向？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Independent of container shape or width (depends only on ρ, g, and depth Δh). At a fixed depth it acts equally in all directions.
C15 · 5.5 Dam WallExtended
Why are reservoir dams built much thicker at the bottom than at the top?
为什么水库大坝底部修建得比顶部厚得多？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Because liquid pressure increases with depth (Δp = ρgΔh), so the bottom wall must withstand a much greater sideways water force.
C16 · 5.5 Total PressureExtended
How does total absolute pressure underwater differ from Δp = ρgΔh?
水下“绝对总压强”与单纯液体压强公式 Δp = ρgΔh 有何区别？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Δp = ρgΔh gives the pressure due to the liquid alone; total pressure adds atmospheric pressure at the surface (p_total = p_atm + ρgΔh).
