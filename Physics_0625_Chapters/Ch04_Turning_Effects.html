4.1 力矩的定义与垂直距离 (The moment of a force)

Turning Effect M = F × d, Perpendicular Distance to Line of Action & Everyday Levers
Core

4.1.1 力矩公式与最易踩坑的“垂直距离 d” Core

力不仅能使物体直线加速，还能使其绕某一个固定转轴（Pivot / Fulcrum）发生转动。力产生转动效应的大小称为力矩（Moment of a force）。

M = F × d   (标准 SI 单位: N m，小尺寸可用 N cm)

阅卷必写定义（1 分关键词）：
M = 力的大小 F × 从支点到力的作用线的垂直距离 d (perpendicular distance from the pivot to the line of action of the force)。
• 力矩的方向（Direction）：在平面内必须标明顺时针（Clockwise, CW）还是逆时针（Anticlockwise, ACW）！
• 特例：若力的作用线直接穿过支点（d = 0），则该力产生的转动效应为 0（这也是双支点算桥墩受力时“选一个支点取力矩”来消去未知力的神技依据！）。

⚠️ 易错警报：力斜着拉时，d 不是杆长而是垂线距离！

用扳手拧螺母或斜拉杠杆时，若拉力方向与杆身成非 90° 夹角，力臂 d 不是手握点到支点的连线长度 L，而是先将力的方向向两端延长画出力的作用线（line of action），再从支点向该直线作垂线（90°）的长度 d_⊥（d_⊥ < L）。因此沿着与扳手垂直（90°）的方向用力且尽量握在最末端，最省力！

📘 例题 4.1 · 扳手长短与施力角度对力矩的影响

A mechanic needs a turning moment of 14.0 N m to loosen a tight nut.
(a) If the mechanic pushes perpendicularly at a distance of 0.35 m from the nut, calculate the force needed.
(b) If the mechanic switches to a shorter spanner where the perpendicular distance is 0.20 m, what force is now needed?

解析 (Solution)：
(a) F = M / d = 14.0 ÷ 0.35 = 40.0 N。
(b) F = M / d = 14.0 ÷ 0.20 = 70.0 N（力臂缩短后所需施力显著增大，故门把手装在远离铰链的一侧、撬棍用长杆都遵循这一规律）。

4.2 力矩平衡原理与双支点杠杆 (Principle of moments & equilibrium)

Clockwise = Anticlockwise Moments, Two Conditions for Equilibrium & Beam Weight / Two-Support Reactions
Core Extended: 含杠杆自身重力与双支点反应力计算

4.2.1 力矩平衡原理与完全平衡的两大充分必要条件 Core

Σ 顺时针力矩 (Sum of CW moments) = Σ 逆时针力矩 (Sum of ACW moments)

物体处于静力平衡（Equilibrium）的两大条件（缺一不可！）：
1. 没有净合力（No resultant force）：Σ F = 0（向上的总支持力 = 向下的总重力/压力，物体既不平动加速也不飞走）；
2. 没有净力矩（No resultant moment / turning effect）：绕任意一点，顺时针总力矩 = 逆时针总力矩，物体不发生转动。

💡 考场解题四步模板：含“尺子自身重量”与“双支点桥梁”

1. 先画全受力图：绝不能漏掉均匀木梁/米尺自身的重力 W_beam = mg，它竖直向下作用在重心（几何中心，如米尺 50 cm 处）！
2. 判断转向（CW vs ACW）：手指按住支点，顺次推每个力的箭头看它想把杆往顺时针还是逆时针掰动。
3. 列力矩方程：当存在两个未知支点支持力 R_A 和 R_B 时，选支点 A 作为转轴取力矩，立刻让 R_A × 0 = 0 消失，直接一步解出 R_B！
4. 用合力为零求另一端：由 R_A + R_B = 向下总重力 直接算出 R_A（再用“绕 B 点取力矩”验算一遍保证 100% 满分！）。

📘 例题 4.2A · 偏心支点平衡（计入米尺自身重力 · g = 9.8 N/kg）

A uniform 100 cm metre rule of mass 0.25 kg is pivoted at the 30 cm mark. Calculate the mass m that must be hung at the 20 cm mark to balance the rule horizontally. (g = 9.8 N/kg)

解析 (Solution)：
① 均匀米尺自身重力 W_rule = 0.25 × 9.8 = 2.45 N，作用在中心 50 cm 处（在支点右侧，距离 50 − 30 = 20 cm，产生顺时针 CW 力矩）：
M_cw = 2.45 N × 20 cm = 49.0 N cm。
② 悬挂重物在 20 cm 处（在支点左侧，距离 30 − 20 = 10 cm，产生逆时针 ACW 力矩）：
M_acw = W_m × 10 cm。
③ 由平衡条件 W_m × 10 = 49.0 ⇒ W_m = 4.90 N ⇒ m = 4.90 ÷ 9.8 = 0.50 kg。

📘 例题 4.2B · 双支点桥梁/木板反应力计算 (g = 9.8 N/kg) Extended

A uniform wooden plank of length 4.0 m and mass 60 kg rests horizontally on two supports A and B at its ends. A crate of mass 100 kg is placed 1.0 m from support A. Calculate the upward reaction forces R_A and R_B at the supports. (g = 9.8 N/kg)

解析 (Solution)：
① 先算向下重力：木板自重 W_plank = 60 × 9.8 = 588 N（作用在中心距 A 2.0 m 处）；货箱重 W_crate = 100 × 9.8 = 980 N（距 A 1.0 m）。
② 绕左支点 A 取力矩（消除 R_A）：
顺时针总力矩 = (980 × 1.0) + (588 × 2.0) = 980 + 1176 = 2156 N m；
逆时针总力矩 = R_B × 4.0 ⇒ R_B = 2156 ÷ 4.0 = 539 N。
③ 利用竖直方向平衡（合力为零）：
R_A + R_B = 980 + 588 = 1568 N ⇒ R_A = 1568 − 539 = 1029 N。
（快速验算：绕 B 取力矩 980×3.0 + 588×2.0 = 4116，而 1029×4.0 = 4116，分毫不差！）

4.3 重心与物体稳定性 (Centre of gravity & stability)

Centre of Gravity Definition, Plumb-Line Experiment (Paper 6), Equilibrium States & Toppling Rule
Core

4.3.1 重心 (Centre of Gravity) 与 Paper 6 悬垂线法实验 Core

重心（Centre of gravity，2023 新纲标准术语）定义为物体全部重力可以看作集中作用的那一点（the point through which the whole weight of an object may be considered to act）。对于密度均匀的规则几何体（如长方体、圆环、直尺），重心就在几何对称中心（注意圆环或空心杯的重心可以位于物体之外的空气中！）。

🔬 Paper 6 必背实验步骤：悬垂线法测不规则纸板重心 (Plumb-Line Method)
  1. 在不规则硬纸板（lamina）边缘附近相距较远的位置打 2 至 3 个小孔；
  2. 用铁架台横插一根大头针/细钉穿过第 1 个小孔，让纸板自由悬垂并轻轻晃动验证无摩擦卡死（swings freely）；
  3. 同一根钉子上挂一根铅垂线（plumb line，细线末端绑小重物），待两者完全静止后，用铅笔沿垂线在纸板上画出一条直线；
  4. 原理说明：静止平衡时重力的力矩必为零，因此重心的垂线一定正处于钉子支点的正下方（沿铅垂线分布）；
  5. 改用第 2 个小孔重复上述操作画第二条直线；两线的交点（intersection）即为重心（再挂第 3 个孔验证三条线交于同一点，或用铅笔尖顶住该交点看能否水平平衡！）。

4.3.2 三种平衡状态、倾倒临界判定与高稳定性设计 Core

平衡类型 轻推倾斜后重心高度变化 重力产生的力矩效果与结果
稳定平衡 (Stable)
例：平放的圆锥/金字塔		 重心升高（C.G. rises） 重力作用线仍在底座内，产生恢复力矩（restoring moment）使物体自动摆回原位。
不稳定平衡 (Unstable)
例：倒立在尖顶的圆锥		 重心降低（C.G. falls） 稍偏即产生倾覆力矩（toppling moment），物体进一步倾倒。
随遇平衡 (Neutral)
例：水平桌上的均匀球体		 重心高度不变 重力始终正对支点，力矩恒为零，新位置直接保持静止平衡。
  • 倾倒铁律（Toppling Rule）：当物体倾斜到使其通过重心的竖直线（vertical line through centre of gravity）落到支撑底面（base）之外时，重力产生绕边缘支点的倾覆力矩，物体必然翻倒！
  • 提高稳定性的两大工程方法（2 分必背）：① 降低重心（lower the centre of gravity）（如跑车底盘低、不倒翁底部灌铅、双层巴士把重发动机和主要乘客安排在下层）；② 增大底面宽度/支撑面积（widen the base / increase base area）（如三脚架张开、烧杯底座外扩、F1 赛车宽轮距）。

SME 1 · 跷跷板力矩平衡与支点支持力 (g = 9.8 N/kg)Core [6 marks]
A uniform seesaw beam of negligible mass is pivoted at its midpoint. A child of mass 35 kg sits 1.8 m to the left of the pivot. (g = 9.8 N/kg)
(a) Calculate the child's weight and the anticlockwise moment produced about the pivot. [3]
(b) An adult of weight 686 N sits on the right-hand side so the seesaw balances horizontally. Calculate the adult's distance from the pivot. [2]
(c) State the upward force exerted by the pivot on the beam. [1]
轻质跷跷板中点支起，左侧 1.8 m 坐 35 kg 小孩（g = 9.8），右侧坐重 686 N 的成年人平衡，求力矩、距离及支点向上推力。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 6 Marks)

C1 (a) W = m g = 35 × 9.8 = 343 N;
C1 Moment = F × d = 343 × 1.8 A1 617.4 N m (accept 617 N m).

C1 (b) Clockwise moment = Anticlockwise moment: 686 × d = 617.4 A1 0.90 m.

B1 (c) Upward pivot force = Total downward weight = 343 + 686 = 1029 N.

中文精析：平衡条件第二条常被忽略——除了力矩相等，支点向上的支持力必须抵消两人总重力 1029 N！

SME 2 · 双支点脚手架木板与自重受力求解 (g = 9.8 N/kg)Extended [7 marks]
A uniform horizontal scaffold plank of length 5.0 m and mass 40 kg rests on two supports P and Q positioned at its two ends (0 m and 5.0 m). A painter of mass 60 kg stands 1.5 m from support P. (g = 9.8 N/kg)
(a) Calculate the weight of the plank and the weight of the painter. [2]
(b) By taking moments about support P, calculate the upward reaction force R_Q exerted by support Q. [3]
(c) Hence calculate the upward reaction force R_P exerted by support P. [2]
长 5.0 m、重 40 kg 的均匀木板架在两端支点 P 和 Q 上，60 kg 油漆工站在距 P 1.5 m 处，求两端支持力 R_Q 与 R_P。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 7 Marks)

B1 (a) Plank weight = 40 × 9.8 = 392 N (acting at midpoint 2.5 m from P);
B1 Painter weight = 60 × 9.8 = 588 N.

M1 (b) Sum of CW moments about P = (588 × 1.5) + (392 × 2.5) = 882 + 980 = 1862 N m;
C1 R_Q × 5.0 = 1862 A1 372.4 N (accept 372 N).

C1 (c) Total upward force = Total downward force: R_P + 372.4 = 588 + 392 = 980 N A1 607.6 N (accept 608 N).

中文精析：油漆工离 P 更近，因此左支点 P 承担了更大份额（607.6 N vs 372.4 N），物理直觉立刻检验出没有算反！

SME 4 · 门把手力臂解释与防倾倒设计原理Core [6 marks]
(a) Explain, using the concept of moments, why a door handle is placed as far as possible from the hinges. [2]
(b) State the exact condition that determines when a tilted object will topple over. [2]

查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 6 Marks)

B1 (a) Places the force at the largest perpendicular distance d from the pivot (hinges);

B1 (b) An object topples when the vertical line through its centre of gravity;

State the Principle of Moments for an object in equilibrium.
默写杠杆平衡原理（Principle of Moments）的考纲标准表述。
👆 点击查看参考答案
When an object is in equilibrium, the sum of clockwise moments about any pivot equals the sum of anticlockwise moments about the same pivot.
C06 · 4.2 Dual ConditionsCore
State the two conditions required for a system to be in complete equilibrium.
写出物体处于完全静力平衡必须同时满足的两大条件。
👆 点击查看参考答案
1. No resultant force (upward forces = downward forces). 2. No resultant moment (clockwise moments = anticlockwise moments).
C07 · 4.2 Pivot ForceCore
How do you find the upward force exerted by a single pivot on a balanced seesaw?
怎样求水平平衡跷跷板中央支点施加的向上支持力？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Use Condition 1 (resultant vertical force = 0): upward pivot force = sum of all downward weights (including the beam's own weight!).
C08 · 4.2 Two SupportsExtended
What is the quickest strategy to solve for two unknown support reactions R_A and R_B on a bridge?
解双支点桥梁的两个未知支持力 R_A 和 R_B 时，最高效的策略是什么？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Take moments about one support (e.g. A) so R_A has zero moment and cancels out, solving directly for R_B; then use R_A + R_B = total downward load.
C09 · 4.2 Beam WeightCore
Where does the weight of a uniform beam act if it is not pivoted at its midpoint?
当均匀木梁或米尺未支在中点时，其自身重力应画在何处？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Vertically downwards at its centre of gravity (geometric midpoint, e.g. 50 cm mark), creating its own turning moment about the off-centre pivot!
C10 · 4.2 Non-Uniform BeamExtended
If a non-uniform plank balances horizontally at its 40 cm mark with no extra weights, where is its C.G.?
若一根非均匀木棒不挂任何重物即可在 40 cm 刻度处水平平衡，其重心在何处？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Directly at the 40 cm mark, because for zero net moment the line of action of its weight must pass through the pivot.
C11 · 4.3 C.G. DefinitionCore
Define the centre of gravity of an object.
默写重心（Centre of gravity）的考纲标准英文定义。
👆 点击查看参考答案
The point through which the whole weight of an object may be considered to act.
C12 · 4.3 Plumb-Line WhyCore
In the plumb-line experiment, why does the centre of gravity always settle directly below the pin?
悬垂线实验中，为什么不规则纸板静止时重心必然停在钉子正下方？
👆 点击查看参考答案
When hanging freely at equilibrium, there is zero turning moment about the pin, so the weight's line of action must pass vertically through the pivot.
C13 · 4.3 Equilibrium TypesCore
Explain the difference between stable, unstable, and neutral equilibrium in terms of C.G. height when tilted.
根据倾斜时重心高度变化，区分稳定平衡、不稳定平衡与随遇平衡。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Stable: C.G. rises when tilted (returns). Unstable: C.G. falls when tilted (topples). Neutral: C.G. stays at constant height (stays in new position).
C14 · 4.3 Toppling RuleCore
State the condition for a tilted object to topple over.
默写物体发生倾倒的临界几何条件。
👆 点击查看参考答案
An object topples over when the vertical line through its centre of gravity falls outside its base.
C15 · 4.3 Improving StabilityCore
State two ways to make an object (such as a lamp stand or racing car) more stable.
提高物体稳定性（防翻倒）的两种通用工程设计方案是什么？
👆 点击查看参考答案
1. Lower the centre of gravity (e.g. heavy base). 2. Widen the base area (e.g. wide wheel track / splayed legs).
