19.2 Series & Parallel Circuits, Combined Resistance & Potential Dividers
Core 核心必考 Extended 拓展进阶

一、串联电路 vs 并联电路五大规律对比 Core Extended

物理特征 串联电路 Series Circuit 并联电路 Parallel Circuit
电流规律
Current		 处处相等：I = I1 = I2 干路电流等于各支路电流之和：Itotal = I1 + I2
Extended 节点电荷守恒：流入节点总电流 = 流出节点总电流
电压规律
Potential difference		 总电压（电源电动势）等于各元件电压之和：
Vtotal = V1 + V2		 各并联支路两端电压完全相等且等于电源电压：
V = V1 = V2
总电阻
Combined Resistance		 RT = R1 + R2
（总阻恒大于任意单个电阻）		 总阻恒小于最小支路电阻（增加并联路径使总电流增大）！
Extended 1RT = 1R1 + 1R2  ⇒  RT = R1R2R1+R2
照明电路实用优劣 一处断开全灭；增接灯泡导致总阻升高、亮度变暗。 家庭照明必用并联：① 各灯均获额定全电压互不影响；② 可用支路开关独立控制；③ 一灯烧毁其余照常工作。

二、电位分压器 (Potential Divider) 与自动传感电路设计 Extended

V₁V₂ = R₁R₂    |    Vout = R₂R₁ + R₂ × Vin
分压铁则：串联电路电流相同 (V = IR)，电阻越大，分得的电压份额就越大！

考场最常考将 LDR 或 NTC 热敏电阻接入分压器并连接输出端 Vout（驱动继电器或蜂鸣器）：

  • 情景 1 · 天黑自动亮起路灯：将 LDR 放在底部 (R2)，Vout 接在 LDR 两端。天黑时 RLDR 剧增 → LDR 分得更高比例电压 (Vout ↑) → 达到继电器触发电压，接通路灯！
  • 情景 2 · 高温火警 / 自动散热风扇：若把 NTC 热敏电阻放在上方 (R1)、定值电阻放在下方 (R2) 并从定值电阻取 Vout：失火升温时 RNTC 剧降 → 它自身分压减小，因此下方定值电阻电压 Vout 升高 → 触发高温警报！（反之若 Vout 直接跨接在热敏电阻两端，则升温反而使 Vout 下降！）
📝 例题 1 · 并联总阻与 NTC 分压器计算 [Extended]

(1) 将 6.0 Ω 与 3.0 Ω 电阻并联接在 12 V 电池两端，求并联总电阻与干路电流。
解：RT = (6.0 × 3.0) / (6.0 + 3.0) = 2.0 Ω（果然小于 3.0 Ω！）；Itotal = 12 / 2.0 = 6.0 A（两条支路各 2.0 A 与 4.0 A，之和等于 6.0 A）。
(2) 某分压器输入 9.0 V，上方定值电阻 R1 = 1000 Ω，下方 NTC 热敏电阻 R2 在 20 °C 时为 2000 Ω，在 60 °C 时降至 500 Ω。求两端输出 Vout（跨接在 NTC 上）变化。
解：20 °C 时 Vout = [2000 / (1000 + 2000)] × 9.0 = 6.0 V；60 °C 时 Vout = [500 / (1000 + 500)] × 9.0 = 3.0 V（同时定值电阻两端电压由 3.0 V 升至 6.0 V）。

19.3 家庭用电安全、三脚插头、保险丝与双重绝缘

19.3 Electrical Safety, Mains Plug (Live/Neutral/Earth), Fuses, Trip Switches & Double Insulation
Core 核心必考

一、三线插头 (3-Pin Plug) 与火线保护原则 Core

导线 Wire 绝缘色标 Colour 电位与核心功能 Role in Safety
火线 (Live wire) 棕色 (Brown) 接交流高电压（英国/考纲标准约 230 V 交变电势）。开关 (Switch) 与保险丝 (Fuse) 必须串联在火线上！
⚠️ 若错装在零线上，即使开关断开或保险丝熔断，用电器内部仍与 230 V 火线相连，触碰金属外壳会触电！
零线 (Neutral wire) 蓝色 (Blue) 提供电流返回供电局的回路，在变电站处接地，电势维持在 约 0 V。
地线 (Earth wire) 黄绿双色条纹
(Green & Yellow)		 专门连接电器的金属外壳 (Metal casing)。正常时无电流；一旦火线松脱碰触金属外壳，地线提供极低电阻接地路径，瞬间涌入极大电流立即熔断火线上的保险丝，使外壳断电防触电！

二、保险丝选型、空气开关与双重绝缘 (Fuses, Trip Switches & Double Insulation) Core

  • 保险丝 (Fuse)：内含低熔点细金属丝，当过载或短路导致电流超过额定值时，电流热效应使保险丝发热熔断 (Melts and breaks circuit)，防止电线过热着火或人体触电。
    • 选型黄金法则：先由 I = P / V 计算电器正常工作电流，再从常见标准规格（3 A, 5 A, 13 A）中选择略高于工作电流 (Just above normal operating current) 的那一档！
    • 例如：I = 4.0 A（920 W, 230 V）→ 选 5 A 保险丝（选 3 A 开机就烧，选 13 A 故障过流时不及时熔断极危险！）。
  • 断路器 / 跳闸开关 (Trip Switch / MCB & RCD)：利用电磁铁吸合或漏电感应自动跳闸切断电路。相比一次性保险丝的两大优势：动作更快、断电后无需更换耗材可直接复位 (Resettable)。
  • 双重绝缘 (Double Insulation, 回字形标志)：电器外壳完全采用非导电塑料 (Non-conducting plastic casing) 密封，且内部带电部分与外壳之间另有独立绝缘层 → 即使内部断线，外壳也绝不可能带电 → 无需接地线 (No Earth wire required)，使用两脚插头！
⚠️ 家庭用电四大常考安全隐患 (Hazards of Mains Electricity)
  1. 绝缘皮破损 (Damaged insulation)：裸露火线引发直接触电或火零线短路起火。
  2. 拖线板过载 / 导线过热 (Overheating / overloading cables)：同一插座并联过多大功率电器，总电流剧增引发 I²R 严重发热着火。
  3. 潮湿环境 (Damp conditions)：水（含电解质杂质）是导体，大幅降低皮肤接触电阻，导致致命电流穿过人体。
  4. 插头接线松动或错误：如接地线脱落、开关装在零线上、用铜丝/锡纸替代保险丝。

Structured Q1 · Core · Paper 3 [4 marks]
A metal-cased electric toaster is connected to the 230 V mains supply via a 3-pin plug.
(a) State why the switch and fuse must both be placed in the live wire rather than the neutral wire. [2]
(b) Explain how the earth wire and fuse work together to protect the user if the live wire comes loose and touches the metal casing. [2]
(a) 解释为何三脚插头与电器中的开关和保险丝都必须装在火线而非零线上。[2]
(b) 当火线松脱触及烤面包机金属外壳时，解释地线与保险丝如何协同保护使用者。[2]
📋 查看 Mark Scheme 官方得分点与中文精析

B1 (a) The live wire is at high alternating potential (~230 V); placing the fuse/switch there disconnects the high voltage from the appliance when open or blown.

B1 If placed in the neutral wire, the appliance would remain connected to the 230 V live supply (causing electric shock if touched).

B1 (b) The earth wire provides a very low-resistance path from the metal case to earth, so a very large surge current flows.

B1 This large current heats and melts the fuse immediately, breaking the live circuit and making the casing safe.

中文精析：地线+保险丝联动机制必写四连环：low-resistance path to earth → large current → melts the fuse → disconnects live supply。
Structured Q2 · Extended · Paper 4 [5 marks]
An automatic streetlamp circuit uses a 12 V d.c. supply, a 2.0 kΩ fixed resistor R₁, and an LDR R₂ connected in series as a potential divider. The coil of a magnetic relay is connected in parallel across the LDR.
(a) In bright daylight, the resistance of the LDR is 400 Ω. Calculate the potential difference across the LDR. [2]
(b) Explain what happens when darkness falls, causing the LDR resistance to rise to 10 kΩ, and why a relay is used to switch on the 230 V streetlamp. [3]
某自动路灯控制电路采用 12 V 直流电源，将 2.0 kΩ 定值电阻 R₁ 与光敏电阻 LDR (R₂) 串联作分压器，磁继电器线圈并联在 LDR 两端。
(a) 白天强光下 LDR 阻值为 400 Ω，计算 LDR 两端分得的电压。[2]
(b) 解释夜幕降临使 LDR 阻值升至 10 kΩ 后的电路反应，并说明为何要用磁继电器去控制 230 V 高压路灯。[3]
📋 查看 Mark Scheme 官方得分点与中文精析

C1 (a) R_total = 2000 + 400 = 2400 Ω; V_LDR = [400 / 2400] × 12

A1 V_LDR = 2.0 V.

B1 (b) When dark, R_LDR increases (to 10 kΩ), so the p.d. across the LDR increases (to [10/12] × 12 = 10 V).

B1 Sufficient current flows through the relay coil, magnetising it to attract and close the switch contacts of the mains lamp circuit.

B1 A relay allows a small current / low-voltage d.c. sensor circuit to safely switch a high-voltage / large-current mains circuit (electrical isolation).

中文精析：分压计算先统一单位（2.0 kΩ = 2000 Ω）；继电器核心作用是“小电流低压控制大电流高压，实现安全隔离”。
Structured Q3 · Extended · Paper 4 [4 marks]
A circuit has a 12 V battery connected to two parallel branches. Branch 1 contains a 6.0 Ω resistor; Branch 2 contains a 4.0 Ω resistor and an 8.0 Ω resistor in series.
(a) Calculate the total resistance of Branch 2 and the combined resistance of the whole parallel combination. [2]
(b) Calculate the total current drawn from the 12 V battery. [2]
12 V 电池接两条并联支路：支路 1 为一个 6.0 Ω 电阻；支路 2 为 4.0 Ω 与 8.0 Ω 两个电阻串联。
(a) 求支路 2 的总电阻及整条并联电路的总等效电阻。[2]
(b) 计算电源输出的总电流。[2]
📋 查看 Mark Scheme 官方得分点与中文精析

B1 (a) Branch 2 (series): R₂ = 4.0 + 8.0 = 12 Ω.

A1 Parallel total: 1/R_T = 1/6.0 + 1/12 = 3/12 ⇒ R_T = 4.0 Ω.

C1 (b) I_total = V / R_T = 12 / 4.0 (or I₁ = 12/6 = 2.0 A, I₂ = 12/12 = 1.0 A, sum = 2.0 + 1.0)

A1 I_total = 3.0 A.

中文精析：混联电路“先局部串联、再整体并联”；也可以分别求两条并联支路电流再相加验算，2.0 + 1.0 = 3.0 A 完全吻合！
Structured Q4 · Core · Paper 3/4 [4 marks]
An electric heater is rated at 920 W on a 230 V mains supply. Available standard fuses are 3 A, 5 A, and 13 A.
(a) Calculate the normal operating current and select the most suitable fuse rating, giving a reason. [3]
(b) Give one advantage of using a circuit breaker (trip switch) instead of a fuse. [1]
一台 230 V 市电电暖器额定功率为 920 W，备选标准保险丝为 3 A、5 A 和 13 A。
(a) 计算正常工作电流并选择最恰当的保险丝规格，说明理由。[3]
(b) 写出使用断路器（空气开关/漏电开关）替代保险丝的一项优势。[1]
📋 查看 Mark Scheme 官方得分点与中文精析

M1 (a) I = P / V = 920 / 230 = 4.0 A.

B1 Choose the 5 A fuse.

B1 Reason: 5 A is just above the normal working current of 4.0 A (a 3 A fuse would melt during normal operation; a 13 A fuse allows dangerous overheating before blowing).

B1 (b) Can be easily reset without replacing / reacts faster / more sensitive.

中文精析：保险丝选型必背英文句式：just above the normal operating current！

Core19.1 · NTC热敏电阻
How does the resistance of an NTC thermistor change as temperature rises?
温度升高时，NTC 热敏电阻的阻值如何变化？
👆 点击掀开答案
Its resistance decreases sharply as temperature increases (Negative Temperature Coefficient).
温度越高，阻值越低；温度越低，阻值越大（与纯金属相反！）。
Core19.1 · 光敏电阻
How does the resistance of a light-dependent resistor (LDR) vary with light intensity?
光敏电阻 (LDR) 的阻值随光照强度如何变化？
👆 点击掀开答案
As light intensity increases, resistance decreases (very high resistance in the dark).
光照越强阻值越小；黑暗环境下阻值极高。
Core19.1 · 2023符号特征
Distinguish the 2023–2028 circuit symbols for a fuse, thermistor, and LDR.
区分 2023 新纲中保险丝、热敏电阻与光敏电阻的标准符号。
👆 点击掀开答案
Fuse: rectangle with continuous wire straight through; Thermistor: rectangle with diagonal line & flat horizontal hook at bottom-left; LDR: resistor inside circle with two arrows pointing inwards.
保险丝=直线贯穿矩形；热敏=带底平钩斜线穿矩形；LDR=圆圈+双箭头向内射向电阻。
Core19.1 · 二极管用途
What is the primary circuit use of a semiconductor diode?
半导体二极管在电路中最主要的功能是什么？
👆 点击掀开答案
Allows current in one direction only; used as a rectifier (converting a.c. to d.c.) and for reverse-polarity protection.
只允许单向电流通过，用于整流（交流变直流）及防反接保护。
Core19.1 · 磁继电器
Why is a magnetic relay used in sensor-controlled mains circuits?
为什么常在传感器控制市电的电路中加入磁继电器？
👆 点击掀开答案
It allows a small current in a low-voltage circuit to safely switch on a large current / high-voltage mains circuit (isolation).
实现强弱电隔离，用安全的小电流低压感应回路去开关危险的高压大电流回路。
Core19.2 · 串联规律
State the rules for current, p.d., and combined resistance in a series circuit.
写出串联电路中的电流、电压与总电阻规律。
👆 点击掀开答案
Current is the same everywhere (I = I₁ = I₂); p.d. is shared (V = V₁ + V₂); R_T = R₁ + R₂.
电流处处相等，总电压等于各分压之和，总电阻等于各电阻之和。
Core19.2 · 并联规律
State the rules for current, p.d., and combined resistance in a parallel circuit.
写出并联电路中的电流、电压与总电阻定性规律。
👆 点击掀开答案
Current splits at junctions (I_in = I₁ + I₂); p.d. is the same across each branch; total resistance is less than the smallest resistor.
干路电流等于支路电流之和，各支路电压相等，并联总阻小于任一支路最小电阻。
Core19.2 · 并联照明优势
Give two advantages of connecting lamps in parallel rather than in series in a house.
写出家庭照明灯采用并联而非串联的两大优势。
👆 点击掀开答案
(1) Each lamp receives the full mains p.d. (full brightness) and can be switched independently; (2) if one breaks, others stay lit.
每盏灯均可获额定全电压且独立开关；一灯损坏其余照常亮。
Extended19.2 · 节点电流守恒
State the junction current rule in terms of conservation of charge.
写出电路节点电流法则及其背后的守恒定律。
👆 点击掀开答案
The sum of currents entering a junction equals the sum of currents leaving it (conservation of charge).
流入节点的总电流等于流出节点的总电流（源于电荷守恒）。
Extended19.2 · 并联公式
State the formula for the combined resistance of two resistors R₁ and R₂ in parallel.
写出两个电阻并联时的总电阻计算公式。
👆 点击掀开答案
1 / R_T = 1 / R₁ + 1 / R₂, which gives R_T = (R₁ R₂) / (R₁ + R₂).
倒数之和等于总阻倒数；两电阻并联可直接用“积除以和”。
Extended19.2 · 电位分压器
Write the potential divider equations linking V₁, V₂, R₁, R₂ and V_out.
写出串联分压器的比例式与输出电压计算式。
👆 点击掀开答案
V₁ / V₂ = R₁ / R₂ and V_out = [R₂ / (R₁ + R₂)] × V_in (larger R gets larger p.d.).
分压比等于电阻比，电阻越大分得的电压份额越高。
Core19.3 · 三线色标
State the colours and typical voltages of the Live, Neutral, and Earth wires in a mains plug.
说出市电三脚插头中火线、零线、地线的颜色与电位。
👆 点击掀开答案
Live: Brown (~230 V a.c.); Neutral: Blue (~0 V return); Earth: Green/Yellow (0 V safety wire to metal case).
火线棕(~230 V)、零线蓝(~0 V)、地线黄绿(接金属外壳保安全)。
Core19.3 · 火线接开关
Why must switches and fuses always be fitted in the live wire?
为什么开关和保险丝必须永远安装在火线上？
👆 点击掀开答案
So that when switched off or blown, the appliance is isolated from the 230 V high voltage (safe to touch/service).
确保断开开关或熔断后，电器内部不再带 230 V 高压电，保障人身安全。
Core19.3 · 地线+保险丝
Explain how the earth wire protects a user if a live wire touches the metal casing.
解释火线意外碰触金属外壳时，地线如何协同保险丝起保护作用。
👆 点击掀开答案
Earth wire provides a low-resistance path to earth → large current flows → melts the fuse → disconnects live supply.
低阻接地通路引发瞬间大电流 → 立刻熔断保险丝 → 切断火线电源。
Core19.3 · 保险丝选型
What rule determines the correct fuse rating for an appliance?
如何为电器挑选正确规格的保险丝？
👆 点击掀开答案
Calculate normal current (I = P / V) and pick the standard rating just above normal current (e.g. 3 A, 5 A, or 13 A).
先算额定电流 I = P/V，再选略高于工作电流的最小标准档位。
Core19.3 · 断路器优势
State two advantages of a trip switch (circuit breaker) over a fuse.
断路器（跳闸开关）相比普通保险丝有哪两项优势？
👆 点击掀开答案
(1) Resettable (no replacement needed); (2) Acts much faster and is more sensitive.
可重复复位无需更换，且跳闸响应速度更快、灵敏度更高。
Core19.3 · 双重绝缘
Why does a double-insulated appliance NOT require an earth wire?
为什么带有双重绝缘标志的电器不需要接地线？
👆 点击掀开答案
It has a non-conducting plastic outer casing (and extra inner insulation), so the exterior cannot become live.
外壳是完全不导电的绝缘塑料且内部双重隔离，外部绝不会带电。
Core19.3 · 用电隐患
State three common electrical hazards in the home.
列举三种常见的家庭用电安全隐患。
👆 点击掀开答案
(1) Damaged insulation; (2) Overheating / overloading sockets and cables; (3) Damp conditions (wet hands/floor).
绝缘层破损、插座过载导致电缆过热起火、潮湿环境大幅减小人体电阻。
