3.1 力的分类、合力与牛顿第一定律 (Forces, resultant force & Newton's 1st law)

Contact & Non-Contact Forces, Resultant Force Along a Line & Balanced Forces Equilibrium
Core

3.1.1 力的作用效果与常见力图鉴 Core

力（Force，单位牛顿 N）是物体间的推拉作用，可以改变物体的大小与形状（size and shape）、运动速率（speed）或运动方向（direction of motion）。

类别 力名称 Force 方向与物理特性（考场必背）
接触力
(Contact)		 摩擦力 Friction 固体接触面间阻碍相对滑动（或趋势）的力；将机械能转化为内能（生热）。
流体阻力 Drag / Air resistance 物体在空气或液体中运动时受到的阻力；随速度增大、迎风面积增大而急剧增大。
支持力 Normal contact force 支撑面垂直（90°）施加给物体的反作用力。
拉力/张力 Tension 拉紧的绳索、弹簧或缆线内部沿绳方向传递的拉力。
浮力 Upthrust 流体（液体或气体）对浸入物体施加的竖直向上托力。
非接触力
(Non-contact)		 重力 Weight 引力场对有质量物体施加的竖直向下（指向地心）引力。
静电力 Electrostatic force 电荷之间隔空吸引（异种）或排斥（同种）的力。
磁力 Magnetic force 磁极间或磁场对磁性材料施加的隔空作用力。

3.1.2 同一直线合力 (Resultant Force) 与牛顿第一定律 Core

  • 合力计算：同向共线力直接相加；反向共线力相减，合力方向跟随较大分力的方向。例如向右推力 50 N 与向左摩擦力 18 N 的合力为 50 − 18 = 32 N 向右。
  • 牛顿第一定律（平衡力 Balanced forces, F_net = 0）：当作用在物体上的合力为零时：
    ① 原来静止的物体 → 继续保持静止（remains stationary）；
    ② 原来运动的物体 → 以恒定速度沿直线匀速运动（continues moving at constant velocity / constant speed in a straight line）。
⚠️ 亚里士多德经典错觉：“维持物体运动需要力”是错的！

如果汽车在平直公路上以 20 m/s 匀速行驶，引擎推力 1400 N，那么地面对车的总阻力必须精确等于 1400 N！合力为零并不代表车停下来了——合力为零意味着加速度为零（速度大小和方向均保持不变）；只有需要改变速度或方向时才需要非零合力！

3.2.1 质量 vs 重力的本质区别与公式 W = mg

W = mg   |   g = Wm   (地球表面标准值: g = 9.8 N/kg = 9.8 m/s²)

重力场强度 (Gravitational field strength g)：定义为单位质量所受的重力（force per unit mass）。2023–2028 新考纲统一取 g = 9.8 N/kg（月球上约为 1.6 N/kg，外太空深空趋近于 0）。

对比维度 质量 Mass (m) 重力 Weight (W)
考纲标准定义 物体所含物质的量（quantity of matter）以及它抵抗运动状态改变的惯性量度（resistance to change in motion / inertia） Ext 引力场对有质量物体施加的万有引力（gravitational force acting on an object that has mass）
标量 / 矢量 标量 Scalar（只有大小） 矢量 Vector（方向始终竖直向下指向地心）

📘 例题 3.2 · 地球与月球上的质量、重力与天平辨析 (g = 9.8 N/kg)

An astronaut together with a space suit has a mass of 75 kg on Earth. Taking g_Earth = 9.8 N/kg and g_Moon = 1.6 N/kg, find (a) their weight on Earth, (b) their mass on the Moon, and (c) their weight on the Moon.

解析 (Solution)：
(a) 地球重力：W_Earth = m g_Earth = 75 × 9.8 = 735 N。
(b) 质量是固有属性与位置无关：m_Moon = 75 kg。
(c) 月球重力：W_Moon = m g_Moon = 75 × 1.6 = 120 N。

3.3 终端速度、行车制动与圆周运动 (Falling, stopping & circular motion)

Parachutist Terminal Velocity Stages, Thinking & Braking Distance, Centripetal Force (Ext)
Core: 空气阻力与行车安全距离 Extended: 跳伞五阶段详解 & 圆周向心力

3.3.1 跳伞员五阶段与终端速度 (Terminal Velocity) 受力全解 Extended 高频 4–6 分简答

跳伞员下落全程重力 W 始终恒定向下，唯一变化的是向上的空气阻力 R（随速度与伞面积增大而变大）：

  1. 刚跳出机舱（t = 0, v = 0）：空气阻力 R = 0，向下合力 F = W，初始加速度 a = 9.8 m/s²（曲线起点最陡）。
  2. 加速下落阶段（W > R）：速度越来越快 ⇒ 空气阻力 R 迅速增大 ⇒ 向下合力 (W − R) 减小 ⇒ 加速度 a 减小（但速度仍在增加，只是增加变慢）。
  3. 第一终端速度（W = R）：阻力增大到与重力完全相等 ⇒ 合力为 0，a = 0 ⇒ 保持恒定的高终端速度匀速下落（约 55 m/s）。
  4. 打开降落伞瞬间（R ≫ W）：伞面面积猛增导致向上的空气阻力瞬间远大于重力 ⇒ 合力方向改为竖直向上 ⇒ 人开始急剧减速（decelerates）！（陷阱：此时运动方向仍然向下，只是速度数值减小！）
  5. 第二（安全）终端速度（R = W）：随着速度大幅下降，空气阻力 R 随之回落，直到再次等于重力 W ⇒ 合力重归 0，以较低的安全终端速度匀速落地（约 5 m/s）。

3.3.2 汽车制动总距离 (Stopping Distance) 两段拆解 Core

总停车距离 Stopping distance = 反应距离 Thinking distance + 刹车距离 Braking distance

• 反应距离（Thinking distance = v × t_reaction）：发现危险到踩下刹车前车匀速行驶的距离（正比于速度 v）。影响因素：车速、疲劳（tiredness）、酒精（alcohol）、药物（drugs）、看手机等分心（distractions）。
• 刹车距离（Braking distance ∝ v²）：踩下刹车到完全停住的距离（车速翻倍，刹车距离暴增至 4 倍！）。影响因素：车速、轮胎磨损（worn tyres）、刹车片老化（faulty brakes）、湿滑或结冰路面（wet/icy road）、车辆总质量偏重。

3.3.3 匀速圆周运动与向心力 (Circular Motion) Extended

  • 为什么速率不变却在加速？物体做匀速圆周运动时，虽然速率（speed）恒定，但运动方向（direction）时刻改变，因此速度（velocity）不断变化 ⇒ 必定存在加速度！
  • 向心力方向：由牛顿第二定律，需要一个始终指向圆心（towards the centre of the circle）、且垂直于速度方向（perpendicular to motion）的合力（称为向心力 Centripetal force）。注意：向心力不是一种独立的新力，而是由拉力（甩球绳）、静摩擦力（弯道车胎）、或万有引力（卫星行星轨道）来充当的合力！
  • 影响向心力大小的三大定性规律：若保持其余变量不变，所需向心力在以下情况会增大：① 物体质量 m 增大；② 运动速率 v 增大；③ 圆周半径 r 减小（转弯越急越容易侧滑！）。
  • 断绳飞出方向：若向心力突然消失（如绳子断裂），物体按牛顿第一定律沿该点切线方向（along the tangent）飞出，绝不是沿着半径向外飞！

3.4 牛顿第二定律：力、质量与加速度 (F = ma)

Resultant Force F = ma, Newton Definition (1 N) & Multi-Force Dynamics (g = 9.8 m/s²)
Extended 核心公式 F = ma

3.4.1 合力决定加速度：F_net = ma Extended

F = ma   |   a = Fnetm   (1 N = 使 1 kg 物体产生 1 m/s² 加速度的力)

• 绝对铁律：公式中的 F 永远是合力（resultant / net force），不是单一推力或牵引力！
• 方向关系：加速度 a 的方向永远与合力 F_net 的方向完全相同。
• 竖直火箭/电梯模型：向上推力 T − 向下重力 mg = ma（先用 g = 9.8 N/kg 算 W = mg，再相减求合力！）。

📘 例题 3.4 · 含摩擦力汽车加速与火箭升空净合力计算 (g = 9.8)

(a) A car of mass 1200 kg has a forward driving force of 3600 N and experiences total resistive forces of 1200 N. Calculate its acceleration.
(b) A rocket of mass 2000 kg produces a vertical upward thrust of 27 600 N at launch. Taking g = 9.8 N/kg, calculate its upward acceleration.

解析 (Solution)：
(a) 合力 F_net = 3600 − 1200 = 2400 N ⇒ a = 2400 ÷ 1200 = 2.0 m/s²。
(b) 先算重力：W = mg = 2000 × 9.8 = 19 600 N；
向上净合力：F_net = 27 600 − 19 600 = 8000 N；
向上加速度：a = 8000 ÷ 2000 = 4.0 m/s²。

3.5 动量、冲量与动量守恒定律 (Momentum & Impulse)

p = mv, Impulse FΔt = Δp, Car Safety Crumple Zones & Conservation of Momentum

本节属于 Extended 扩展考纲内容（0625 Supplement）。仅报考 Core 的同学可略过本节或切换顶部视图过滤。

3.5.1 动量、冲量与汽车防撞安全设计 Extended

动量 p = mv (单位: kg m/s)   |   冲量 Impulse = FΔt = Δp = m(v − u) (单位: N s)

• 动量是矢量：碰撞涉及反弹或相向运动时，必须事先规定正方向（如向右为 +，向左为 −）！
• 合力即动量变化率：F = Δp / Δt。
• 气囊、溃缩区（crumple zones）、安全带与头盔的 3 分标准解释：在碰撞前后总动量变化量 Δp 相同的情况下，安全装置通过延长碰撞作用时间 Δt（increases collision / impact time），从而根据 F = Δp / Δt 大幅减小乘客所受的平均撞击力 F（reduces average / peak force）！

3.5.2 动量守恒定律 (Principle of Conservation of Momentum) Extended

考纲必背表述：当系统不受外力合力作用时（closed / isolated system with no external resultant force），碰撞（或爆炸）前的总动量等于碰撞后的总动量：

m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂   |   黏合碰撞：(m₁ + m₂)v   |   爆炸反冲：0 = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂
📘 例题 3.5A · 相向碰撞粘连动量守恒（矢量符号正负必考！）

Trolley A (4.0 kg) moves right at 5.0 m/s and collides head-on with Trolley B (2.0 kg) moving left at 4.0 m/s. They stick together after impact. Find their common velocity.

解析 (Solution)：
规定向右为正方向（+），则 u_A = +5.0 m/s，u_B = −4.0 m/s。
碰前总动量：p_before = (4.0 × 5.0) + (2.0 × −4.0) = 20.0 − 8.0 = +12.0 kg m/s。
碰后共同速度：(4.0 + 2.0) v = +12.0 ⇒ v = 12.0 ÷ 6.0 = +2.0 m/s (向右)。

📘 例题 3.5B · 墙面反弹的冲量与平均冲击力计算

A 2.0 kg ball hits a wall perpendicularly at 10.0 m/s and rebounds along the same line at 6.0 m/s. The contact lasts 0.080 s. Calculate (a) the change in momentum and (b) the average force exerted by the wall on the ball.

解析 (Solution)：
(a) 设撞墙前方向为正（u = +10.0 m/s），则反弹速度为负（v = −6.0 m/s）：
Δp = m(v − u) = 2.0 × (−6.0 − 10.0) = 2.0 × (−16.0) = −32.0 kg m/s（大小为 32 N s，方向离开墙面）。
(b) 平均作用力大小：F = |Δp| / Δt = 32.0 ÷ 0.080 = 400 N。

3.6.2 矢量合成两大方法：比例尺作图法 & 直角勾股三角法 Extended

📘 例题 3.6 · 直角两力的矢量合成与角度表述

Two forces of 40 N acting due East and 30 N acting due North act simultaneously on a crate. Calculate the magnitude and direction of the resultant force.

解析 (Solution)：
① 合力大小：R = √(40² + 30²) = √(1600 + 900) = √2500 = 50 N；
② 与正东方向夹角：tan θ = 30 ÷ 40 = 0.75 ⇒ θ = arctan(0.75) ≈ 36.9°；
完整答案：50 N at 36.9° North of East（或方位角 bearing 053.1°）。

SME 1 · 跳伞员重力、开伞瞬间合力与新终端速度 (g = 9.8 N/kg)Core Extended [7 marks]
A skydiver and equipment have a total mass of 80 kg. (g = 9.8 N/kg)
(a) Calculate the total weight of the skydiver and equipment. [2]
(b) Ext At the instant the parachute opens, the upward air resistance is 2384 N. Calculate the resultant force and the acceleration of the skydiver, stating their direction. [3]
(c) Explain, in terms of forces, why the skydiver soon reaches a new, lower constant speed. [2]
总质量 80 kg 的跳伞员（g = 9.8 N/kg），开伞瞬间受到向上阻力 2384 N，求重力、合力及加速度，并解释如何达到更低的终端速度。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 7 Marks)

C1 (a) W = m g = 80 × 9.8 A1 784 N.

B1 (b) Resultant force = 2384 − 784 = 1600 N upwards;
C1 a = F / m = 1600 ÷ 80 A1 20 m/s² upwards (deceleration of 20 m/s²).

B1 (c) As the skydiver slows down, air resistance decreases;
B1 until air resistance again equals weight (784 N), so resultant force becomes zero and speed is constant.

中文精析：开伞瞬间合力与加速度方向均竖直向上，但人仍在向下运动，因此表现为强烈减速（而不是向上飞起！）。

SME 2 · 行车反应距离、刹车力与总停车距离Core Extended [7 marks]
A car of mass 1200 kg is travelling at 20 m/s when the driver sees a hazard. The driver's reaction time is 0.60 s. Once the brakes are applied, the car decelerates uniformly to rest in 4.0 s.
(a) Calculate the thinking distance. [2]
(b) Ext Calculate the deceleration and the average braking force acting on the car. [3]
(c) Calculate the total stopping distance, and state one road condition that increases the braking distance. [2]
1200 kg 汽车以 20 m/s 行驶，司机反应时间 0.60 s，刹车后 4.0 s 匀减速停下，求反应距离、刹车力与总停车距离。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 7 Marks)

C1 (a) Thinking distance = v × t = 20 × 0.60 A1 12 m.

B1 (b) Deceleration = (20 − 0) ÷ 4.0 = 5.0 m/s²;
C1 F = m a = 1200 × 5.0 A1 6000 N.

B1 (c) Braking distance = ½ × 20 × 4.0 = 40 m ⇒ Total stopping distance = 12 + 40 = 52 m;
B1 Wet / icy road (reduces friction between tyres and road).

中文精析：思考距离按匀速矩形算（12 m），刹车距离按匀减速三角形面积算（40 m），两者相加得总停车距离 52 m。

SME 3 · 动量、碰撞冲量与安全气囊减伤机理Extended [7 marks]
During a crash test, a car of mass 1500 kg travelling at 18 m/s hits a rigid barrier and comes to rest.
(a) Calculate the initial momentum of the car. [2]
(b) The crumple zone ensures the collision lasts 0.45 s. Calculate the average force acting on the car during the impact. [2]
(c) Explain, in terms of momentum and time, how an airbag reduces the risk of injury to the driver. [3]
1500 kg 汽车以 18 m/s 撞墙静止，溃缩区使碰撞历时 0.45 s，计算碰撞初动量、平均冲击力，并默写气囊减伤 3 分解释。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 7 Marks)

C1 (a) p = m v = 1500 × 18 A1 27 000 kg m/s (or N s).

C1 (b) F = Δp / Δt = 27 000 ÷ 0.45 A1 60 000 N.

B1 (c) The driver experiences the same change in momentum (to come to rest);
B1 the airbag increases the collision/impact time Δt;
B1 since F = Δp / Δt, the average force acting on the driver is reduced.

中文精析：三点缺一不可：① same change in momentum；② increases time of impact；③ reduces average force (F = Δp/Δt)。

SME 4 · 正交矢量合力与卫星圆周向心加速度Extended [7 marks]
Two tugboats pull a ship simultaneously: one pulls with a force of 6000 N due East, and the other pulls with a force of 8000 N due North.
(a) Calculate the magnitude and direction (angle north of east) of the resultant force on the ship. [4]
(b) A communications satellite orbits the Earth in a circular path at constant speed. Explain why it is accelerating and state the direction of the resultant force acting on it. [3]
两拖船分别向东拉 6000 N、向北拉 8000 N，求合力大小及与正东方向夹角；并解释匀速绕地卫星为何存在加速度及其合力方向。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 7 Marks)

C1 (a) R = √(6000² + 8000²) A1 10 000 N;
C1 tan θ = 8000 ÷ 6000 = 1.333 A1 53.1° North of East (or bearing 036.9°).

B1 (b) Although speed is constant, the direction of motion is continuously changing;
B1 so its velocity is changing (hence it is accelerating);
B1 the resultant gravitational force acts towards the centre of the Earth (perpendicular to the satellite's motion).

中文精析：矢量题若只算 10 000 N 不写角度 θ = 53.1° 会扣一半分数；圆周运动必答“direction changes → velocity changes”。

👆 点击查看参考答案
Mass is a measure of the quantity of matter in an object, and its resistance to change in motion (inertia) when at rest or moving.
C03 · 3.2 Weight & gCore
Define weight and gravitational field strength, stating Earth's standard value.
定义重力与重力场强度，并写出地球表面标准数值（2023–2028 新纲）。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Weight is a gravitational force on an object that has mass. Gravitational field strength g is force per unit mass (g = W / m = 9.8 N/kg on Earth).
C04 · 3.2 Beam BalanceCore
Why does a beam balance give the same reading on the Moon as on Earth, whereas a newton meter does not?
为什么托盘天平在月球和地球读数一样，而弹簧测力计读数不同？
👆 点击查看参考答案
A beam balance compares unknown mass with standard masses (gravity affects both sides equally). A newton meter measures weight, which drops because g on the Moon is smaller.
C05 · 3.3 Terminal VelocityExtended
Explain how a falling object reaches terminal velocity in terms of weight and air resistance.
从重力和空气阻力角度解释下落物体如何达到终端速度。
👆 点击查看参考答案
As speed increases, air resistance increases, reducing the resultant downward force and acceleration. Eventually air resistance equals weight (resultant force = 0), so speed becomes constant.
C06 · 3.3 Parachute OpeningExtended
What happens to the forces and motion immediately after a skydiver opens the parachute?
跳伞员刚打开降落伞的瞬间，受力、合力方向与运动状态如何变化？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Air resistance suddenly becomes much greater than weight, giving an upward resultant force. The skydiver decelerates (still moving downwards) until air resistance drops back equal to weight.
C07 · 3.3 Stopping DistanceCore
Distinguish thinking distance from braking distance and list two factors affecting each.
区分反应距离与刹车距离，并各列举两个影响因素。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Thinking distance (proportional to v): speed, driver tiredness / alcohol / distractions. Braking distance (proportional to v²): speed, worn tyres/brakes, wet/icy road, vehicle mass.
C08 · 3.3 Circular MotionExtended

Acts perpendicular to motion, towards the centre of the circle. Force increases if mass increases, speed increases, or radius decreases.
C09 · 3.4 Newton's 2nd LawExtended

👆 点击查看参考答案

Define momentum and state its SI units.

👆 点击查看参考答案
Momentum is the product of mass and velocity (p = m v). It is a vector quantity; unit is kg m/s (or N s).
C11 · 3.5 Impulse & ForceExtended
Define impulse and express resultant force in terms of momentum change.
定义冲量，并用动量变化率写出合力的表达式。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Impulse = F Δt = Δp = m(v − u). Resultant force is the rate of change of momentum (F = Δp / Δt).
C12 · 3.5 Crumple ZonesExtended
How do crumple zones, airbags, and crash helmets reduce injuries during collisions?
默写汽车溃缩区、气囊和安全头盔降低伤害的 Mark Scheme 标准句型。
👆 点击查看参考答案
For the same change in momentum Δp, they increase the collision time Δt, which reduces the average force (F = Δp / Δt) on the occupant.
C13 · 3.5 Conservation LawExtended
State the Principle of Conservation of Momentum and its required condition.
默写动量守恒定律及其成立前提条件。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Provided no external resultant force acts, the total momentum before a collision/explosion equals the total momentum after (m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂).
C14 · 3.5 Recoil / Opposite SignExtended
When a stationary cannon fires a shell forwards, why does the cannon recoil backwards?
静止大炮向前发射炮弹时，为何炮身必然向后反冲？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Initial total momentum is zero. By conservation of momentum, total momentum remains zero (m_shell v_shell + m_cannon v_cannon = 0), so cannon gains equal and opposite momentum.

A scalar has magnitude only (distance, speed, mass, energy, density, pressure). A vector has both magnitude and direction (displacement, velocity, force, weight, momentum, acceleration).
