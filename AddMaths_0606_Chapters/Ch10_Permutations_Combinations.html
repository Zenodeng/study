10.1 阶乘记号与含未知数阶乘化简

Factorial Notation: n!, n! / (n − r)! & Simplifying Algebraic Factorials
Paper 1 无计算器约分技巧 0! = 1 的本质约定

10.1.1 阶乘定义、递推性质与 0! = 1

对正整数 n：n! = n × (n − 1) × (n − 2) × ··· × 2 × 1  |  特殊约定：0! = 1
剥壳递推律：n! = n · (n − 1)! = n(n − 1) · (n − 2)!  |  n!(n − r)! = n(n − 1)···(n − r + 1)

为什么 0! = 1？由递推式 n! = n · (n − 1)! 代入 n = 1 得 1! = 1 · 0!，且“将 0 个物品排列”有且仅有 1 种（空排列）方式，故 0! = 1。

📝 无计算器阶乘约分与代数求解

题目： (a) Evaluate 10!7! · 3! without a calculator.   (b) Solve (n + 2)! = 30 · n! for positive integer n.   (c) Simplify n! + (n + 1)!(n − 1)!.

解析：
(a) 大阶乘向小阶乘“剥皮”约分：10 × 9 × 8 × 7!7! × 3 × 2 × 1 = 10 × 9 × 86 = 10 × 3 × 4 = 120。
(b) 把左侧展开到 n!：(n + 2)(n + 1) · n! = 30 · n!，两边消去 n! > 0：
n² + 3n + 2 = 30 ⇒ n² + 3n − 28 = 0 ⇒ (n − 4)(n + 7) = 0。因 n ∈ ℤ⁺，故 n = 4（舍去 −7）。
(c) 分子先提公因式 n!：n! [1 + (n + 1)](n − 1)! = n(n − 1)! (n + 2)(n − 1)! = n(n + 2)。

10.2 乘法计数原理与受限数字排列

Arrangements & Counting Principle: Digit Restrictions, Parity & Case-Splitting
先排“最苛刻位置”铁律 首位非零与奇偶冲突分情况

10.2.1 乘法原理 vs 加法原理，以及数字排列三大陷阱

  • 乘法原理（Multiplication Principle / AND）：完成一件事分 k 个依次步骤，各步选择数相乘：N = n₁ × n₂ × ··· × nₖ。
  • 加法原理（Addition Principle / OR）：互斥的多种情况（Cases），各情况数相加。
  • 框格填数三大考场红线：
    1. 看清是否允许重复使用数字（with repetition vs without repetition / distinct digits）。
    2. 最受限制的位置优先填（首位不能为 0；末位决定奇偶数或 5 的倍数；首位决定是否 > k）。
    3. 当首位限制与末位限制抢同一个数字池（尤其是 0 或同时可用做首位的数字）时，必须按互斥 Case 拆分！

10.3 排列 nPr：相邻捆绑法、不相邻插空法与重复元素排列

Permutations nPr: Glue/Bundle Method, Gap/Slot Method & Identical Repeated Letters
捆绑乘法 · 插空先排后插 k ≥ 3 严禁用“总减捆绑”！

10.3.1 排列公式与三大经典受限模型

1. 有序选取排列：ⁿPᵣ = n!(n − r)!（顺序敏感：AB ≠ BA）
2. 含重复元素全排列（n 个中有 p 个相同A、q 个相同B…）：n!p! q! ···
  • 模型 A · 相邻捆绑法（Adjacent / Together — Glue & Bundle）： 将必须挨在一起的 k 个元素粘成 1 个超级大块，与其余 n − k 个元素一起共 (n − k + 1) 个大块全排列，再乘大块内部排列数：
    (n − k + 1)! × k!
  • 模型 B · 不相邻插空法（Separated / No two adjacent — Gap & Slot）： 先将不受限的 (n − k) 个元素排好（共 (n − k)! 种），它们左右及两头产生 (n − k + 1) 个空隙，再从这些空隙中挑出 k 个有序插入：
    (n − k)! × ⁿ⁻ᵏ⁺¹Pₖ（若插入的 k 个元素本身全同，则改为乘以 ⁿ⁻ᵏ⁺¹Cₖ！）。
🚫 致命误区：3 个及以上元素要求“互不相邻（No two adjacent）”，绝对不能用 Total − All Together！

当 k = 2 时，“不相邻”只有 2 个分开，用 Total − Together 确实等于插空法。
但当 k ≥ 3（如 3 个元音互不相邻）时，All_Together 只减掉了“3 个全粘在一起”的情况，漏减了“2 个元音粘在一起、另 1 个单飞”的情况！因此 3 个及以上要求互不相邻，必须老老实实用插空法（Gap Method）！

📝 含重复字母 + 不相邻混合题（PARALLEL）

题目： Find the number of different arrangements of the 8 letters of the word PARALLEL in which: (a) there are no restrictions, (b) all three L's are together, (c) no two L's are adjacent.

解析： 字母统计：P(1), A(2), R(1), L(3), E(1)，共 8 个字母。
(a) 总排列数：8!2! · 3! = 40 3202 × 6 = 3360。
(b) 3 个 L 捆绑成 1 个大块 [LLL]，与 P, A, A, R, E 共 6 块（含 2 个相同 A；[LLL] 内部相同只有 1 种排法！）：6!2! = 360。
(c) 先排非 L 的 5 个字母 P, A, A, R, E：5!2! = 60 种；产生 6 个空隙，由于 3 个 L 完全相同，只需用组合挑出 3 个空隙放入（无需再排列！）：⁶C₃ = 20。总计 60 × 20 = 1200。

10.4 组合 nCr：“至少/至多”、补集策略与先选后排混合题

Combinations nCr: Unordered Selection, Complement Rule & Choose-Then-Arrange
选团队不看顺序用 nCr “至少 1 个”优先用总减无

10.4.1 组合公式、性质与三大解题策略

无序组合：ⁿCᵣ = nr = n!r!(n − r)! = ⁿPᵣr!  |  对称性：ⁿCᵣ = ⁿCₙ₋ᵣ  |  ⁿC₀ = ⁿCₙ = 1
题型策略 识别关键词 考场标准解法
1. 分类相加 vs 补集减法 at least, at most（至少/至多） 若正面情况少（如 2 种），直接相加；若出现 “at least 1”，一律用补集：Total − (None) 最快不漏！
2. 某两人绝不同组 A and B refuse to be together / cannot both be chosen 补集秒杀： (无限制总数) − (A 与 B 均被选中的情况)。例如从 10 人选 4 人，甲乙不同队：¹⁰C₄ − ⁸C₂ = 210 − 28 = 182。
3. 先选后排混合题 selected … and then arranged in a row 严格两步走：Step 1 用 ⁿCᵣ 挑出人选；Step 2 用阶乘 / 捆绑 / 插空把选出的人排队，两者相乘！
📝 经典混合例题：组队 + 特定限制

题目： A committee of 5 is chosen from 6 men and 4 women. Find the number of committees that include at least 2 women and at least 1 man.

解析：
• 补集法：总选法 ¹⁰C₅ = 252。不合格情况为：0 女 5 男 (⁶C₅ = 6)、1 女 4 男 (⁴C₁ × ⁶C₄ = 4 × 15 = 60)、0 男 5 女 (0，因仅 4 女)。
合法选法 = 252 − (6 + 60) = 186。
• 正面核对：2女3男 (⁶C₃·⁴C₂ = 20×6 = 120) + 3女2男 (15×4 = 60) + 4女1男 (6×1 = 6) = 186 ✓。

📝 Part B · SaveMyExams 综合解答大题 (Structured Questions)

SME 10.1 · 代数排列组合证明与求解[6 marks]
(a) Show that ⁿP₃ − ⁿP₂ = n(n − 1)(n − 3) for n ≥ 3. [3]
(b) Hence solve the equation ⁿP₃ − ⁿP₂ = 6 · ⁿC₂. [3]
(a) 化简证明排列恒等式；(b) 由此解出满足条件的正整数 n。
查看 SaveMyExams 标准采分步骤 (Mark Scheme · 6 Marks)

M1 ⁿP₃ = n(n − 1)(n − 2) and ⁿP₂ = n(n − 1).

M1 Factor out n(n − 1): LHS = n(n − 1)[(n − 2) − 1].

A1 = n(n − 1)(n − 3) (shown).

M1 (b) Substitute ⁿC₂ = n(n − 1)2 into (a): n(n − 1)(n − 3) = 6 · n(n − 1)2 = 3n(n − 1).

M1 Since n ≥ 3, n(n − 1) ≠ 0; divide through: n − 3 = 3.

A1 n = 6（检验：⁶P₃ − ⁶P₂ = 120 − 30 = 90，6 · ⁶C₂ = 6 × 15 = 90 ✓）。

SME 10.2 · 重复字母单词捆绑与插空综合 (ARRANGE)[7 marks]
Find the number of different ways of arranging all 7 letters of the word ARRANGE such that:
(a) there are no restrictions, [2]
(b) the two R's are next to each other, [2]
(c) no two vowels are next to each other. [3]
用 7 个字母单词 ARRANGE（含 A×2, R×2）求无限制、双 R 相邻、及任意两元音不相邻的排列数。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 7 Marks)

M1 (a) Letters: 2 A's, 2 R's, 1 N, 1 G, 1 E. Total = 7!2! · 2! A1 = 1260.

M1 (b) Bundle [RR] as 1 block → 6 blocks (with 2 A's): 6!2! A1 = 360.

M1 (c) Consonants R, R, N, G (4 letters, 2 R's) arranged in 4!2! = 12 ways, creating 5 gaps.

M1 Choose 3 gaps out of 5 and arrange vowels A, A, E: ⁵C₃ × 3!2! = 10 × 3 = 30.

A1 Total = 12 × 30 = 360.

SME 10.3 · 团队组合 + 拒绝同组 + 先选后排插空[8 marks]
A team of 5 students is to be chosen from 5 boys and 4 girls.
(a) Find the number of different teams that contain at least 3 boys. [3]
(b) One boy, Adam, and one girl, Bella, refuse to be in the team together. Find the number of teams with at least 3 boys in which Adam and Bella are not both included. [2]
(c) If a chosen team of 3 boys and 2 girls is seated in a row of 5 chairs, find the number of ways this can be done if the 2 girls do not sit next to each other. [3]
从 5 男 4 女中选 5 人小队：(a) 至少 3 男；(b) 满足至少 3 男且男女两人不同队；(c) 选 3 男 2 女并排就座且两女不相邻。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 8 Marks)

M1 (a) Cases (3B2G, 4B1G, 5B0G): ⁵C₃ · ⁴C₂ + ⁵C₄ · ⁴C₁ + ⁵C₅ · ⁴C₀ (M1).

A1 = 10(6) + 5(4) + 1(1) = 60 + 20 + 1 = 81.

M1 (b) Subtract teams with both Adam & Bella AND at least 3 boys (need 3 more from remaining 4B, 3G with ≥2B): ⁴C₂ · ³C₁ + ⁴C₃ · ³C₀ = 18 + 4 = 22.

A1 Valid teams = 81 − 22 = 59.

M1 (c) Choose 3B2G: ⁵C₃ · ⁴C₂ = 60；M1 Arrange 3 boys (3! = 6) and slot 2 girls into 4 gaps (⁴P₂ = 12).

A1 Total = 60 × 6 × 12 = 4320.

考前核心概念与计数策略默写库 (Chapter 10 · 14 题)

Bilingual Permutations & Combinations Concept & Pitfall Flashcards
显示 14 / 14 题
PC01 · 10.1Factorial
State the value of 0! and show how to simplify (n + 1)! / (n − 1)!.
写出 0! 的值并说明如何化简连续阶乘商。
👆 点击查看参考答案
0! = 1. Expand the larger factorial down to the smaller one: (n + 1)n(n − 1)! / (n − 1)! = n(n + 1).
PC02 · 10.2Multiplication vs Addition
When do you multiply counts vs add counts in combinatorics?
计数问题中什么时候用乘法（AND），什么时候用加法（OR）？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Multiply across consecutive stages of ONE task (AND); Add across mutually exclusive separate cases (OR).
PC03 · 10.2Most Restricted First
Which positions must always be filled first in constrained arrangement problems?
受限排列问题中，填槽位的先后顺序铁律是什么？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Always fill the most restricted positions first (e.g., first digit ≠ 0, last digit even/odd, or endpoints fixed).
PC04 · 10.2Case-Split Trap
Why must you split into Case 1 (ends in 0) and Case 2 (ends in 2, 4, …) when forming even numbers from a set containing 0 without repetition?
用含 0 的数字集组不重复偶数时，为什么要把“个位为 0”单独拆成一种情况？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Because 0 is forbidden in the leading digit! If 0 is already used at the end, the leading digit has 1 more available choice than if 2 or 4 is used at the end.
PC05 · 10.2Repetition Check
Contrast the number of 3-digit numbers from {1,2,3,4,5} with repetition allowed vs without repetition.
比较“可重复”与“不可重复”组成 3 位数的计算式差异。
👆 点击查看参考答案
With repetition: 5 × 5 × 5 = 5³ = 125. Without repetition: ⁵P₃ = 5 × 4 × 3 = 60.
PC06 · 10.3Perm vs Comb
Write the formulas for ⁿPᵣ and ⁿCᵣ and explain the single key difference between them.
写出排列 ⁿPᵣ 与组合 ⁿCᵣ 公式，并说明核心区别。
👆 点击查看参考答案
ⁿPᵣ = n!/(n−r)! (order matters, e.g. digits/lining up); ⁿCᵣ = n!/[r!(n−r)!] (order does NOT matter, e.g. teams/committees), with ⁿPᵣ = r! · ⁿCᵣ.
PC07 · 10.3Glue Method
How do you arrange n distinct items when k specific items must all be adjacent ("together")?
默写 k 个元素必须“相邻（捆绑法）”的公式与两层逻辑。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Glue the k items into 1 super-block → arrange (n − k + 1) blocks, then multiply by internal permutations: (n − k + 1)! × k!.
PC08 · 10.3Gap Method
How do you arrange n distinct items when k specific items must have NO two adjacent ("separated")?
默写“互不相邻（插空法）”的标准两步公式。
👆 点击查看参考答案
First arrange the other (n − k) items in (n − k)! ways, creating (n − k + 1) gaps (including both ends), then slot k items: (n − k)! × ⁿ⁻ᵏ⁺¹Pₖ.
PC09 · 10.3k ≥ 3 Trap
Why does Total − All Together give the WRONG answer when 3 items must have no two adjacent?
为什么当 3 个元素互不相邻时，“总数 − 3 个全捆绑”会算错？
👆 点击查看参考答案
It fails to subtract cases where 2 of the 3 items are adjacent while the 3rd is separate! Always use the Gap Method for k ≥ 3.
PC10 · 10.3Repeated Items
How do you find distinct permutations of n items containing p identical A's and q identical B's?
含相同重复元素的全排列公式是什么？如果把 3 个相同字母 L 插空，空隙选法用 P 还是 C？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Divide out identical swaps: n! / (p! q! …). When slotting identical L's into m gaps, use ᵐCₖ (not ᵐPₖ, since identical L's have only 1 internal order!).
PC11 · 10.4Symmetry
State the symmetry property of ⁿCᵣ and use it to solve ¹⁵Cₓ = ¹⁵C₂ₓ₋₃ (two possible values!).
写出组合对称恒等式，并指出求解 ⁿCₐ = ⁿC_b 时的双解结构。
👆 点击查看参考答案
ⁿCᵣ = ⁿCₙ₋ᵣ. Either a = b (x = 2x−3 ⇒ x = 3) OR a + b = n (x + 2x − 3 = 15 ⇒ x = 6).
PC12 · 10.4Complement
What is the fastest strategy for "at least 1" or "A and B cannot both be selected"?
“至少有 1 个”与“甲乙不同组”题型的统一高效策略是什么？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Use the Complement Method: Total − Unwanted (i.e., Total − None selected, or Total − Both A and B selected).
PC13 · 10.4Choose + Arrange
How do you count 5-letter passwords made by choosing 2 vowels from 5 and 3 consonants from 6, then arranging them?
“从多组中分别抽取再排列”遵循什么计算步骤？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Choose first, then Arrange: (⁵C₂ × ⁶C₃) × 5! (apply any adjacency/end rules in the arrange step!).
PC14 · 10.4Division vs Team
Compare splitting 6 people into a named Team A (3) & Team B (3) vs two unnamed groups of 3.
把 6 人分成“有名称的甲乙两队（各 3 人）”与“无名称的两组（各 3 人）”有何区别？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Named teams: ⁶C₃ × ³C₃ = 20. Unnamed equal-sized groups: divide by 2! to avoid double-counting identical splits ⇒ 20 / 2! = 10.
