8.1 弧度制 (Radians) 与无计算器特殊角精确三角值

8.1 Radian Measure & Exact Values for π/6, π/4, π/3 Without a Calculator
Paper 1 禁用计算器：背熟两大特殊三角形 Paper 2 务必把计算器切换为 RAD 模式

8.1.1 弧度定义与角度/弧度精确互化

1 弧度 (1 radian) 定义为：当圆弧的长度恰好等于圆半径 r 时，该圆弧所对的圆心角即为 1 rad（1 rad ≈ 57.3°）。由于整圆周长为 2πr，一周角 360° = 2π rad，因此核心换算恒等式为：

π rad = 180°   |   角度 → 弧度：× π180°   |   弧度 → 角度：× 180°π

Paper 1 书写规范：常见角度换弧度必须保留 π 的最简分数形式（例如 150° = 5π6，225° = 5π4），不要乘出小数 3.14159！

8.2 弧长公式 s = rθ 与扇形周长问题

8.2 Length of an Arc (s = rθ) & Perimeter of Minor / Major Sectors
θ 必须先化为弧度 Radian! 扇形周长 = s + 2r（勿漏两条半径!）

8.2.1 弧长与周长公式及优弧/劣弧区分

弧长 (Arc Length)：s = r θ   |   扇形周长 (Sector Perimeter)：P = r θ + 2r = r(θ + 2)

重大区分：① Minor arc / sector (劣弧/小扇形) 对应圆心角 θ < π；② Major arc / sector (优弧/大扇形) 对应圆心角 2π − θ。
③ 周长必加两条直边：算扇形周长 (Perimeter of sector OAB) 时，除圆弧 rθ 外必须加上半径 OA + OB = 2r！

例题 8.2 · 优扇形周长反推半径与角度

圆心角为 120° 的劣扇形 OAB 半径为 r = 6 cm，求优扇形 (Major Sector) 的精确弧长与周长。
解：劣弧圆心角 120° = 2π3 rad ⇒ 优扇形圆心角 θmajor = 2π − 2π3 = 4π3 rad。
优弧长 s = 6 × 4π3 = 8π cm；优扇形周长 = 8π + 2(6) = (8π + 12) cm。

8.3 扇形面积、弓形面积、弦长与组合阴影区域

8.3 Area of a Sector (½r²θ), Segment ½r²(θ − sin θ), Chord 2r sin(θ/2) & Compound Shaded Regions
弓形 = 扇形 − 三角形 五大组合图形割补母题模板

8.3.1 扇形、弓形与弦长三大核心公式

1. 扇形面积 (Sector Area)：Asector = 12 r² θ = 12 r s
2. 弓形面积 (Segment Area)：Asegment = 12 r² θ − 12 r² sin θ = 12 r² (θ − sin θ)
3. 弦长公式 (Chord Length AB)：AB = 2r sin(θ2)   [或余弦定理 √2r²(1 − cos θ)]

优弓形 (Major Segment) 技巧：用“整圆面积 πr² 减去劣弓形面积”最快！若直接代公式 12r²(θmajor − sin θmajor)，注意因 θmajor > π 故 sin θmajor < 0，负负得正等效于加上三角形 ΔOAB！

例题 8.3A · Paper 1 精确弓形面积与周长 (r = 6, θ = 2π/3)

圆半径 r = 6 cm，圆心角 θ = 2π3 rad。求劣弓形 (Minor Segment) 的精确周长与面积。
① 弦长 AB = 2(6) sin(π3) = 12 × √32 = 6√3 cm；弧长 s = 6(2π3) = 4π cm。
② 弓形周长 = 弧长 + 弦长 = (4π + 6√3) cm（注意弓形周长不是加 2r！）。
③ 弓形面积 = 12(36)(2π3 − sin 2π3) = 18(2π3 − √32) = (12π − 9√3) cm²。

8.3.2 五大组合阴影区域 (Shaded Compound Regions) & 扇形内切模型

高频组合图形类型面积拆解策略 (Area)周长拆解策略 (Perimeter — 沿着阴影外框走一圈！)
1. 切线直角三角形减扇形
（AT 切圆于 A，交半径延长线于 T）		 Area(ΔOAT) − Area(OAB)
= 12 r² (tan θ − θ)		 AT + BT + Arc(AB)
= r tan θ + (rcos θ − r) + rθ
2. 两圆相交重叠“透镜形”
（Lens Region）		 拆成两个背靠背的弓形：
Seg₁ + Seg₂		 只算外围两段圆弧：
r₁θ₁ + r₂θ₂（绝不能加中间公共弦！）
3. 扇形内接三角形
（如直径半圆内接直角三角形）		 先用直角或正弦/余弦定理求内接三角形边长，再 Sector − Δ 半圆内接角 ∠APB = 90° ⇒ PA = 2r cos α, PB = 2r sin α
4. 扇形内切小圆
（圆心距 R − ρ 沿角平分线）		 利用垂直切线直角三角形：
sin(θ2) = ρR − ρ 求出 ρ		 阴影 = Sector − πρ²；周长 = Rθ + 2R + 2πρ
5. 定周长扇形最大面积
（如周长固定为 P）		 由 2r + rθ = P ⇒ rθ = P − 2r
代入 A = 12r(P − 2r) = P2r − r²		 配方求最值：当 r = P4 时面积最大，此时恒有 θ = 2 rad！
例题 8.3B · 切线直角三角形阴影 (r = 6, θ = π/3) 与定周长最值

(a) 如图 8.2 右图，扇形 OAB 半径 r = 6 cm，∠AOB = π3，AT 为圆在 A 点的切线交 OB 延长线于 T。求阴影区域 ABT 的精确周长与面积。
(b) 若某扇形周长固定为 20 cm，求其最大面积及对应的圆心角 θ。

(a) 因为 ∠OAT = π2：AT = 6 tan(π3) = 6√3 cm；OT = 6cos(π/3) = 12 cm ⇒ BT = 12 − 6 = 6 cm；弧 AB = 6(π3) = 2π cm。
阴影周长 = (6√3 + 6 + 2π) cm；阴影面积 = 12(6)(6√3) − 12(36)(π3) = (18√3 − 6π) cm²。
(b) 2r + rθ = 20 ⇒ rθ = 20 − 2r ⇒ A = 12r(20 − 2r) = 10r − r² = 25 − (r − 5)²。
当 r = 5 cm 时面积最大为 25 cm²，此时 θ = 20 − 105 = 2 rad。

SME 8.1 · Paper 1 无计算器精确劣弓形与优弓形面积[6 marks]
A circle has centre O and radius 12 cm. Chord AB subtends an angle of π3 radians at O.
(a) Find the exact perimeter of the minor segment bounded by chord AB and arc AB. [3]
(b) Find the exact area of the minor segment and hence the exact area of the major segment. [3]
无计算器：求劣弓形的精确周长，以及劣弓形与优弓形的精确面积。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 6 Marks)

B1 (a) Minor arc AB = 12 × π3 = 4π cm.

M1 Chord AB = 2(12) sin(π6) = 24 × 12 = 12 cm (since ΔOAB is equilateral).

A1 Perimeter of minor segment = (4π + 12) cm.

M1 (b) Minor segment area = 12(12)²(π3 − sin π3) = 72(π3 − √32) = (24π − 36√3) cm² (A1).

A1 Major segment area = π(12)² − (24π − 36√3) = (120π + 36√3) cm².

SME 8.2 · 切线直角三角形减扇形组合阴影 (Paper 2 RAD)[7 marks]
OAB is a sector of a circle of radius 8 cm with ∠AOB = 1.1 radians. The tangent to the circle at A meets OB produced at T.
(a) Find the lengths AT and BT, and hence the perimeter of the shaded region ABT, correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
(b) Find the area of the shaded region ABT to 3 significant figures. [3]
计算切线与扇形外围所夹阴影区域 ABT 的周长与面积（计算器务必使用 RAD 模式！）。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 7 Marks)

M1 (a) Since ∠OAT = π2, AT = 8 tan(1.1) = 15.718… cm (in RAD mode!).

M1 OT = 8cos(1.1) = 17.637… cm ⇒ BT = 17.637 − 8 = 9.637… cm.

B1 Arc AB = 8 × 1.1 = 8.8 cm.

A1 Perimeter = 15.718 + 9.637 + 8.8 = 34.2 cm (to 3 s.f.).

M1 (b) Area ΔOAT = 12(8)(15.718…) = 62.872… cm²；Sector OAB = 12(8)²(1.1) = 35.2 cm² (M1).

A1 Shaded area = 62.872 − 35.2 = 27.7 cm² (to 3 s.f.).

SME 8.3 · 两圆互相过圆心交叠“透镜”精确面积与周长 (Paper 1)[7 marks]
Two identical circles of radius 6 cm have centres O₁ and O₂ such that each circle passes through the centre of the other. The circles intersect at A and B.
(a) Explain why ∠AO₁B = 2π3 radians. [2]
(b) Find the exact perimeter of the overlapping lens region bounded by the two arcs AB. [2]
(c) Find the exact area of this overlapping lens region. [3]
两等圆各自经过对方圆心，证明圆心角为 2π/3 并求重叠透镜区的精确周长与面积。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 7 Marks)

M1 (a) O₁A = O₂A = O₁O₂ = 6 cm (all radii!), so ΔO₁AO₂ and ΔO₁BO₂ are equilateral.

A1 Each angle ∠AO₁O₂ = ∠BO₁O₂ = π3 ⇒ ∠AO₁B = 2π3 rad.

M1 (b) Lens perimeter = 2 × arc AB = 2 × 6(2π3) = 8π cm (A1).

M1 (c) Area of one segment = 12(6)²(2π3 − sin 2π3) = 18(2π3 − √32) = 12π − 9√3.

M1 Lens area = 2 × segment = (24π − 18√3) cm² (A1).

SME 8.4 · 扇形内切圆精确半径与阴影面积 (Paper 1)[7 marks]
A sector OAB has centre O, radius 9 cm and ∠AOB = π3 radians. A circle of centre C and radius ρ cm is inscribed inside the sector so that it touches OA, OB and the arc AB.
(a) Express OC in terms of ρ and show that ρ = 3. [4]
(b) Find the exact area of the region inside sector OAB that lies outside the inscribed circle. [3]
半径 9 cm、圆心角 π/3 的扇形内切一圆，求内切圆半径 ρ 及扇形内余下阴影精确面积。
查看评分细则 (Mark Scheme · 7 Marks)

B1 (a) Since the circle touches arc AB internally, OC = 9 − ρ.

M1 By symmetry, OC bisects ∠AOB, so in right triangle with radius to OA (⊥ OA), angle is π6:

M1 sin(π6) = ρ9 − ρ ⇒ 12 = ρ9 − ρ.

A1 9 − ρ = 2ρ ⇒ 3ρ = 9 ⇒ ρ = 3.

M1 (b) Sector area = 12(9)²(π3) = 27π2；Circle area = π(3)² = 9π (M1).

A1 Shaded area = 27π2 − 9π = 9π2 cm².

C8-01 · 8.1 弧度定义Radian
Define 1 radian geometrically and state the exact conversion between degrees and radians.
写出 1 弧度的几何定义及角度与弧度的精确换算关系。
👆 点击查看参考答案
1 radian is the angle subtended at the centre when arc length equals radius (s = r). Conversion: π rad = 180° (× π/180 to rad, × 180/π to deg).
C8-02 · 8.1 π/4 三角形Exact Trig
State exact sin, cos, tan of π4 and 3π4 without a calculator.
无计算器默写 π/4 与 3π/4 的 sin, cos, tan 精确值。
👆 点击查看参考答案
From 1–1–√2 triangle: sin(π/4) = cos(π/4) = √2/2, tan(π/4) = 1. In Q2 (3π/4): sin stays +√2/2, while cos = −√2/2 and tan = −1.
C8-03 · 8.1 π/6 三角形Exact Trig
State exact sin, cos, tan of π6 and π3 from the 1–√3–2 triangle.
利用 1−√3−2 直角三角形默写 π/6 与 π/3 的精确三角值。
👆 点击查看参考答案
sin(π/6) = cos(π/3) = ½；cos(π/6) = sin(π/3) = √3/2；tan(π/6) = 1/√3；tan(π/3) = √3.
C8-04 · 8.1 钝角符号Obtuse Angle
What are the exact values of sin(2π3), cos(2π3), sin(5π6), cos(5π6)?
写出扇形钝角 2π/3 与 5π/6 的正余弦精确值。
👆 点击查看参考答案
In Q2, sin is positive and cos is negative: sin(2π/3) = √3/2, cos(2π/3) = −½；sin(5π/6) = ½, cos(5π/6) = −√3/2.
C8-05 · 8.1 考场模式Calculator Trap
What is the #1 calculator mistake in Paper 2 circular measure questions?
Paper 2 做弧度制计算题最致命的计算器误操作是什么？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Leaving calculator in DEG mode when computing sin(1.2) or tan(0.8)! Always check for the R / RAD indicator on screen.
C8-06 · 8.2 弧长Arc Length
Write the arc length formula s and the mandatory unit requirement on θ.
默写弧长公式及对圆心角 θ 的单位前提条件。
👆 点击查看参考答案
s = r θ, where θ MUST be in radians! If given θ in degrees, convert by × π/180 before using s = rθ.
C8-07 · 8.2 扇形周长Perimeter
Why is the perimeter of a sector NOT equal to rθ, and what is the correct formula?
为什么扇形周长不等于 rθ？正确公式是什么？
👆 点击查看参考答案
A sector has two straight radii as well as the curved arc: Perimeter = rθ + 2r = r(θ + 2).
C8-08 · 8.2 优劣转换Major vs Minor
If minor sector angle is θ, what is the angle of the major sector?
若劣扇形圆心角为 θ 弧度，则优扇形圆心角是多少？
👆 点击查看参考答案
θmajor = 2π − θ (remember full circle = 2π, NOT 360!).
C8-09 · 8.3 扇形面积Sector Area
State both forms of the area of a sector (in terms of r, θ and in terms of r, s).
写出扇形面积的两个等价公式。
👆 点击查看参考答案
A = ½ r² θ and A = ½ r s (since s = rθ; super handy when given arc length and area!).
C8-10 · 8.3 弓形面积Segment Area
State the minor segment area formula and explain how each term arises geometrically.
默写劣弓形面积公式，并说明其中两项的几何来源。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Area = ½ r² (θ − sin θ) = Sector (½r²θ) minus Isosceles Triangle OAB (½r² sin θ).
C8-11 · 8.3 弦长公式Chord Length
Give TWO exact formulas for chord length AB of a circle of radius r and angle θ.
写出求半径 r、圆心角 θ 的圆弦长 AB 的两个公式。
👆 点击查看参考答案
① Half-angle right triangle: AB = 2r sin(θ/2); ② Cosine rule: AB = √[2r²(1 − cos θ)]. Note perimeter of segment = rθ + 2r sin(θ/2).
C8-12 · 8.3 优弓形Major Segment
What is the safest way to find the area of a MAJOR segment without sign errors?
怎样求优弓形 (Major Segment) 面积最不容易算错符号？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Areamajor = πr² − Areaminor segment = πr² − ½r²(θ − sin θ) (or Major Sector + Triangle OAB).
C8-13 · 切线阴影Tangent Sector
Tangent at A meets OB produced at T (∠AOB = θ). Write the area and perimeter of shaded ABT.
写出切线三角形 OAT 减去扇形 OAB 的阴影面积与周长。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Since ∠OAT = 90°, AT = r tan θ and OT = r / cos θ. Area = ½ r² (tan θ − θ); Perimeter = r tan θ + (r/cos θ − r) + rθ.
C8-14 · 重叠透镜Overlapping
How do you find the perimeter and area of the overlapping lens of two intersecting circles?
如何拆解两个圆重叠形成的“透镜区域”的周长与面积？
👆 点击查看参考答案
Area = sum of two segments (Seg₁ + Seg₂); Perimeter = Arc₁ + Arc₂ (NEVER include the internal common chord!).
C8-15 · 扇形内切圆Inscribed Circle
A circle of radius ρ is inscribed in a sector of radius R and angle θ. Write the relation linking ρ, R, θ.
写出扇形内切圆半径 ρ 与扇形半径 R、圆心角 θ 满足的关系式。
👆 点击查看参考答案
Line of centres passes along angle bisector (θ/2) with OC = R − ρ: sin(θ/2) = ρ / (R − ρ).
C8-16 · 定周长最值Max Sector Area
For any sector with fixed perimeter P, what value of θ maximises its area?
周长 P 固定的扇形，圆心角 θ 为多少时面积达到最大值？
👆 点击查看参考答案
A = ½r(P − 2r) = ½Pr − r² peaks at r = P/4, giving arc s = P/2 ⇒ θ = 2 radians ALWAYS!
