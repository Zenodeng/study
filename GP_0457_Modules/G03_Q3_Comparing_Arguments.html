3.1 Q3 (15–16 分) Level 4 评分真相与“六维交叉对比框架”

Component 1 Q3: Official Level 4 Mark Scheme Truth, Fatal Pitfalls & 6-Dimension Cross-Comparison Matrix
15–16 Marks · 建议用时 20 min 只评“论证强度”，绝非表态“你同意谁”

一、Q3 官方 Level 4 (13–16 分) 评分真相与“两大致命低分错误”

在 Cambridge IGCSE Global Perspectives (0457 · 2025–2027) 笔试 Component 1 中，Question 3（15–16 分）通常围绕 Source 4（两位立场对立的作者、专家或利益相关方围绕同一全球议题展开辩论），提问形式高度固定：

“Which argument is more convincing, [Person A]’s or [Person B]’s? Your answer should consider both arguments and you should use examples from the source.”

阅卷真相：剑桥考官给 Q3 打分采用 Level-of-Response（4 档分阶给分）。要想突破 13–16 分的 Level 4，你的任务不是判断“谁的观点在道德上更正确”，而是像一名逻辑与证据审计员（Argument Auditor）一样，把两段文字拆解到“证据、样本、平衡度、偏见、推论链、修辞语调”六个维度上做同维正面交锋对比。

评分档位 (Level) 分值区间 考官核心判别标准 (Mark Scheme Descriptors)
Level 4 13–16 分 全程围绕 argument strength（论证强度） 开展持续、显性的同维比较（sustained explicit comparison）；精准引用双方原文例证评估证据、逻辑、偏见与平衡度；能兼顾较强方的微小局限或较弱方的局部亮点，给出有理有据的最终裁决。
Level 3 9–12 分 尝试比较双方论证与证据，但部分段落退化为“逐人概括观点”，或仅对比了 1–2 个浅层维度（如只谈“谁有数字、谁没数字”），缺少对既得利益、逻辑跳跃或反驳意识的深度剖析。
Level 2 5–8 分 典型封顶区！往往表现为“先写完 A 再写 B”的块状摘要（sequential summary），缺乏对比连接词；或者把重点放在复述主题观点与个人站队表态（“我觉得 A 说得对，因为环保重要”）。
Level 1 1–4 分 简单摘抄原文句子、仅做一句毫无依据的断言（“A is more convincing because she is a professor”），或完全只写其中一方。
🚨 考场两大致命低分错误（踩中任意一条直接痛失 Level 3 / Level 4！）

致命错误 ①：先写完 A 再写 B，全文无同维对比（锁死 Level 2，通常仅得 6–8 分）
很多考生习惯第一段夸完 A 的优点和缺点，第二段再夸/批 B 的优点和缺点，最后补一句“所以 A 更好”。这种写法叫 分头描述（Sequential Description）——两人的证据和逻辑从未放在同一把尺子上比较！考官会批注 “Two separate accounts; little sustained comparison”，直接封顶 Level 2。

致命错误 ②：大段复述立场“我同意谁谁说得对”，而不分析“如何论证 (Reasoning & Evidence)”（大量丢分！）
例如写：“I agree with Maya because climate change is killing people and banning cars will save the earth.” —— 这是在替作者继续吵架，而不是评估作者如何论证！哪怕你在现实中完全反感某位商业代表的立场，只要他在 Source 4 里拿出了更严密的第三方审计数据、充分回应了反方且语调克制，而另一位环保人士只靠情绪煽动和道听途说，Q3 满分答案也必须判定“前者在论证上更 convincing”。

二、Q3 制胜核心：六维逐点交叉对比框架 (The 6-Dimension Cross-Comparison Matrix)

审读 Source 4 时，直接在两段文字旁用铅笔标出以下 6 个维度序号 (D1–D6)。写作时每一个主体段锁定 2 个相邻维度，让 Person A 与 Person B 在同一句话或同一段落内形成直接对比：

对比维度 高频高分审计问题 (Audit Questions) 强论证特征 (Stronger) vs 弱论证特征 (Weaker)
D1 · 证据类型与可核验性
Evidence & Verifiability		 主张背后是否有明确年份、独立机构、同行评议研究或可复查数据支撑？ 强：引用具名独立研究（如 2024 OECD 18-city study）、可核验统计比例。
弱：仅靠个人轶事 (anecdotal evidence)、模糊传言 ("many people feel") 或无出处数字。
D2 · 样本与覆盖面
Sample & Scope		 证据的样本量多大？调查对象是否单一、地区/时间是否受限？ 强：多年份跨阶层/跨地区大样本追踪。
弱：便利抽样小样本（如“门口 45 位顾客的周末问卷”）、单城个案硬推全国 (hasty generalisation) 或过时旧数据。
D3 · 正反平衡度与反驳意识
Balance & Counter-argument		 作者是否承认现实代价、例外情况或对立观点，并给出合理回应？ 强：主动做让步与限定（concedes short-term disruption and proposes targeted exemptions）。
弱：单边主义 (one-sided)、刻意挑选有利事实 (cherry-picking)、完全回避自身方案的成本。
D4 · 既得利益与视角偏见
Vested Interest & Bias		 作者的身份、资金来源或商业利益是否削弱其中立性？隐藏了哪些未证前提？ 强：独立学术/公共健康评估机构，利益冲突较低，公开前提条件。
弱：行业协会会长、软件/产品创始人直接受益于该政策（vested interest），或暗藏未证假设 (unstated assumption)。
D5 · 因果推论链与逻辑跳跃
Causal Chain & Logic		 前提能否合理推出结论？中间有没有跳跃、滑坡谬误或非黑即白二分法？ 强：机制清晰（政策措施 → 行为激励/中间变量 → 可测结果）。
弱：把相关性当因果 (correlation vs causation)、滑坡恐吓 ("will turn into ghost towns overnight")、非此即彼 (false dichotomy)。
D6 · 语言客观度 vs 情绪渲染
Objective Tone vs Emotive Language		 作者用严谨学术限定语，还是用煽动性形容词、道德绑架和绝对词？ 强：克制、精确限定（tends to, indicates, in peak-hour corridors）。
弱：极端夸张与情绪操弄（outrageous war, intellectual poison, guaranteed, completely worthless）。

Four-Paragraph Level 4 Template, Dialectical Concession Strategy & Interactive Comparison Builder

一、Q3 四段式高分模板与英文衔接骨架（考场 20 分钟标准配时）

Q3 不需要花哨修辞或冗长背景引言。300–350 词的四段密集比较论证远胜过 500 词的水分复述。请严格遵循以下四段骨架推进：

段落与耗时 核心得分任务 考场直接套用英文衔接骨架 (Sentence Frames)
Paragraph 1
开篇定调
(~50 词 · 2 min)		 直接表明哪一方的论证总体更具说服力，并用 1 句话定下两大比较支柱（如“实证可靠性”与“平衡客观度”）。 Overall, [Person A]’s argument is significantly more convincing than [Person B]’s, primarily because it is anchored in verifiable empirical evidence and a balanced consideration of trade-offs, whereas [Person B] relies heavily on emotive rhetoric and unrepresentative claims.
Paragraph 2
维度 D1+D2+D5
证据与逻辑正面交锋
(~120 词 · 7 min)		 正面强对比双方证据、样本与因果推论链：必须同时引用 A 与 B 的具体原文例子，用 Whereas / By contrast 对撞。 Firstly, in terms of evidence quality and scope, [Person A] substantiates their case with [specific source/year/stat], establishing a clear causal link between [X] and [Y]. By contrast, [Person B] bases their broad claim on [anecdote / biased small poll], committing a hasty generalisation without independent verification.
Paragraph 3
维度 D3+D4+D6
平衡、偏见交锋 + 高阶辩证让步
(~120 词 · 7 min)		 对比双方反驳意识、既得利益与语言语调，并特别写入高阶辩证：点出较强方的 1 处微小局限，同时承认较弱方的 1 处局部亮点！ Secondly, regarding balance and objectivity, [Person A] adopts a measured register and explicitly concedes [limitation/counter-point], whereas [Person B] uses alarmist language (‘...’) shaped by a clear vested interest as [role]. Admittedly, [Person A]’s evidence is not flawless, as [minor scope/cost gap]; conversely, [Person B] does raise one valid concern regarding [specific stakeholder pain point], even though their overall reasoning lacks rigour.
Paragraph 4
权衡判断总结
(~45 词 · 2 min)		 做加权总结而非“各打五十大板”和稀泥：说明为什么较强方的系统优势仍压倒其局部瑕疵。 In conclusion, while [Person B] highlights a legitimate practical concern and [Person A]’s data carries minor regional limits, [Person A]’s disciplined reasoning, neutral tone, and corroborated evidence make their argument substantially more persuasive overall.
💎 冲刺 15–16/16 分的灵魂秘诀：“强方微瑕 + 弱方微光”高阶辩证 (Dialectical Nuance)

绝大多数拿到 11–12 分（Level 3 顶端）的考生，最大瓶颈在于把较强的作者写成“完美圣人”、把较弱的作者写成“一无是处”。剑桥 0457 高分考官极其看重多维批判性平衡（Nuanced Evaluation）：

  • 给较强方挑 1 处真瑕疵：例如即使作者引用了权威机构大样本，也要检查——样本是否只覆盖高收入/大城市？是否遗漏了财政执行预算细节？数据年份是否略早？
  • 替较弱方认 1 处局部亮点：哪怕弱方通篇情绪化且存在商业利益偏见，也要客观承认——他提出的问题关切本身（如郊区小商户流失客源、学生失去基础写作训练）在现实中确实值得警惕，只是他的证据不足以支撑其极端结论。

一、全真演练 Topic A：城市中心区是否应禁止私家车通行？

建议用时：5 分钟精读标出 D1–D6 证据/逻辑锚点 + 15 分钟写出四段比较评估，随后打开官方维度拆解与 15/16 vs 6/16 红笔对照范文逐句精批。

Source 4 · Forum Debate: Should Private Cars Be Banned from City Centres?

Dr Maya Chen (Senior Urban Transport Researcher, MetroPolis Policy Institute)

“Restricting private petrol and diesel cars in congested city centres during peak hours is an evidence-backed necessity rather than an anti-driver ideology. A 2024 OECD comparative study tracking 18 metropolitan centres that introduced low-emission car-free zones found an average 27 per cent drop in roadside nitrogen dioxide within 24 months and a 19 per cent improvement in emergency ambulance response times. Crucially, business footfall rose by 8 per cent once former traffic lanes were converted into dedicated bus corridors and shaded pedestrian plazas. Critics reasonably warn that suburban shift workers and independent shopkeepers who rely on frequent deliveries could face real disruption if alternatives are absent. To mitigate this, municipal bans must exempt off-peak commercial freight, disabled permit holders, and residents living inside the zone, while channelling congestion revenues directly into affordable electric shuttle buses. Admittedly, most of the 18 cities studied already possessed dense underground rail networks—and full municipal transition costs are not yet published—so smaller, car-dependent towns would need phased public-transit investment before enforcing similar limits.”

Arthur Pendelton (Director, Regional Motor Retailers Association)

“Banning private vehicles from city centres is an outrageous war on ordinary working families that will inevitably turn vibrant high streets into economic ghost towns overnight. In an informal weekend poll conducted outside my three downtown vehicle showrooms, 88 per cent of the 45 drivers questioned stated they would immediately stop visiting city-centre shops if car restrictions were introduced. People who have never run a business always fail to realise that families carrying heavy groceries cannot simply balance bags on a rainy bicycle. Furthermore, technological progress is already solving vehicle pollution: manufacturers expect electric models to dominate new sales by 2035, making restrictive municipal bans completely pointless. While cleaner urban air is naturally desirable, punishing motorists simply forces loyal consumers to drive to out-of-town retail parks, guaranteeing the irreversible collapse of local independent retailers.”

Topic A · Q3 Simulation[16 marks]
Which argument is more convincing, Dr Maya Chen’s or Arthur Pendelton’s? Your answer should consider both arguments and you should use examples from the source.
请先用六维矩阵审计两人材料，再展开对比范文检查自己是否写出了“交叉交锋 + 辩证让步”。
展开 Topic A 六维快速审计表 (6-Dimension Pre-Writing Plan)
维度 Dr Maya Chen (较强方) Arthur Pendelton (较弱方)
D1 证据 具名独立报告（2024 OECD 18 城市追踪），含可核验具体量化指标（NO₂ 降 27%、救护车提速 19%、客流增 8%）。 仅凭自办周末展示厅外 45 名车主的非正式问询（88%），缺乏独立核验与商户真实账目数据。
D2 样本 跨 18 城两年期数据；微小局限：多数样本城市已有发达地铁，且未披露市政补贴总预算。 严重自选偏倚（selection bias）：只问刚在汽车展厅停车的 45 名司机，绝不代表全城公交/步行消费者。
D3 平衡 明确承认郊区轮班工人与独立小店送货困境，提出非高峰货运/残障豁免及收入反哺公交的缓释措施。 单边叙事；局部亮点：确实点出了外围居民采购大件物品与无轨交衔接区小商户的真实担忧。
D4 偏见 城市交通智库学者，无直接卖车商业利害冲突。 汽车零售协会会长（典型 vested interest），禁车直接打击其会员新车销量与门店客源。
D5 逻辑 清晰因果链；有条件限定（先建替代公交再限行）。 滑坡谬误（ghost towns overnight / irreversible collapse）+ 把未来电动化预测当成已解决现状（且忽视刹车粉尘与拥堵问题）。
D6 语调 克制、客观、学术化限定词（evidence-backed, peak hours, mitigate）。 高度情绪渲染与稻草人攻击（outrageous war, people who have never run a business, completely pointless）。

✅ 高分示范：Level 4 范文（325 词 · 考官实批 15/16） Level 4 · 15/16 Marks

Overall, Dr Maya Chen’s argument for targeted peak-hour vehicle restrictions is substantially more convincing than Arthur Pendelton’s blanket opposition, primarily because Chen anchors her claims in corroborated, multi-city research and balanced policy mitigations, whereas Pendelton relies on a biased convenience sample, vested interests, and alarmist rhetoric.

Firstly, in terms of evidence quality and logical rigour, Chen substantiates her case with a 2024 OECD comparative study across 18 metropolitan centres showing a 27% fall in nitrogen dioxide, 19% faster ambulance response times, and an 8% increase in pedestrian footfall. This builds a verifiable causal chain between restricting cars, improving urban mobility, and sustaining retail activity. By contrast, Pendelton bases his sweeping economic warning on an informal weekend poll of just 45 motorists parked outside his own showrooms (88%). Because this tiny sample automatically excludes bus users, cyclists, and local pedestrians, it suffers from severe selection bias. Furthermore, Pendelton commits a logical leap by treating an uncertain future prediction about 2035 electric vehicle sales as proof that congestion and urban air restrictions are “completely pointless” today.

Secondly, comparing balance, vested interest, and tone, Chen maintains a measured, analytical register and explicitly concedes potential disruption to shift workers and independent shops before proposing freight exemptions and shuttle-bus funding. Conversely, Pendelton employs emotive hyperbole—labelling restrictions an “outrageous war” creating “ghost towns overnight”—while holding a clear vested interest as director of a motor retailers association whose members profit from car sales. Admittedly, Chen’s argument carries minor limitations, as her 18-city evidence largely reflects rail-rich metropolises and omits full transition budget figures. Equally, Pendelton raises one legitimate practical concern regarding suburban families hauling heavy goods without reliable transit alternatives.

In conclusion, despite Chen’s omission of exact municipal costings and Pendelton’s valid point on car-dependent shoppers, Chen’s independent longitudinal data, objective tone, and balanced concessions make her argument far more persuasive overall.

考官评分解析 (15/16)：① 第 1 句立刻锁定判断与对比支点；② 第 2、3 段全部采用“同维对撞”架构（证据样本对撞、因果逻辑对撞、平衡与利益冲突对撞）；③ 第 3 段末完美展示“强方微瑕（缺预算、偏大城市地铁样本）+ 弱方微光（郊区采购现实痛点）”，因而拿到 Level 4 高段 15/16（距 16 分满格仅差对 Chen 的 MetroPolis 智库自身潜在立场略作一句审慎检视）。
❌ 低分反例：Level 2 范文（182 词 · 考官红笔实批 6/16） Level 2 · 6/16 Marks

I think Dr Maya Chen’s argument is more convincing because air pollution is very dangerous for our health and cities have too much traffic. In the first source, Chen says banning cars reduces nitrogen dioxide by 27% and helps ambulances arrive 19% faster. She also says shop footfall increased by 8%, and she suggests exempting delivery vans and disabled drivers so businesses do not suffer. I completely agree with her because everyone should take the bus to save the environment.

On the other hand, Arthur Pendelton disagrees with banning cars. He says it is a war on families and will turn streets into ghost towns. He interviewed 45 drivers at his car showrooms and 88% said they would stop shopping downtown. He also argues that electric cars will solve pollution by 2035. However, his opinion is wrong because electric cars still take a long time to arrive and shops will actually be fine if people walk. Overall, Maya Chen gives better facts so she wins the debate.

• 错误 1（块状分头摘要）：第 1 段只讲 Chen，第 2 段只讲 Pendelton，中间仅用一句毫无分析功能的 On the other hand 隔开，两人的样本、利益冲突、语言风格从未放进同一维度比较！

Source 4 · Education Symposium: Should Secondary Schools Impose a Total Ban on Generative AI for Homework?

Sofia Alvarez (Founder & CEO, EduGuard AI Detection Systems)

“Allowing secondary school students to touch generative AI tools for homework is intellectual poison that is rapidly turning an entire generation into mindless plagiarists. In our company’s internal demonstration trial of 60 student essays last autumn, experienced teachers failed to identify AI-written paragraphs 70 per cent of the time without specialist software. Because take-home essays can no longer be trusted, school qualifications will become completely worthless within two years unless ministries impose an immediate, total ban backed by strict automated screening. Teachers warn that young adolescents who outsource brainstorming to chatbots stop developing independent critical-thinking habits—a genuine concern that cannot be ignored. However, anyone suggesting that teenagers can be taught to use AI ‘responsibly’ is simply living in a fantasy world; you either ban it completely or accept the death of genuine education.”

Prof. David Okafor (Director of Curriculum Research, National Education Council)

“While unguided copy-pasting from chatbots unquestionably threatens foundational drafting skills, a blanket homework ban is both practically unenforceable and educationally counterproductive. A 2025 longitudinal study led by the National Education Council tracking 2,400 students across 18 socioeconomically diverse secondary schools found that learners using structured AI-prompt checklists for essay revision improved their analytical organisation scores by 18 per cent—without any drop in subsequent unassisted, pen-and-paper exam performance. Notably, gains were strongest among English-as-an-Additional-Language and dyslexic students who used AI solely as a diagnostic tutor. Rather than banning technology students will inevitably encounter in university and workplaces, schools should pair home drafting with brief in-class oral check-ins and handwritten checkpoint paragraphs. Admittedly, our study also noted that rural schools lacking reliable home broadband saw negligible improvement unless school computer labs remained open after classes, indicating that digital-access funding must accompany any curriculum reform.”

展开 Topic B 红笔精批范文：Level 4 (15/16) vs Level 2 (6/16) 逐句对照
✅ 高分示范：Level 4 范文（328 词 · 考官实批 15/16） Level 4 · 15/16 Marks

Overall, Prof. David Okafor’s argument against a blanket AI homework ban is far more convincing than Sofia Alvarez’s call for total prohibition, because Okafor supports his balanced recommendations with large-scale, independent longitudinal data, whereas Alvarez exhibits a strong commercial vested interest and relies on hyperbolic logic.

First, comparing evidence credibility and sample scope, Okafor cites a 2025 National Education Council study tracking 2,400 students across 18 socioeconomically diverse schools, demonstrating an 18% improvement in analytical organisation without harming unassisted exam results. This directly tests and refutes the fear that AI assistance erodes core exam competence. In sharp contrast, Alvarez relies on her own company’s internal trial of merely 60 essays (70% non-detection rate) to extrapolate an extreme prediction that qualifications will become “completely worthless within two years.” Not only is this sample tiny and uncorroborated, but Alvarez also presents a false dichotomy—claiming schools must either ban AI entirely or “accept the death of genuine education”—ignoring practical verification methods such as Okafor’s oral check-ins.

Second, evaluating bias, balance, and tone, Alvarez undermines her objectivity through emotive insults (“intellectual poison”, “mindless plagiarists”, “fantasy world”) and an acute conflict of interest: as CEO of an AI-detection firm, her business profits directly if schools mandate automated screening. Conversely, Okafor uses a measured register and explicitly acknowledges both the threat of unguided copy-pasting and equity benefits for dyslexic and EAL learners. Admittedly, Okafor’s proposal has a practical limitation—his own study notes negligible gains in rural schools lacking home broadband unless extra lab funding is secured. Nevertheless, while Alvarez validly highlights the genuine pedagogical risk of adolescents outsourcing critical drafting habits, this legitimate concern does not justify her extreme, unsubstantiated conclusions.

In conclusion, although Okafor’s approach depends on addressing rural digital inequality and Alvarez raises a real warning about cognitive shortcuts, Okafor’s independent large-scale evidence, neutral tone, and balanced safeguards make his argument decisively stronger.

3.4 Q3 比较连接词、证据评价词与让步对比句框卡 (16 张)

16 Essential Q3 Comparative Connectors, Evidence Evaluation Terms & Dialectical Concession Frames
显示 16 / 16 题
衔接 01开篇总判
Substantially more convincing ... primarily because ...
开篇第 1 句亮明谁总体更有说服力并直接锁定两大比较支柱
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Overall, [A]’s argument is substantially more convincing than [B]’s, primarily because it integrates corroborated data and balanced trade-offs, whereas [B] relies on emotive assertions.
衔接 02硬对撞连接词
Whereas / By contrast / Conversely / In sharp contrast
防止“先写完 A 再写 B”的四大强对比枢纽词
点击卡片揭晓答案
句内对撞用 Whereas（置于句中不断句）；跨句强转用 By contrast, / Conversely, / In sharp contrast,；确保每段至少出现 2 次！
衔接 03同域对比引出
Both writers address [topic], yet [A] ... while [B] ...
如何从“共同点”顺滑切入“论证质量差距”？
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：While both writers acknowledge the urgency of [issue], [A] substantiates this concern with longitudinal statistics, whereas [B] reduces it to a single personal anecdote.
衔接 04相对强弱修饰
Decisively stronger / Empirically superior / Comparatively weak
替代苍白的 “better / worse” 的考场高阶程度副词
点击卡片揭晓答案
高分替换词：empirically superior（实证更优）、analytically rigorous（分析更严密）、comparatively uncorroborated（相对缺乏佐证）。
证据 05权威与核验
Corroborated / Verifiable / Peer-reviewed / Longitudinal
精准描述较强方“硬核数据”的四大黄金定语
点击卡片揭晓答案
Corroborated（多方印证）、Verifiable（可核查来源与年份）、Peer-reviewed（同行评议）、Longitudinal（多年追踪，优于单次快照）。
证据 06弱证据定性
Anecdotal evidence / Uncorroborated assertion / Self-reported
一针见血给“个人故事/小道消息/自吹数字”定性
点击卡片揭晓答案
Anecdotal evidence（个案轶事，代表性极低）；Uncorroborated assertion（未经证实的断言）；Self-reported company trial（企业自测，独立性存疑）。
证据 07样本偏倚识别
Selection bias & Unrepresentative convenience sample
如何精准批评“只在自家店门口问 45 名司机”的调查漏洞？
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Polling only [45 motorists at his showroom] introduces severe selection bias, producing an unrepresentative convenience sample that excludes non-driving residents.
证据 08范围跨度质疑
Limited generalisability across different socio-economic contexts
挑出较强方“大城市/特定人群研究”的微小推广局限
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Although rigorous, the study’s generalisability is slightly constrained because its sample focuses mainly on [rail-rich cities], omitting rural or low-infrastructure contexts.
谬误 09既得利益动机
Vested interest & Conflict of interest
如何礼貌而犀利地拆穿“行业协会/软件老板”的立场偏见？
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：As [role], [B] holds a clear vested interest because [policy outcome] directly protects or expands their commercial revenue, casting doubt on the neutrality of their claims.
谬误 10滑坡与二分法
Slippery slope & False dichotomy (Either-or fallacy)
拆解“一旦限制就彻底毁灭 / 要么全禁要么教育死亡”的逻辑谬误
点击卡片揭晓答案
Slippery slope（把局部调控夸大为不可避免的全面崩溃）；False dichotomy（强行将多维折中选项压扁为极端二选一）。
谬误 11偷换预测为事实
Conflating uncertain prediction with guaranteed future outcome
批评作者把“2035 年技术预测”当成“现在无需行动的铁证”
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：[B] commits a logical leap by treating an unverified future projection ([2035 EV adoption]) as a guaranteed remedy for immediate present-day harms.
谬误 12语调对撞词汇
Measured, analytical register vs Alarmist, emotive hyperbole
高分对比双方语言客观度与情绪操弄的成对术语
点击卡片揭晓答案
较强方用 measured, qualified, analytical register；较弱方用 alarmist hyperbole / emotive rhetoric（如 “outrageous war”, “poison”）以情绪替代证据。
辩证 13强方微瑕让步
Admittedly, [Stronger]’s argument is not flawless, as ...
点出较强方样本、预算或前提上的 1 处真实短板以拿满 Level 4
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Admittedly, [A]’s case carries minor limitations: it omits [exact municipal costs] and relies heavily on data from [high-income cities], narrowing its immediate applicability elsewhere.
辩证 14弱方微光肯定
While [Weaker] validly identifies ..., this single point cannot outweigh ...
肯定较弱方的 1 处真实关切，但随即压回主判断
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：While [B] raises a legitimate practical concern regarding [stakeholder hardship], this isolated merit cannot outweigh their lack of corroborating data and overt bias.
辩证 15主动反驳加分
Pre-empts counter-arguments and proposes targeted mitigation
如何表扬较强方在论证中体现出“双面平衡度（Balance）”？
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：[A] enhances credibility by pre-empting counter-arguments—explicitly acknowledging [disruption to shift workers] and proposing targeted exemptions rather than ignoring trade-offs.
辩证 16加权终判句
On balance, [A]’s systemic rigour outweighs ...
杜绝“各有千秋”和稀泥的 Q3 结尾终局裁决句框
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：On balance, despite [A]’s minor cost omission and [B]’s valid warning on [X], [A]’s corroborated evidence, neutral register, and balanced concessions render their argument decisively more convincing.
