2.1 Q2A (8 分) 来源可信度双向深度评估模型 RAVEN 与统计漏洞

Component 1 Q2(a): Balanced RAVEN Evaluation (Strengths vs Limitations) & Five Statistical Flaws
C1 Q2A · 8 分核心题型 杜绝一边倒踩坑 · 必写正反双向平衡

一、为什么 70% 考生在 Q2A 只拿到 3–4/8 分？（Level 2 vs Level 4 的分水岭）

Component 1 Q2(a) 常见提问方式为：“How well does the evidence in Source 3 support the argument that...?” 或 “Evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of the evidence used by [Author] in Source 3.” (8 marks)。考生最常见的致命失误是陷入“非黑即白的极端一边倒”——一看到企业报告或博主帖子就通篇批评“它有偏见、毫无参考价值”；或者一看到大学教授研究就盲目夸赞“100% 绝对可靠”。

⚠️ 考官评分铁律：单边评价永远锁死在 Level 2 (3–4/8 分)

Cambridge 0457 评分标准明确要求：要进入 Level 4 (7–8 分)，必须做到“对同一来源进行双向平衡且充分展开的评估（balanced, developed evaluation）”——即清晰论证“一方面它为何具备一定的权威或参考价值（Strengths）”，同时剖析“另一方面它受限于何种利益偏见、抽样漏洞或方法局限（Limitations）”，并且每一条评价都必须扣紧原文具体细节（如具体年份、样本量、作者头衔、赞助背景或用词）。

二、RAVEN 五维精细拆解与考场双向套用公式

维度字母 英文全称与审查焦点 正面参考价值写法 (Strength) 反面局限漏洞写法 (Limitation)
R Reputation
声誉与出版平台公信力		 源自同行评审学术期刊、联合国 / WHO、国家统计局或知名主流调查报道，经过严格事实核查。 无署名个人博客、自媒体贴、未经过同行评审的企业内部白皮书，缺乏独立纠错与把关机制。
A Ability to perceive
感知能力、一手经历与观察视野		 作者处于现场一线（如本地骑行快递员谈主干道拥堵痛点、公立医院急诊室连续三年真实临床档案）。 二手转述传闻（“a friend told me”）、仅在单一地点观察却妄断全国形势，视野存在严重盲区。
V Vested interest & Bias
既得利益动机与文本偏见		 研究由独立公共科研基金赞助并公开声明无商业利益冲突，文本语气克制、不回避不利数字。 赞助方/作者在政策通过后直接增收或减损利润（如饮料厂反对包装税），并用情绪化词汇掩盖负外部性。
E Expertise
专业资质与领域对口度		 公共卫生教授评估低排放区对儿童哮喘影响；交通规划工程师评估道路拓宽与通勤效率。 跨界外行权威谬误（如网红博主或演艺名人空谈复杂财政与工程成本，缺乏专业方法训练）。
N Neutrality & Currency
客观中立度、时效性与交叉印证		 使用近 3–5 年最新纵向追踪数据，主动承认自身局限性、披露样本误差或引用多方交叉证据。 使用 8–10 年前过时数据论证高速变化的行业；或全篇绝对断言（always / completely eliminate），无反方让步。
📐 Q2A (8 分) 双向平衡黄金结构公式
Paragraph 1 (Strength): On the one hand, [Source] has merit because [R/A/E/N 原文细节] → making it useful for [局部/特定层面]...
Paragraph 2 (Limitation): On the other hand, its reliability is significantly undermined by [Vested interest / 统计漏洞原文细节] → so the overall claim cannot be accepted without independent corroboration.
注意：即使面对极高可信度的大学研究，第 2 段依然能从样本地域限制（如仅测温带城市）、未隔离家庭变量、外部效度不足切出精准批判！反之，面对极差的企业公关帖，第 1 段也必须公允承认其行业内部一线数据或局部成本顾虑！

三、Q2A 必考：样本与统计五大常见致命漏洞

在评价 Source 3 所引用的调查或数据时，仅靠“有偏见”远远不够。高分考生能精准叫出以下 5 大统计学与抽样方法漏洞的英文专业名称并结合原文点破：

漏洞序号与名称 考场特征识别（原文常见破绽） Level 4 英文批判高分句型
1. 样本太小
Small Sample Size		 “interviewed 14 cyclists” / “surveyed 20 VIP shoppers” 后却用来支撑全市乃至全国政策结论。 “A sample of only [14] individuals is far too small to support sweeping nationwide conclusions and risks random anomalies skewing the results.”
2. 样本偏差
Sampling / Selection Bias		 在地铁站问“大家支持公共交通吗”；在品牌专柜调查“VIP会员”；线上粉丝群投票。 “Surveying solely [urban VIP shoppers / cycling followers] creates severe selection bias because it excludes rural, low-income, or non-user perspectives.”
3. 相关性非因果
Correlation vs Causation		 “After policy X started, Y dropped 18%” 却未设对照组，忽视同期季节、经济或配套改革影响。 “The source confuses correlation with causation by ignoring confounding variables such as [seasonal shifts / concurrent funding] and lacking a control group.”
4. 数据过时
Outdated Data (Lack of Currency)		 在 2025/2026 年的试卷中引用 2014–2017 年的数据来论述电商、AI、包装回收或手机习惯。 “Citing figures from [2016] undermines currency, as consumer behaviour and recycling infrastructure have shifted substantially over the past decade.”
5. 只问意愿 / 自报偏差
Hypothetical Intent & Self-Reporting Bias		 问受访者 “Would you be willing to buy eco-friendly packs?”（口头表态愿意，现实中却因涨价而不买）。 “Measuring stated hypothetical willingness rather than real purchasing data creates social desirability bias, as respondents overstate virtuous behaviour.”

2.2 Q2B (8 分) “How could you test this claim?” 四要素满分答题模板

Component 1 Q2(b): Designing Rigorous Mixed-Method Empirical Research to Test a Claim
C1 Q2B · 8 分科研设计题 四要素闭环 · 告别空洞“做个问卷上网查”

一、为什么“I would do a questionnaire and search the internet”只能得 2–3 分？

Q2(b) 典型设问是：“Explain how you could test the claim that [e.g. switching to paper straws reduces customer complaints / banning cars cuts commute times].” (8 marks)。如果只泛泛而谈“我会发问卷调查周围同学，并在网上查政府数据”，缺少研究方法多样性、具象化样本规模与分层规则、可量化指标与对照逻辑、以及对研究局限的反思控制，阅卷考官只能判在 Level 1–2（2–4 分）。要稳定拿下 7–8/8 分，必须逐层填满下方四要素闭环框架：

二、Q2B 四要素考场逐段填词模板

得分要素 考场必写核心内容清单 高分英文示范短语 (直接套用)
要素 ①
具体多种混合方法		 同时组合 3 种互补手段：一手定量（匿名问卷或实地对照试验）+ 一手定性（半结构化深度访谈）+ 二手客观档案数据（政府统计、医院急诊或零售交易流水），拒绝单一网上搜索。 “To achieve triangulation, I would combine a quantitative anonymous survey, semi-structured interviews, and official secondary records (e.g. municipal waste audits / sales logs over 12 months).”
要素 ②
对象、样本量与分层		 明确谁是受访群体，写清合理具体样本量（如 400–600 份问卷 + 20 场访谈），并说明采用分层随机抽样（Stratified random sampling）覆盖城市/郊区/乡村、不同年龄与收入群体；涉及政策效果时设立实验组 vs 对照组。 “I would recruit a stratified sample of 500 residents across urban, suburban, and rural districts covering varied income brackets, alongside a control group of non-participating neighbourhoods.”
要素 ③
指标、对比与证伪判据		 写清问卷里具体测什么问题（用 1–5 分制李克特量表或具体客观行为记录，避免只问“是否愿意”），并通过前后对比（Before-and-After）或组间对比建立明确检验逻辑：什么结果支持断言、什么结果推翻断言。 “Using a 1–5 Likert scale and objective before-and-after measurements (e.g. actual weekly plastic disposal weight), if the intervention group shows no measurable reduction—or rising costs—the absolute claim would be refuted.”
要素 ④
局限性与误差控制		 预判研究本身的局限（如自报偏差 self-reporting bias、受试者因知道被观察而改变行为的 Hawthorne effect、时间成本高导致纵向跟踪困难），并交代匿名制、知情同意、客观记录交叉验证等对策。 “Admittedly, surveys risk self-reporting bias and longitudinal tracking is costly; making responses anonymous, gaining informed consent, and cross-checking against objective municipal data would mitigate these limitations.”

Moreover, a 2016 industry briefing noted that aluminium is infinitely recyclable. Governments should therefore abandon proposed corporate packaging taxes and allow voluntary brand initiatives like ours to lead the transition.

Source 3B (个人社交帖子): Viral Social Media Thread by @CityCyclist_Leo (42,000 Followers · Full-Time Urban Bike Courier, 2025)

Banning all private cars from central city avenues and replacing them with enclosed cycle highways will immediately cut every commuter’s travel time in half at zero financial cost! Over the past three weeks delivering packages downtown, I timed my own bike rides and spoke with 14 fellow full-time couriers in our rider chat group—every single one agreed that lazy drivers in metal boxes are poisoning our lungs and blocking the streets. City hall needs to stop pandering to car owners and shut down central car lanes tomorrow!

Source 3C (大学近 3 年跟踪研究): Adapted from Dr. A. Mensah et al. (2024), Department of Urban Public Health, University of Leeds (Published in Environmental Health Perspectives)

Introducing 500-metre Low-Emission Zones (LEZs) around secondary schools alongside free electric school buses can significantly improve adolescent respiratory health. Our three-year longitudinal study (2021–2024) tracked 4,200 students aged 12–16 across 18 state schools—comparing 9 intervention schools with 9 demographically matched control schools—using verified hospital emergency admissions and roadside PM2.5 sensors. Intervention schools recorded a 22% decline in asthma-related emergency visits. Funded by an independent National Medical Research Grant with no commercial conflicts declared, the authors nonetheless caution that findings apply primarily to humid, densely populated cities and could not fully isolate indoor household air pollution.

三、3 套完整 Q2A (8 分) & Q2B (8 分) Level 4 满分示范 vs Level 2 低分红笔对比

展开下方 3 套评分标准，重点观察 蓝色高亮（核心评估证据 / 四要素科研设计要点）、绿色高亮（双向平衡与学术因果衔接词） 与 红色波浪线（Level 2 低分常见硬伤） 的差别：

实战套题 1 · 企业公关报告 (Source 3A) [Q2A 8 marks + Q2B 8 marks]
Q2(a): How well does the evidence in Source 3A support FreshPack’s argument against corporate packaging taxes? (8 marks)
Q2(b): Explain how you could test the claim: “Switching to thin-walled aluminium cans will completely eliminate coastal plastic pollution within two years.” (8 marks)
【套题 1 靶点】企业利益冲突报告：既要挖掘其行业立场与真实痛点，又要精准打击小样本、只问意愿、2016 过时数据及绝对词 “completely eliminate”。
展开套题 1 · Q2A (8 分) Level 4 满分 vs Level 2 低分对照与拆解
Q2(a) Level 4 满分示范 · 双向平衡评估 Level 4 · 8 / 8 Marks

On the one hand, Source 3A offers limited partial support by demonstrating that FreshPack has direct insider operational expertise in beverage packaging (Ability & Expertise) and is actively investing in patented aluminium alternatives. Furthermore, referencing a quantitative survey percentage (89%) and the well-known recyclability of aluminium highlights potential consumer openness to alternative materials if prices stay competitive.

On the other hand, the evidence is severely undermined by strong commercial vested interest: as a beverage corporation lobbying against packaging taxes, FreshPack stands to lose substantial profits if taxed, giving it a clear motive to exaggerate voluntary schemes. Methodologically, the sample of just 80 VIP loyalty-card shoppers across two city-centre supermarkets suffers from small sample size and severe selection bias, excluding rural, budget-conscious, and non-loyalty shoppers. Crucially, asking whether customers would “gladly choose” cans measures hypothetical intent rather than real purchasing data (social desirability bias), while citing a 2016 briefing lacks currency. It also ignores that beverage bottles represent only one fraction of coastal plastic (fishing nets and industrial pellets remain untouched), making the claim to “completely eliminate” plastic pollution unsupported.

Q2(a) Level 2 低分对照 · 单边绝对否定踩坑样本 Level 2 · 3 / 8 Marks

FreshPack’s evidence is completely useless and totally unreliable because companies always lie to make money. They only asked 80 people in supermarkets and used old 2016 data, so nothing in Source 3A can be trusted at all. Also, aluminium mining causes pollution too (引入原文未展开的泛泛常识，未分析原数据缺陷).

【得分拆解】Level 4 先用 1 段肯定企业行业实操视角与消费者价格弹性线索，再用第 2 段精准叠打“商业免税利益 + 80 名城市 VIP 抽样偏差 + 只问意愿 + 2016 过时 + 忽略渔网等其他海滩塑料源”，稳拿 8/8；Level 2 因极端一边倒全盘否定且未拆解统计机制，锁死在 3/8。

展开套题 1 · Q2B (8 分) Level 4 四要素满分科研设计方案
Q2(b) Level 4 满分示范 · 检验“两年内彻底消除海滩塑料污染” Level 4 · 8 / 8 Marks

First, to test both material substitution and real coastal waste reduction, I would use mixed secondary and primary methods. I would collect independent longitudinal coastal waste-audit records over 24 months from environmental NGOs and municipal clean-up agencies across 12 diverse beaches (urban tourist hubs and remote fishing coasts), sorting debris by type (beverage bottles vs fishing gear, food wrappers, and microplastics). Second, I would conduct a stratified field trial across 20 supermarkets (10 adopting aluminium cans vs 10 matched control stores retaining plastic bottles), tracking actual weekly POS sales volumes alongside anonymous exit questionnaires with 600 shoppers spanning rural, suburban, and varied income brackets using a 1–5 scale on price sensitivity and disposal habits. This directly tests the absolute word “completely eliminate”: if coastal audits after two years still show high plastic volumes from non-beverage sources or low aluminium recycling capture rates, the claim is refuted. Admittedly, ocean currents wash in foreign debris, confounding local store trials; cross-referencing local municipal recycling tonnage and conducting 20 interviews with waste-plant managers would isolate domestic impact and mitigate bias.

【得分拆解】齐备 ① 混合方法（24个月海滩垃圾分类档案 + 超市对照实验 + 600人分层问卷 + 20场回收厂访谈）；② 明确对象与对照组；③ 真实 POS 销量与海滩塑料分类重量指标 + 针对 “completely eliminate” 设定明确推翻判据；④ 承认洋流输入跨界干扰并交代交叉对策。

【套题 2 靶点】社交媒体博主帖子：既要公平肯定一线骑手的日常路况洞察（Ability to Perceive），又要批判 14 人同行群样本偏差、情绪词以及忽视老年人/货运/基建成本。
展开套题 2 · Q2A (8 分) Level 4 满分 vs Level 2 低分对照与拆解
Q2(a) Level 4 满分示范 · 从草根帖中挖出合理一面并精准反驳 Level 4 · 8 / 8 Marks

On the one hand, @CityCyclist_Leo’s post carries genuine practical relevance through his direct first-hand Ability to Perceive: as a full-time urban bike courier navigating central streets daily, his three-week personal commute timing and consultation with fellow riders provide authentic grassroots insight into downtown gridlock and cyclist safety hazards. The post is also current (2025) and reflects real-time urban congestion conditions.

On the other hand, his evidence fails to substantiate universal policy claims due to severe methodological and neutrality weaknesses. First, the post lacks Expertise and Reputation: he is an unverified social media influencer rather than an urban transport economist or civil engineer, and his claims have undergone no peer review. Second, interviewing just 14 fellow couriers from a rider chat group represents a tiny, heavily biased sample (Selection bias & Vested interest)—all respondents already benefit professionally from empty car-free roads, while elderly commuters, disabled residents, bus passengers, and shop suppliers are entirely excluded. Third, loaded emotive phrases like “lazy drivers in metal boxes are poisoning our lungs” demonstrate blatant Bias, and claiming “zero financial cost” overlooks construction expenses for enclosed cycleways and lost municipal parking or retail revenue.

Q2(a) Level 2 低分对照 · 仅因“社交媒体”就两句话打死 Level 2 · 3 / 8 Marks

Social media is fake news so Source 3B has zero strengths. Leo only asked 14 friends in his chat group and called drivers lazy, which shows he is biased and nobody should listen to him. He is not a professor so his post cannot support the argument.

【得分拆解】Level 4 成功打破“社交帖子毫无价值”的懒惰模板，精准肯定了 2025 时效性与全职骑手的一线拥堵感知（Ability to Perceive），随后直指外行身份（Expertise）、14人同温层抽样偏差（Selection bias）、职业既得利益（Vested interest）和忽略基建及残障群体的漏洞。

展开套题 2 · Q2B (8 分) Level 4 四要素满分科研设计方案
Q2(b) Level 4 满分示范 · 检验“所有通勤者耗时减半且零财政成本” Level 4 · 8 / 8 Marks

To rigorously test this dual claim (travel time and cost), I would design a multi-method pilot study. First, for the “zero financial cost” claim, I would obtain secondary municipal engineering tenders and fiscal budgets from cities that previously converted car lanes (e.g. Paris or Bogota), alongside interviewing 10 civil engineers and 15 central retail owners to quantify construction costs, maintenance, and delivery-delay impacts. Second, during a 6-month trial on two comparable central corridors (one converted vs one unchanged control corridor), I would gather objective GPS traffic-sensor and public-transit timing logs before and after implementation, paired with a stratified anonymous questionnaire of 600 commuters explicitly segmented across cyclists, suburban car commuters, elderly residents, and disabled passengers, recording actual door-to-door commute minutes. This tests the sweeping claim “every commuter’s travel time in half”: if non-cyclists experience longer detours or municipal construction costs exceed zero, the absolute claim is disproved. As a limitation, short-term trials may suffer from temporary driver confusion; comparing month-1 vs month-6 sensor data and guaranteeing anonymous responses ensures accurate long-term trends.

【得分拆解】把双层断言拆开检验：用市政工程标书+商户访谈测“零成本”，用GPS客观耗时+控制走廊+600名多群体分层问卷（覆盖老年与郊区乘客）测“所有通勤者时间减半”，完美破解原断言漏洞！

实战套题 3 · 大学近 3 年跟踪研究 (Source 3C) [Q2A 8 marks + Q2B 8 marks]
Q2(a): Evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of Dr. Mensah et al.’s study (Source 3C) as evidence for expanding school Low-Emission Zones nationwide. (8 marks)
Q2(b): Explain how you could test whether implementing school Low-Emission Zones would achieve similar asthma reductions in your own town or region. (8 marks)
【套题 3 靶点】高信度学术研究：切勿强行胡编“教授收黑钱”，而应正面盛赞同行评审、独立基金、大样本对照组与客观医疗记录，再以地理外部效度（湿润高密度城市 vs 本土干燥/乡村）及家庭室内混杂变量切入局限！
展开套题 3 · Q2A (8 分) Level 4 满分 vs Level 2 低分对照与拆解
Q2(a) Level 4 满分示范 · 对高权威学术文献的高阶审辩 Level 4 · 8 / 8 Marks

On the one hand, Source 3C provides exceptionally strong and credible evidence across nearly every RAVEN criterion. It has high Reputation and Expertise as a 2024 peer-reviewed paper in Environmental Health Perspectives led by public health researchers at the University of Leeds. Its Neutrality and lack of Vested Interest are reinforced by independent National Medical Research Grant funding and a transparent conflict-of-interest statement. Methodologically, tracking 4,200 students across 18 demographically matched intervention and control schools over three years (2021–2024) using objective hospital admission logs and PM2.5 sensors avoids small-sample and self-reporting flaws, making the 22% asthma reduction highly reliable within that setting.

On the other hand, the study still has important limitations when generalised to nationwide policy. First, as the authors transparently acknowledge, geographical representativeness is restricted: results from humid, densely populated cities with viable electric bus networks may not transfer to arid, windy regions or rural districts lacking transit infrastructure (low external validity). Second, confounding household variables such as indoor dampness, parental smoking, or home heating fuels could not be fully isolated, meaning part of the asthma variance may stem from domestic rather than roadside air. Finally, limiting the sample to state-school adolescents aged 12–16 leaves impacts on younger primary children unverified.

Q2(a) Level 2 低分对照 · 生硬套背偏见模板或只夸不批 Level 2 · 3–4 / 8 Marks

Dr. Mensah’s study is extremely reliable because it comes from Leeds University, tracked 4,200 students for three years, and used hospital records, so every country should immediately follow it without question. However, maybe the professors were biased to get more research money next year (无证据乱扣利益帽).

【得分拆解】面对高可信学术材料，绝不要乱猜“教授为了骗经费而造假”！真正 A* 级批判在于检验外部效度（地理/气候/公交基建能否外推至全国）、未隔离的室内混杂因素与年龄段限制（仅测 12–16 岁）。

展开套题 3 · Q2B (8 分) Level 4 四要素本土化迁移检验方案
Q2(b) Level 4 满分示范 · 本土学校低排放区成效验证设计 Level 4 · 8 / 8 Marks

To test whether this model works in my own region, I would run a 24-month comparative mixed-method study across 6 local secondary schools—3 piloting 500m Low-Emission Zones with free electric shuttle buses and 3 socio-economically matched control schools across both urban and semi-rural areas (approx. 1,800 students aged 11–16). First, I would obtain anonymised secondary clinic and local hospital asthma-visit logs for 12 months before and 24 months during the pilot, paired with continuous roadside PM2.5 and NO2 air monitors at school gates. Second, to overcome Source 3C’s indoor-pollution gap, I would distribute a stratified anonymous parent-student questionnaire (1–5 symptom scale) explicitly recording confounding home variables (cooking fuels, indoor smoking, dampness), and conduct semi-structured interviews with 15 school nurses and local bus operators regarding compliance and drop-off bottlenecks. If intervention schools show significant respiratory improvement compared to matched control schools after controlling for household factors, the local effectiveness claim is supported; if drops mirror control schools, ambient regional weather dominates instead. Finally, strict ethical consent, data anonymisation, and seasonal adjustment address privacy concerns and seasonal flu spikes.

【得分拆解】直接回应 Source 3C 原文缺口！在本土实验设计中专门加入“家庭室内污染问卷变量排查”以及“实验校 vs 对照校前后 12/24 个月门诊数据对比”，同时兼顾伦理匿名与流感季混杂调整，展现顶级探究素养。

2.4 RAVEN 评估术语、统计样本缺陷与 Q2B 科研设计通用句框库 (16 张)

16 High-Yield Cards for RAVEN Evaluation, Statistical Flaws & Q2B Empirical Research Sentence Frames
显示 16 / 16 题
RAVEN 01R · Reputation
Reputation & Peer Review (来源声誉与审查)
如何判断一个来源的机构公信力高还是低？
点击卡片揭晓答案
高可信：同行评审期刊（peer-reviewed journal）、WHO / UN、大学研究机构、国家统计局。
低可信：无署名博客、社交帖子、无编辑把关的公司广告稿。
RAVEN 02A · Ability to Perceive
Ability to Perceive (一手亲历感知力)
为什么面对社交媒体个人帖子时，A 维度常常能写出最稳的 Strength？
点击卡片揭晓答案
即使博主没有学术背景，一线亲历者（如医生、快递骑手、当地教师）掌握真实一线观察与切身痛点（first-hand operational insight），比完全脱离现场的二手转述更有温度与线索价值。
RAVEN 03V · Vested Interest
Vested Interest vs Independent Funding
为什么拆解 Vested Interest 时必须查清赞助方（Funding）？
点击卡片揭晓答案
商业企业或游说团赞助的研究存在财务或政策游说动机（如反对包装税的饮料公司），往往只公布有利结果；相反，独立科研基金且公开利益声明的报告偏见风险大幅降低。
RAVEN 04E · Expertise
Domain Expertise vs False Authority
什么叫“跨界权威谬误”？如何在 Q2A 中优雅拆穿它？
点击卡片揭晓答案
某人在 A 领域有名（如体育明星或网红博主）并不代表其具备 B 领域（如气候模型或城市财政规划）的专业资质。英文写法：lacks relevant domain expertise in...
RAVEN 05N · Neutrality & Currency
Balanced Tone, Caveats & Currency (中立、局限声明与时效)
为什么“主动承认自己局限性的报告”在考官眼中反而更加可信？
点击卡片揭晓答案
客观学者的典型标志是语气克制并主动披露样本范围或混杂局限（acknowledges caveats），反观满口 completely / never / disaster 的文章往往以偏见取代实证。同时数据需尽可能处于近 3–5 年内。
缺陷 06漏洞 1 · 小样本
Small Sample Size (样本量过小)
为什么采访 14 位同行或调查 80 人通常无法支撑全国政策结论？
点击卡片揭晓答案
样本规模过小极易受偶然极端个案扭曲（vulnerable to anomalies），统计误差幅度大，无法可靠地外推到宏观城市或全国人口。
缺陷 07漏洞 2 · 抽样偏差
Selection / Sampling Bias (代表性偏差)
在市中心高端超市或骑手群里做问卷，为什么会引发严重的抽样偏误？
点击卡片揭晓答案
抽样地点或渠道天然过滤掉了农村、低收入、老年或其他利益相关群体（unrepresentative sample），导致得出的“89% 支持率”只是同温层回声。
缺陷 08漏洞 3 · 相关非因果
Correlation Confused with Causation
看到“政策落地后下个月垃圾少了 18%”时，为什么不能马上断定因果成立？
点击卡片揭晓答案
缺少匹配对照组（control group）时，变化可能来自天气、旅游淡旺季或同步出台的其他政策等混杂变量（confounding variables）。
缺陷 09漏洞 4 · 数据过时
Outdated Data / Lack of Currency
用 2016 年报告去论证 2025 年回收体系或数字经济政策，失分点在哪里？
点击卡片揭晓答案
近十年来技术成本、物流基础设施与公众消费习惯已发生剧变，引用过时近十年的旧数字缺乏时效性（currency），无法准确反映当下现实。
缺陷 10漏洞 5 · 只问意愿
Hypothetical Intent & Social Desirability Bias
问消费者“如果价钱差不多你愿意选环保包装吗”在统计学上有什么致命隐患？
点击卡片揭晓答案
态度与行为脱节！人们在问卷里常为了显得环保而勾选“愿意”（social desirability bias），但真实掏钱时却仍按习惯或便宜买单，必须用真实 POS 销售记录核查。
Q2B 11要素 ① · 混合方法
Mixed Methods & Triangulation (三角互证)
如何用最简洁的组合搭出 Q2B 高分科研方法矩阵？
点击卡片揭晓答案
1 定量 + 1 定性 + 1 二手客观档案：例如 stratified anonymous survey (定量) + semi-structured interviews (深挖原因) + official sales/clinic/transport logs (客观对照)。
Q2B 12要素 ②③ · 分层与指标
Stratified Sampling, Control Group & Operational Metrics
怎样写样本和测量指标才不会被批成“假大空”？
点击卡片揭晓答案
写实数字和群体：如 600 commuters across urban/suburban districts，设立未实施政策的 matched control group，并将指标具体化为 1–5 Likert scale / weekly sales / PM2.5 readings 及明确的“支持/推翻断言判据”。
Q2B 13要素 ④ · 局限与控制
Hawthorne Effect, Anonymity & Mitigation
Q2B 结尾反思局限性和误差控制时，最讨喜的两个专业术语是？
点击卡片揭晓答案
① Self-reporting bias（受访者虚报）→ 用匿名提交 + 客观记录核对控制；
② Hawthorne effect（知道被观察而刻意表现）→ 用延长试点周期与隐藏真实观测指标缓解。
句框 14Q2A 双向平衡框
Q2A Balanced Strength–Limitation Frame
考场遇到任何材料都可以直接套用的双向衔接骨架是？
点击卡片揭晓答案
On the one hand, [Source] holds partial merit due to [expertise / first-hand insight / recent data]... On the other hand, its reliability is significantly constrained by [vested interest / small biased sample / confounding factors], requiring independent corroboration.
句框 15Q2A 学术材料限制框
External Validity & Confounding Caveat Frame
遇到“大学近3年大样本完美论文”不知怎么写缺点时，直接套哪一句？
点击卡片揭晓答案
Despite strong methodological rigour and independent funding, the study’s external validity is limited to [specific urban/climate setting] and cannot fully isolate confounding household variables such as...
句框 16Q2B 证伪判据与收尾框
Q2B Testing Logic & Limitation Closing Frame
如何在 Q2B 最后两行字把 6 分答案稳稳拉升到 8/8 满分？
点击卡片揭晓答案
To test the absolute claim “[quote]”, if the intervention group shows no significant improvement over the matched control group, the claim would be refuted. Ensuring anonymity and cross-checking survey replies against official logs would further mitigate self-reporting bias.
