4.1 Q4 (20–24 分) 压轴大题“五维政策评估矩阵”与硬性评分门槛

Component 1 Q4: The 5-Dimension Policy Evaluation Matrix & Mandatory Evaluation of Rejected Alternatives
20–24 Marks · 建议用时 22–25 min 只夸首选方案必丢大分 · 必须逐一反驳两项落选方案

一、Q4 评分真相：为什么“只夸自己选的方案”最高只能拿一半分数？

在 Cambridge IGCSE Global Perspectives (0457 · 2025–2027) Component 1 笔试中，Question 4（20–24 分）是整张试卷分值最高的压轴决策论文，单题就占笔试总分的 三分之一左右。命题格式通常给出 3 个互相竞争的政策或行动方案（Options 1 / 2 / 3），设问为：

“Which course of action would you recommend? Explain your choice by considering the different options and using evidence from the sources.”

阅卷真相：题干中的 “considering the different options” 不是客套话，而是划分 Level 2 与 Level 4 的硬性门槛！很多考生花 90% 篇幅歌颂自己选中的方案，只在结尾加一句 “Options 1 and 2 are worse”，这种写法在官方评分标准中会被直接锁定在 Level 2（7–12 分），当场白白扔掉 10–12 分！

评分档位 (Level) 分值区间 (以 24 分为例) 考官核心判别标准 (Mark Scheme Descriptors)
Level 4 19–24 分 站在多维权衡立场（时间、成本、可行性、公平、副作用）对首选方案与两个落选方案展开全面对比评估；精准引用 Source 实证与多利益相关方得失；能主动承认首选方案的局部局限并提出缓释措施，形成有条件支撑的结论。
Level 3 13–18 分 较好地论证了首选方案，并对其他选项进行了部分比较或驳斥，但往往漏掉其中 1 个落选方案、未充分使用材料数据，或把首选方案写得毫无代价与短板。
Level 2 7–12 分 主要围绕“为什么我选这个方案”做单边陈述，对另外两个选项仅做一两句笼统贴标签式的否定（如 “too expensive / won’t work”），缺乏深入权衡分析。
Level 1 1–6 分 脱离材料空谈口号、机械抄写方案简介，或仅给出未加论证的个人偏好断言。

二、Q4 压轴 20–24 分“五维政策评估矩阵” (5-Dimension Decision Matrix)

拿到三选一题目后，先花 3 分钟在草稿纸上画一张五维矩阵，把 3 个方案放在同一张矩阵里横向打分。只要五维全部踩实，你的内容覆盖面就能天然达到 Level 4：

评估维度 核心审计问题 (Key Evaluation Questions) 考场高分切入视角
D1 · 短期应急 vs 长期治本
Short-term vs Long-term Effectiveness		 政策见效有多快？是治标止血，还是能根治结构性病因并持续多年？ 对比见效时间差 (time lag)：例如重罚/禁令短期立竿见影但易反弹；基建/教育改造见效慢（2–5 年），但能长效改变行为与制度结构。
D2 · 财政成本与现实可行性
Cost & Practical Feasibility		 初期投入多大？政府/学校财政能否负担？现有基层执法与基建跟得上吗？ 区分初期资本支出 (upfront capital cost)、长期运维费用与执法可行性 (enforceability)；绝不空喊“cheap/expensive”，要指出资金挤占效应 (opportunity cost)。
D3 · 多元利益相关方冲突与公平性
Stakeholders & Equity		 谁受益、谁受损？政策是否误伤弱势群体，还是加剧城乡/贫富鸿沟？ 至少拆解 3 类主体冲突：低收入家庭 (low-income households)、中小企业/商户 (SMEs)、农村 vs 城市 (rural vs urban divide)，警惕“累退性惩罚 (regressive impact)”。
D4 · 直接正效应与潜在负面副作用
Direct Benefits vs Unintended Consequences		 覆盖规模多大？是否会诱发意想不到的反效果、逃避行为或二次危害？ 剖析非预期后果 (unintended consequences)：如强硬禁令催生地下黑市、一刀切罚款导致逃避检测、机械增派任务引发教师/基层倦怠。
D5 · 显性比较并反驳另外两项落选方案
Evaluating Rejected Alternatives		 另外两个方案有什么表面优点？为什么它们在权衡下仍不及首选方案？ 拿 Level 4 必备硬条件！对两项落选方案必须分别写透“先承认其 1 点局部合理性 → 再拆穿其致命硬伤 → 显性对比说明首选方案为何仍更优”。

4.2 Q4 四段 22 分钟考场高分作文结构与条件化结论艺术

22-Minute Four-Paragraph Essay Blueprint, Dual-Rejection Rebuttal & Conditionalised Conclusions
4 段结构 · 22 分钟完卷 先认合理性再拆硬伤 · 宏观条件收尾

一、Q4 四段 22 分钟考场高分作文结构与时间分配

在考场 85 分钟全卷节奏中，Q4 推荐总用时 22–25 分钟（含 3 分钟列五维提纲 + 17 分钟撰写四段约 350–400 词 + 2 分钟核查材料引用与比较词）。请严格按下列四段逻辑落笔：

段落与耗时 必须写进的高分动作 考场直接复用英文衔接骨架
Paragraph 1
开篇立场定调
(~50 词 · 3 min)		 直截了当选出唯一首选方案，点明你用来裁决 3 个选项的核心标准（如 long-term sustainability, social equity, and practical feasibility）。不写废话背景。 To address [crisis] effectively, I recommend Option [3] because it achieves the strongest balance between long-term structural impact, equity for vulnerable groups, and practical enforceability compared with Options [1] and [2].
Paragraph 2
首选方案深挖
三大收益 + 一条代价/缓释
(~130 词 · 6 min)		 用 Source 里的具体数字/案例阐明首选方案的 3 大核心收益（短长效、多群体公平受益、系统协同）；随后主动承认 1 条潜在代价或时间滞后，并提出可操作的 缓解措施 (Mitigation)。 Option [3] delivers three decisive advantages. First, ... [source stat]. Second, ... [equity benefit for low-income/rural groups]. Third, ... [long-term systemic change]. Admittedly, this option carries [high upfront cost / a 2–3 year rollout lag]; however, this drawback can be mitigated by phasing implementation in high-need districts first and partnering with local NGOs.
Paragraph 3
双方案逐一退却反驳段
(~140 词 · 6 min)		 逐一处理两个落选方案：先用 Admittedly / While 承认其 1 点局部合理性（如短期省钱或见效快），再迅速用 However / By contrast 拆穿其致命硬伤（公平倒挂、黑市副作用、杯水车薪），并显性对比为何仍逊于首选方案。 While Option [1] appears attractive in the short term due to [low upfront spending / rapid deterrence], it is fundamentally flawed because it disproportionately penalises [low-income / rural households] and triggers [unintended black-market workaround]. Similarly, although Option [2] offers [wide initial reach], it fails to resolve [structural root cause] and overburdens [frontline staff], leaving it far less sustainable than Option [3].
Paragraph 4
宏观条件化结论
(~50 词 · 2 min)		 超越简单重复立场！写出“宏观条件化结论 (Conditionalised Conclusion)”：点明首选方案若配合何种前提条件（预算保障、社区沟通、或吸收落选方案中低成本的宣传元素），效能将实现最大化。 In conclusion, Option [3] represents the most sustainable and equitable investment, provided that municipal authorities ring-fence multi-year funding and integrate Option [2]’s low-cost public awareness element to bridge the initial rollout period.
🔍 Level 2 空话 vs Level 4 精准论证对照表（立刻戒掉这 4 句“水词”！）
维度 ❌ Level 2 低分空话（毫无材料依据） ✅ Level 4 满分高阶表达（结合维度 + 材料 + 权衡）
谈成本 “Option 3 is expensive, so it is bad.” “Although Option 3 demands substantial upfront capital, its preventive focus reduces recurring emergency healthcare/cleanup expenditures over a ten-year horizon.”
谈公平 “Option 1 is unfair to people.” “Option 1 imposes a regressive burden on multi-generational low-income households who lack capital to upgrade inefficient appliances.”
驳落选 “Option 2 will not work at all.” “While Option 2 raises surface awareness quickly at minimal cost, it places unrealistic clinical pressure on untrained schoolteachers without expanding specialist treatment.”
写结论 “Therefore, Option 3 is the best choice.” “Provided that pilot funding targets high-risk wards first and transparent auditing tracks outcomes, Option 3 outperforms both short-term and superficial alternatives.”

4.3 全真决策模拟：2 套三选一政策命题 + Level 4 (22/24) 范文 + 6 题策略诊所

Full Practice: Two 3-Option Policy Decisions (Youth Mental Health & Heatwaves/Water) + 22/24 Models + 6 MCQs
Topic A 青年心理健康 · Topic B 极端热浪与城市缺水 22/24 红笔批注示范 + 6 道交互单选

一、三选一全真模拟 Topic A：应对全球青年心理健康危机国家方案

建议用时：3 分钟填写五维权衡矩阵 + 19 分钟完成 4 段完整论证，随后展开 22/24 红笔批注范文与得分拆解。

Source Booklet Summary · Policy Briefing: Addressing the Youth Mental Health Crisis

Context & Evidence According to a 2025 Ministry of Health survey, diagnoses of anxiety and depression among 13–18-year-olds have risen by 41% since 2019, yet rural clinics face waiting lists exceeding 7 months. Teenagers report three overlapping pressures: intense exam competition, household financial insecurity, and cyberbullying on social platforms. A national parliamentary committee is evaluating three proposals:

  • Option 1 · Nationwide Under-18 Smartphone & Social Media Ban: Enforce mandatory age-verification locks prohibiting social media accounts and school smartphones for all citizens under 18. Supporters note minimal direct government spending and an immediate cut in late-night screen fatigue. Critics warn that teenagers bypass filters via VPNs or older siblings’ devices, enforcement drains police/school resources, and isolated rural or marginalised youth lose vital online peer-support networks without addressing exam or poverty stress.
  • Option 2 · Mandatory Teacher Mental-Health First Aid & National Awareness Campaign: Train existing secondary teachers through a two-day online workshop to spot early distress and run assemblies across all schools within 6 months. Supporters highlight rapid national rollout (costing only USD 12 million) and quick reduction in stigma. Critics report severe teacher burnout (64% of surveyed staff feel already overloaded) and warn that identifying distressed students without hiring clinical specialists merely lengthens clinic waiting lists.
  • Option 3 · Fund School-Based Counsellors & Free Community Youth Wellness Hubs: Invest USD 180 million over three years to place accredited counsellors in secondary schools—prioritising high-need urban and rural districts—paired with free drop-in community wellness centres and digital-literacy workshops. Supporters cite a pilot in Northern Province that cut severe crisis referrals by 31% and boosted school attendance among low-income students. Critics point to the high upfront fiscal cost and a 2–3 year training lag before all rural posts can be filled.
Topic A · Q4 Simulation[24 marks]
Which course of action would you recommend to the government? Explain your choice by considering the different options and using evidence from the sources.
【审题导引】三个选项各有利弊——没有“完美零成本”选项！推荐 Option 3，但必须写透其成本与 2–3 年培养周期如何缓释，同时分别肯定 Option 1/2 的局部优势并精准驳倒。
展开 Topic A 五维决策权衡提纲矩阵 (3-Minute Pre-Writing Plan)
评估维度 Option 1 (全国封杀社媒) Option 2 (教师短期培训+宣讲) ✅ Option 3 (专业驻校心理师+社区中心)
短期 vs 长期 短期看似减少熬夜刷屏，长期无法化解学业/家庭焦虑源。 6 个月内快速落地去污名，但无法治本、不能替代专业临床治疗。 长期治本：试点使危机转介降 31%；存在 2–3 年培训滞后期。
成本与可行 财政表面支出低，但技术绕过（VPN/借号）导致执法几乎失灵。 极省钱（USD 12M）且覆盖快，但受限于教师专业深度不足。 投入高（USD 180M / 3 年），需分阶段优先覆盖高需求贫困与农村校。
公平与利益方 伤害依赖线上互助的偏远地区或边缘青年（equity 受损）。 加重已超负荷教师（64% 过劳）压力，对重度患者无实质救治。 极度公平：免费面向低收入家庭，重点缓解农村 7 个月候诊困境。
副作用 催生地下规避行为与代际冲突。 只“筛查出问题”却无医生接诊，导致医院排队进一步恶化。 需防范跨年度预算被削减及初期人力空窗期（可结合轻量数字科普过渡）。

✅ 高分示范：Q4 Level 4 范文（392 词 · 考官实批 22/24） Level 4 · 22/24 Marks

To address the 41% surge in youth anxiety and depression sustainably, I recommend Option 3—funding school-based counsellors and free community wellness hubs—because it provides the strongest combination of long-term clinical effectiveness, equity for disadvantaged youth, and genuine root-cause prevention compared with Options 1 and 2.

Option 3 offers three decisive advantages. First, it delivers proven long-term effectiveness by targeting underlying stressors rather than surface symptoms: the Northern Province pilot reduced severe crisis referrals by 31%. Second, it directly enhances social equity by providing free professional care inside schools and drop-in centres, eliminating financial barriers for low-income families and cutting the 7-month rural clinic waiting list. Third, embedding digital-literacy workshops equips adolescents to navigate online hazards independently without isolating them from peers. Admittedly, Option 3 requires a substantial USD 180 million investment and faces a 2–3 year training lag before rural vacancies are filled. However, this limitation can be mitigated by phasing recruitment into highest-need rural and low-income districts first while partnering with accredited NGO tele-counsellors during the transition.

Conversely, both alternative options suffer from critical structural weaknesses. While Option 1 involves minimal direct spending and may curb late-night screen fatigue quickly, it is practically unenforceable because teenagers routinely bypass age locks via VPNs or older siblings’ devices, draining school enforcement capacity. Worse still, a blanket ban produces harmful unintended consequences by severing isolated rural and marginalised youth from supportive online communities while leaving exam and poverty pressures untouched. Similarly, although Option 2 achieves rapid national reach at just USD 12 million and reduces stigma, it is clinically superficial and inequitable toward staff: with 64% of teachers already overworked, a two-day online course risks burnout and, by identifying distressed pupils without expanding specialist capacity, perversely lengthens hospital queues.

In conclusion, Option 3 clearly outperforms Options 1 and 2 as a durable, equitable solution, provided that the government ring-fences multi-year funding for rural recruitment and incorporates Option 2’s low-cost stigma-reduction assemblies as a supporting short-term bridge.

考官评分拆解 (22/24 · 稳居 Level 4 高段)：
• 为何稳拿 22 分：① 首选 3 收益全扣材料数字（41% 激增、Northern Province 降 31%、7 个月农村候诊）；② 坦承 USD 180M 成本与 2–3 年滞后并给出高需求区优先+NGO 远程心理过渡缓释；③ 逐一完成对 Option 1（承认省钱减屏疲劳 → 拆 VPN 绕过+伤害边缘群体）与 Option 2（承认 USD 12M 快铺开去污名 → 拆 64% 教师过劳+反向拉长候诊名单）的退却反驳；④ 结尾用 provided that 吸收 Option 2 低成本宣讲实现宏观条件协同！
• 最后 2 分冲刺 24/24 提示：若能补充一句评估 Option 3 面临的“社区中心可能被非高危学生挤占、需设分级分诊机制（triage system）”以及 Northern Province 试点规模是否代表全境的微调提醒，即可达 24/24 完美境界。

二、三选一全真模拟 Topic B：缓解极端热浪与城市缺水市政决策

注意对比科技基建方案（海水淡化+补贴空调）与自然生态基建方案（树荫廊道+透水路面+公租房雨水回用）之间在长期财务、气候副作用与弱势群体公平性上的深度权衡。

Source Booklet Summary · Municipal Crisis Report: Extreme Heatwaves & Urban Water Scarcity

Context & Evidence Summer temperatures in Coastal City X now exceed 41 °C for three weeks annually, while reservoir levels dropped to 28% capacity last August. Heat-related hospitalisations rose sharply among elderly residents in dense, concrete low-income apartments. Small street vendors and local workshops also struggle with rising utility costs. City councillors are reviewing three courses of action:

  • Option 1 · Steep Progressive Water Tariffs & Heavy Over-Use Fines: Double water tariffs above basic household quotas and fine residential or commercial over-consumption heavily within 3 months. Supporters note negligible municipal spending and cite a trial in Southern District that cut affluent suburban lawn watering by 14%. Critics warn that multi-generational low-income families sharing one water meter face punishing bills (regressive impact), risking illegal meter tampering, while water pricing does nothing to cool lethal street temperatures.
  • Option 2 · Construct a Coastal Desalination Plant & Subsidise Domestic Air-Conditioners: Spend USD 420 million to build an industrial desalination plant (operational in 5 years) alongside immediate rebates on household AC units. Supporters stress rainfall-independent freshwater security and instant indoor relief for AC buyers. Critics highlight massive capital debt, high energy demand supplied largely by coal-fired power (increasing emissions and pushing AC waste heat back onto streets), and ongoing electricity bills that poorest renters still cannot afford.
  • Option 3 · Urban Tree-Canopy Corridors, Cool Pavements & Public-Housing Greywater Retrofits: Invest USD 140 million over three years to plant shade trees, lay permeable heat-reflective pavements in high-risk wards, and install subsidised rainwater/greywater recycling tanks in public apartments. Supporters cite municipal hydro-modelling showing a 2–4 °C drop in street heat and a 25–30% cut in potable water demand, creating local maintenance jobs and directly shielding low-income elderly tenants. Critics note that trees require 3–5 years to mature into full shade and demand disciplined irrigation maintenance during severe droughts.
Topic B · Q4 Simulation[24 marks]
Which course of action would you recommend to the city council? Explain your choice by considering the different options and using evidence from the sources.
【审题导引】拆解核心陷阱：Option 2 表面解决水与热，但有 5 年建成滞后 + USD 420M 巨债 + 燃煤发电与空调废热加重热岛副作用 + 穷人交不起电费！Option 3（USD 140M）水热双治且聚焦高危老旧公租房，虽有树荫成熟滞后但可用轻量临时降温站缓释。

评估维度 Option 1 (阶梯水价重罚) Option 2 (海水淡化+空调补贴) ✅ Option 3 (树荫廊道+透水路面+中水回用)

✅ 高分示范：Q4 Level 4 范文（395 词 · 考官实批 22/24） Level 4 · 22/24 Marks

Confronted with 41 °C heatwaves and reservoirs falling to 28% capacity, I recommend Option 3—urban tree-canopy corridors, cool pavements, and public-housing greywater retrofits—because it simultaneously mitigates both heat and water scarcity at a sustainable fiscal cost while protecting low-income elderly residents most effectively.

Option 3 delivers three substantial benefits. First, it provides integrated long-term efficacy, projected to cut street temperatures by 2–4 °C and reduce municipal potable water demand by 25–30%, tackling both crises concurrently. Second, at USD 140 million, it costs one-third of Option 2’s desalination plant while generating local maintenance jobs and keeping utility bills low for small street vendors. Third, targeting high-risk public apartments maximises social equity for heat-vulnerable elderly tenants who cannot afford private cooling. Admittedly, tree canopies require 3–5 years to mature into full shade and need consistent irrigation during droughts. Nevertheless, this delay can be mitigated by installing permeable cool pavements and greywater-fed drip irrigation in Year 1, paired with temporary public cooling centres during peak summer weeks.

In contrast, Options 1 and 2 fall short on equity and unintended consequences. Although Option 1 requires minimal municipal outlay and cut suburban garden watering by 14% within three months, it is deeply regressive and incomplete: multi-generational low-income households sharing a single meter face punitive bills, encouraging meter tampering while leaving outdoor 41 °C heat untouched. Similarly, while Option 2 guarantees rainfall-independent water supply and immediate indoor AC relief for buyers, its economic and environmental side effects undermine its value: demanding USD 420 million and a five-year build lag, it forces coal-fired electricity spikes that worsen urban heat-island exhaust and leaves low-income renters unable to pay ongoing AC running bills.

In conclusion, Option 3 offers the most resilient, climate-compatible path forward, provided that the council pairs it with Option 1’s targeted luxury-lawn surcharges—strictly exempting multi-occupant low-income meters—to help finance rapid Year-1 cooling infrastructure.

考官评分拆解 (22/24)：① 首尾一贯锁定 水热双治 + 弱势老年公平 + USD 140M 财力可控；② 精准捕捉到 Option 2 最隐蔽的恶化室外热岛效应副作用（空调外机废热 + 燃煤发电）与 5 年建成滞后；③ 用“中水回用滴灌新栽树苗 + 首年铺设透水凉爽路面 + 临时纳凉中心”巧妙化解树荫 3–5 年成长周期；④ 结尾将 Option 1 改造为“仅针对豪宅草坪的豁免式附加费”来反哺 Option 3，尽显顶层政策思维！

4.4 Q4 政策论证高阶动词、多利益方权衡与反驳句框卡 (16 张)

16 High-Yield Q4 Policy Evaluation Verbs, Equity Vocabulary & Alternative-Rebuttal Frames
显示 16 / 16 题
动词 01治本与根源
Address root causes / Tackle structural drivers rather than symptoms
表达首选方案“根治结构性病因，而非只治表面症状”
点击卡片揭晓答案
高频动词搭配：addresses underlying structural drivers / tackles the root causes rather than merely masking surface symptoms.
动词 02一举两得/协同
Concurrently mitigate / Deliver dual co-benefits / Reinforce
精准描述政策同时改善多个危机或产生协同正效应
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Option 3 concurrently mitigates both heat stress and water scarcity, delivering dual co-benefits at one-third of the capital cost.
动词 03挤占与恶化
Divert scarce funds / Exacerbate / Perversely worsen
指出落选方案挤占关键预算或“反向恶化”现有危机的高分动词
点击卡片揭晓答案
Divert scarce public funds from essential clinics（挤占稀缺预算）；exacerbate urban heat-island effects / perversely lengthen waiting lists（适得其反地加重问题）。
动词 04落地可行性
Prove unenforceable / Strain administrative capacity / Bypass
分析禁令或罚款“纸上谈兵、难以执法落地”的精准动词组
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：A blanket ban would prove practically unenforceable, as users routinely bypass restrictions and strain school administrative capacity.
公平 05累退伤害
Disproportionately penalise / Regressive financial impact
指出一刀切涨价或重罚对低收入群体的不公打击
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Steep tariffs exert a regressive financial impact that disproportionately penalises multi-generational low-income households sharing a single meter.
公平 06城乡与弱势倾斜
Bridge the urban-rural divide / Shield vulnerable cohorts
阐述首选方案如何兼顾农村、老年人或无产阶层租客公平
点击卡片揭晓答案
Bridge the urban-rural service divide（弥合城乡资源落差）；shield vulnerable cohorts（庇护高危老年或低收入租客群体）。
公平 07商业与民生平衡
Operational burden on SMEs vs Long-term utility savings
分析政策对小商户/街头摊贩与居民家庭之间利益冲突的表达
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：While fines impose an acute compliance burden on small street vendors, greywater retrofits generate sustained utility savings for households and micro-businesses alike.
公平 08资本与回报权衡
Upfront capital expenditure vs Recurring preventive savings
破解“首选方案初期太贵”批评的核心财政反辩术语
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Although requiring high upfront capital expenditure, preventive hubs offset costs through recurring savings in emergency hospitalisation and crisis care.
反驳 09短平快方案退却
While Option [X] offers immediate low-cost appeal, ...
先肯定“省钱快效”，再转向“治标不治本/副作用”的句框
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：While Option [1] offers immediate low-cost appeal by cutting [surface metric], it remains fundamentally flawed because it ignores [root cause] and induces [unintended harm].
反驳 10浅层宣讲/培训反驳
Although Option [Y] achieves rapid reach, it proves clinically superficial
拆穿“全民宣讲 / 短期急救培训”看似覆盖广实则无力解决重度危机的句框
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Although Option [2] achieves rapid national reach and lowers stigma, it proves clinically superficial and overburdens [64% exhausted staff] without adding treatment capacity.
反驳 11非预期反弹警示
Unintended consequences / Perverse incentive / Counterproductive
高阶概括政策引爆地下黑市、篡改水表或热岛排热的三大词汇
点击卡片揭晓答案
Unintended consequences（非预期后果）、perverse incentive（逆向激励，如私改水表）、counterproductive（事与愿违/适得其反）。
反驳 12显性优于对比
Leaves it far less sustainable / Decisively outperformed by Option [Z]
每个落选方案驳完之后，必须挂回首选方案做比较的收束钩子
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Taken together, this high fiscal debt and 5-year delay leave Option [2] decisively outperformed by Option [3] on both cost-effectiveness and equity.
条件 13承认成本/时间短板
Admittedly, this policy faces a [2–3 year rollout lag / upfront cost]
在 Paragraph 2 末尾主动点破自己首选方案的一处局限
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Admittedly, Option [3] is not free of drawbacks: it requires substantial upfront capital and faces a 2–3 year implementation lag before rural posts mature.
条件 14可操作缓释句框
This limitation can be mitigated through phased deployment and ...
紧接上句给出“分期试点 + 过渡协同”的务实缓释方案
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：However, this limitation can be effectively mitigated by phasing deployment into high-need districts first and pairing interim [NGO tele-counselling / cooling hubs].
条件 15条件化收尾连词
Provided that / Contingent upon / Subject to ring-fenced funding
替代苍白 “So it is the best” 的 Level 4 条件化收尾枢纽词
点击卡片揭晓答案
Provided that（只要……）、Contingent upon（取决于……）、Ring-fenced multi-year funding（专款专用的跨年度财政保障）。
条件 16双方案轻量融合
Integrate Option [X]’s low-cost element as a supporting bridge
如何在结论段吸纳落选方案中的 1 点良性元素实现效能最大化？
点击卡片揭晓答案
句框：Option [3] achieves peak impact provided that authorities lock in multi-year maintenance budgets and integrate Option [2]’s low-cost awareness element as a short-term bridge.
