0. 使用指南：这份模板怎么用才真正提分

0.1 三条总原则（阅卷老师反复强调）

  1. 这不是背诵考试，是应用考试。定义只值第一步的分；把定义套到题目给的具体细节上、解释“为什么”，才拿后面的分。
  2. 每一分都对应一个动作：Point → Evidence → Explanation →（需要时）Judgement。只有观点没有证据、只有证据（引用）没有解释，都是半分答案。
  3. 先读题、再读源。带着问题读 Source，边读边划：数字/事实划一条线，观点句划波浪线，谬误触发词（always / all / experts say / 情绪词）圈起来。

0.2 考场时间分配（85 分钟 / 50 分，按分值 ≈ 每分 1.5 分钟）

0.3 套模板的正确姿势

  • 模板里的 [红色方括号] 必须全部换成本题 Source 里的具体内容，留着空话等于没答。
  • 每个段落只做一件事：一个谬误、一个评价标准、一个设计步骤、一个论证点。段与段之间用连接词分开阅卷老师才好给分。
  • 字数跟分值走：2 分题 2–3 句，4 分题一小段，8 分题 3–4 段。写满≠写对，跑题的长文反而扣组织印象。
  • 引用要短：一句里截 3–8 个词就够，引用后立刻跟一句解释 “This shows … because …”。

1. 指令词速查：写错类型直接丢分

指令词阅卷要什么不要做
Identify从原文准确摘出，抄准即可改写、加解释、自己编数字
Explain先定义，再说明本例为什么符合只给定义不联系例子
Summarise用自己的话：1 句总观点 + ≥3 个细节大段抄原文
Analyse拆开结构 + 解释如何成立、为何重要只复述内容
Evaluate强弱两面 + 有理由的最终判断只夸或只骂；两边平均不表态
Design / Suggest设计你自己的研究方案并 justify跑去评价题目给的旧研究（那是 Q2a）

2. 核心概念速查（Q1 地基）

概念一句话定义（英文，可背）识别信号
Fact 事实A statement that can be verified / proved true or false.数据、日期、可查证事件
Opinion 观点A personal view that cannot be directly proved.I think / in my view
Value judgement 价值判断A statement judging something as good/bad, fair/unfair, important/unimportant.should, must, fair, unfair, important
Prediction 预测A statement about what may happen in the future; it has not happened yet.will, going to, likely to
Objective 客观Presented impartially, without personal feelings influencing it.多面呈现、少情绪词
Subjective 主观Influenced by personal beliefs, feelings or standpoint.强烈情绪词、单一立场
Perspective 视角The standpoint from which someone views an issue, shaped by their role, experience, interests, values and information.问“为什么他这样看”而不只是“他说了什么”
Vested interest 既得利益A personal / financial interest that may make someone benefit from a particular outcome.资助方、雇主、身份利益
Perspective 为什么不同？万能原因库（挑 2–3 个套）：身份/角色不同 · 亲身经历不同 · 利益不同 · 价值观不同 · 文化背景不同 · 掌握的信息不同 · 所处层级不同（个人/地方/国家/全球）。

3. 逻辑谬误万能模板（Q1 高频小题）

铁律只写出谬误名称 = 最多一半分。必须完成四步：① 命名 → ② 引用原句 → ③ 指出触发点 → ④ 解释推理断在哪里 / 需要什么证据才成立。

The statement “[短引用]” is an example of [fallacy name]. This is a fallacy because [触发点：e.g. it uses the absolute word “always” / it attacks the person, not the argument], so it assumes [被偷偷假设的东西] without evidence. To be convincing, the writer would need [什么样的证据].
谬误触发点解释句（套这一句）
Overgeneralisation 过度概括always / all / never / everyone“It generalises from [one case / limited evidence] to all cases, but exceptions may exist.”
Ad hominem 人身攻击攻击说话者本人“It attacks the person rather than their reasoning, so the claim itself is never actually disproved.”
Circular argument 循环论证结论 = 前提换说法“The conclusion simply restates the reason, so nothing is actually proved.”
Appeal to authority 诉诸权威“experts say…” 无证据“It relies on who said it rather than evidence, and the ‘expert’ may not be an expert in this field / may have a vested interest.”
Appeal to emotion 诉诸情感恐惧/同情/愤怒词“It tries to make the reader feel [emotion] instead of giving evidence, so feeling convinced is not the same as the reasoning being valid.”
False dichotomy 虚假二选一either…or… 只有两选项“It presents only two options as if there were no alternatives, but [第三种可能] is also possible.”

✅ 范例（Exam Guide hunger 例）

“The statement ‘Hunger always destroys health’ is an overgeneralisation. The word ‘always’ assumes hunger harms health in every case without exception, but the source gives no evidence covering all situations — for example, short-term hunger may not cause lasting harm. Hunger is usually harmful, but ‘usually’ does not prove ‘always’.”

拆解：命名 ✓ 引用 ✓ 触发词 always ✓ 反例 ✓ 结论与证据的差距 ✓。

❌ 差答案

“This is an overgeneralisation because it is exaggerated.” —— 没引用、没说断在哪，阅卷只能给名称分。

4. Q1 分析来源与观点：逐小题模板

Q1 常见链条：(a) Identify → (b) Explain 术语/谬误 → (c) Summarise a perspective → (d) 最重要因素短文。分值以试卷为准，下面按典型分值给长度。

Q1(a) Identify 类（1–2 分）

直接抄：[原文中的确切短语/数字，不改写]
  • 问 “identify a fact / an opinion” 就抄那一句原句，不要解释（解释是下一题的事）。
  • 抄之前核对：fact 必须可验证；opinion 里通常有判断词。

Q1(b) Explain 概念/谬误类（2–4 分）

[Term] means [英文定义一句]. In the source, “[短引用]” is an example because [它具体符合定义的哪一点].
范例（value judgement）：“A value judgement is a statement that judges something as good, bad, fair or important. ‘We must not ignore their struggle’ is a value judgement because the word ‘must’ shows what the writer believes people ought to do — it expresses a moral judgement, not a verifiable fact.”

Q1(c) Summarise a Perspective（4–6 分）

结构：总观点 1 句 → 3 个细节（每个配短引用）→ 态度收尾 1 句。用自己的话，引用只作证据。

Overall, [人物/组织] believes that [一句话总立场]. Firstly, they argue [细节1], saying “[引用1]”. Secondly, they point out [细节2] (“[引用2]”). Finally, they suggest [细节3 / 主张的行动], for example “[引用3]”. Their tone is [urgent / hopeful / critical…] because [语言证据].

✅ 范例（慈善机构视角，hunger 例）

“Overall, the charity believes hunger is getting worse but can be solved if people act together. It describes people who ‘must not be ignored’, showing it sees hunger as an urgent moral problem. It calls on readers to ‘join us’ and to ‘support our food programmes’, so its solution is collective action and donations. Its tone is urgent but hopeful: the problem is serious, yet presented as solvable.”

常见丢分：只列观点不给证据 · 把某人的 opinion 写成 fact · 全段抄原文没有自己的总括句 · 只写“他说了什么”不写“他为什么这样看”（有余力补一句原因：身份/经历/利益）。

Q1(d) “最重要因素 / 原因”短文（6–10 分，Q1 拉分题）

阅卷逻辑：只选一个 + 给 ≥3 个理由 + 每个理由有证据或解释 + 说明为什么比其他因素更重要。罗列所有因素不选 = 拿不到判断分。

第 1 句（选择）：The most important factor is [只选一个].

理由 1（范围）：This affects [多少人 / 多大范围], because [证据].

理由 2（机制）：It leads directly to [问题], since [因果解释].

理由 3（连锁）：It also causes further problems such as [连锁后果 1] and [连锁后果 2], which makes it more serious than [另一个因素], because [对比理由].

结尾：Therefore, tackling [该因素] would [最直接的改善], which is why it should be prioritised.

“为什么更重要”的比较标准库（挑 2–3 个）：影响人数最多 · 后果最严重 · 持续时间最久 · 会引发其他问题（root cause）· 最直接 · 最难逆转。

✅ 范例骨架（poverty & unemployment，hunger 例）

“The most important cause is poverty and unemployment. Without income, families cannot buy food even when food is available, so this factor affects everyday survival for large numbers of people. It is also a root cause: poverty leads to poor healthcare and low education, which then make hunger harder to escape. Other causes matter, but if people had stable incomes, many of those effects would be reduced. Therefore poverty should be tackled first.”

常见丢分：不做选择把因素全列一遍 · 只写 “this is important” 不说为什么 · 排名没有比较句 · 引用了不解释引用与问题的关系 · 写自己知道的话题知识而不是用 Source。

2025 真题 Q1 Protest 例题（Sources 1–2，逐小题对 Mark Scheme）

1(a) Identify one method of non-violent, peaceful protest [1 分]

题型 = 纯摘抄，答一个就够，多答不错但浪费时间。Mark Scheme 接受的答案只有 Source 1 列出的这 6 个：

protest marches / campaigns / non-cooperation and disobedience / strikes / occupation of buildings / publicity stunts —— 任选其一，原词抄写。
陷阱：不要答 Source 1 没列的自创方法（如 social media posting）；不要加解释，1 分题解释不加分。

1(b)(i) Identify one example of a prediction from Source 2 [1 分]

Mark Scheme 只认 Source 2 里的这两句（任选一句，原句抄准）：

① “Globalisation means that these issues will affect all countries.”
② “Protest movements will grow.”

1(b)(ii) Explain why it is a prediction [2 分] —— 2 分模板

2 分 = 定义 1 分 + 贴合该句 1 分。只写定义、和 (i) 的句子对不上，只得 1 分（Mark Scheme 原话：shows understanding but relationship to the example is not clear）。

A prediction is a statement about what might happen in the future — something that has not happened yet. [所选句] is a prediction because [will affect / will grow 指的是将来会发生的事], not something that has already happened or can be verified now.
✅ 满分示范（选句②）：“A prediction is a statement about what might happen in the future. ‘Protest movements will grow’ is a prediction because ‘will grow’ describes something that has not happened yet — the writer is forecasting that protests will increase in the future.”

1(c) Describe the perspective of the writer in Source 2 [6 分] —— 五要素模板

Source 2 是一份全球社会正义行动组（action group working towards global social justice）的传单。Mark Scheme（Table A）按 Level 给分，并列出视角的 5 个要素——要素覆盖越全、原文材料用得越频，分越高：

Level分Mark Scheme 描述（关键词背下来）
35–6Clear analysis · describes a wide range of elements · frequent use of relevant material / examples from the source
23–4Some analysis · a range of elements · some use of source material
11–2Limited analysis · limited range · little or no use of source material

Issue 议题：The writer is concerned about [global issues / global social justice] and wants to “make the world a better place” / create a secure future.

Values 价值观：They believe it is important to care about these issues — “We cannot ignore these global issues” — ignoring them is presented as wrong.

Cause 原因：Because of globalisation, these issues “will affect all countries”, so no country can treat them as someone else’s problem.

Consequence 后果：If nothing is done, the future is insecure — “Time is running out”; the impact is global, not local.

Action 行动：People must “join together” and take action / protest — “Don’t ignore the situation.”

✅ Level 3 示范（五要素齐 + 频繁引原文）：“The writer, from an action group working towards global social justice, believes global issues must be tackled urgently and together. Their issue is the set of global problems listed, from poverty and pandemics to global warming, which they want addressed to ‘make the world a better place’ and secure the future. Their values are clear: ‘We cannot ignore these global issues if we want a secure future’, supported by the UN Sustainable Development Goals. The cause they identify is globalisation, which means ‘these issues will affect all countries’, so the consequences are global and time is short — ‘Time is running out.’ Their proposed action is collective: people should ‘join together’ and protest rather than ignore the situation — ‘Don’t ignore the situation.’”

自查：Issue ✓ Values ✓ Cause（globalisation）✓ Consequence ✓ Action ✓，引用 5 处 → 这就是 wide range + frequent material。

Level 1 写法（反面）：只写 “The writer thinks global issues are bad and people should protest.” —— 只有 1 个模糊要素、零引用，封顶 1–2 分。

1(d) Which global issue is the most significant? Explain why. [8 分]

先从 Source 2 的清单里只选一个议题（8 个任选，无标准答案）：unemployment & lack of economic development · pandemics & inadequate health care · poverty & social inequality · migration & refugees · war, armed conflict & violence · global warming & climate change · crime & political corruption · environmental damage & loss of biodiversity。

理由（英文可直接用）套法

✅ 示范骨架（选 global warming & climate change）：“The most significant issue is global warming and climate change. It affects the most people internationally, because no country is exempt — as Source 2 notes, globalisation means these issues ‘will affect all countries’. It also creates a vicious circle: warming damages farming and health, which deepens poverty, which leaves communities less able to adapt. It has multiple consequences for other aspects of life — food, health care, migration — for many different groups, and it is exceptionally difficult to solve because it requires coordinated global action. Other issues are serious, but climate change worsens several of them at once, which is why it should be prioritised.”

5. Q2a 评价已有研究：模板与例题

关键区别 Exam Guide 明确：这题不要最后判一句“这个研究总体好/不好”，重点是把 2–3 个优点 + 2–3 个缺点逐个讲透。每个点用同一个三步结构：

Point：One strength / weakness is its [评价标准]. Evidence：The research [具体做法，如 interviewed the manager of a local food project]. Explanation：This strengthens / weakens the findings because [对可信度/可推广性的具体影响].

10 个评价标准速查（背英文词 + 一句解释）

标准问自己什么解释句式
Credibility 可信度做研究的人懂行吗？一手来源吗？“…has first-hand experience, so the information is likely to be accurate.”
Reliability 可靠性重复做能得到类似结果吗？方法记录清楚吗？“…used the same questions for everyone, so the study could be repeated consistently.”
Validity 有效性测的是它声称要测的吗？结论跟得上证据吗？“…actually measures [X], so the conclusions follow from the evidence.”
Relevance 相关性直接回答研究问题吗？“…directly addresses the research question, rather than a related topic.”
Currency 时效性够新吗？这个话题变化快吗？“…is recent, which matters because [话题] changes quickly.”（注意：新≠自动更好）
Sample size 样本量人数配研究目的吗？定性深访可以小，但不能推广；定量调查需要足够大
Representativeness 代表性选的人能代表目标人群吗？“…only includes [一类人], so the results cannot be generalised to [更大人群].”
Bias / Vested interest 偏见资助方/被访者立场会怎样影响？必须写机制：“The funder may want [结果], which could influence [选题/提问/解读].”只说“有资助所以有偏见”不得分
Methodology 方法适配方法配 claim 吗？访谈适合挖观点，不适合测全国比例
Evidence → Conclusion结论有没有跑在证据前面？“The conclusion goes beyond the evidence because [证据只覆盖…].”

✅ Q2a 范例（访谈本地 food project 经理的研究，hunger 例）

Strength 1 — Credibility：“One strength is credibility. The researcher interviewed the manager of a local food project, who works with hungry families every day. This is primary evidence from someone with first-hand experience, so the details about local needs are likely to be accurate.”

Weakness 1 — Representativeness：“However, the sample is a weakness. Only one local project was studied, so the findings reflect one area and cannot be generalised to world hunger, where causes and conditions differ widely.”

Weakness 2 — Reliability（细节型，特别加分）：“Reliability is also limited because the interviewee said they could not hear some questions clearly. This means the notes may record answers to questions that were misunderstood, so repeating the interview might not produce the same results.”

注意每个点都落到了研究里的具体细节，而不是空喊标准名——这正是 Guide 列的第一类常见错误（“naming a criterion without applying it”）的反面。

常见丢分：只列强弱不解释影响 · 假设大样本自动可靠 / 小样本自动没用 · 混淆 reliability 与 validity · 假设新研究自动更好 · 把资助当成偏见的自动证据 · 同一个点换个说法重复两遍 · 编造 Source 里没有的假设。

6. Q2b 设计研究检验 Claim：模板与完整例题

先做这一步拆 claim 成可测量的东西。例如 “most people” = 超过 50%？“grow their own food” = 过去 12 个月种过？频率多少算数？定义不清，后面全白搭。

Exam Guide 要求：至少 3 种方法（如问卷 + 访谈 + 现有数据），每种都要解释为什么适合这个 claim；方案要详细到别人照着能做。

八步设计清单（按此顺序写，一步一段）

步写什么模板句
1 Aim一句话研究目的“The aim is to test whether [claim 改写成可测量形式] in [目标人群].”
2 Methods≥3 种，primary + secondary 结合“I would use a questionnaire, interviews, and existing official data so the results can be cross-checked (triangulation).”
3 Population谁和 claim 有关“The target population is [人群], because [与 claim 的关系].”
4 Sample人数范围 + 抽样方式“I would survey [数量范围] people selected by random / stratified sampling across [年龄/地区], to avoid a self-selected sample.”
5 Data & Questions收集什么、问什么（中立具体）见下方好/坏问题对照
6 Analysis先定判断标准再分析“The claim would be supported if more than 50% … and the official data also shows …”
7 Justification每步为什么适合本 claim每段末尾加一句 “This is appropriate because …”
8 Limitations困难 + 怎么减小“A limitation is [偏差]; to reduce this I would [措施].”

问题设计：好问题 vs 引导性问题

❌ Leading / biased✅ Neutral & specific
“Don't you think growing your own food is great?”“In the past 12 months, have you grown any food yourself? How often: weekly / monthly / never?”
“Do you agree most people love gardening?”“Which best describes you: I grow food regularly / occasionally / never?”

✅ Q2b 完整范例（Claim: “Most people grow their own food”）

“The aim is to test whether more than half of adults in [my city] grow food themselves regularly. First, I would use a questionnaire with a stratified random sample of about 200 adults across different age groups and districts, asking neutral questions such as how often they grew food in the past 12 months. A questionnaire is appropriate because the claim is about ‘most people’, so I need numbers from many people, not just opinions. Second, I would interview about 10 of the respondents in depth to understand why they do or do not grow food, which explains the patterns behind the numbers. Third, I would compare my results with existing government / gardening-association data; if my survey and the official data agree, the conclusion is more reliable (triangulation). I would not only share the questionnaire on my own social media, because that would reach mainly people like me — a self-selected sample. The claim would be supported only if over 50% report growing food regularly AND the secondary data shows a similar proportion. A limitation is self-report bias — people may exaggerate — which I would reduce by making the questionnaire anonymous and asking about a specific time period.”

检查：aim 可测量 ✓ 3 种方法 ✓ 样本人数+抽样 ✓ 中立问题 ✓ 分析标准（50%）✓ 三角验证 ✓ 局限+改进 ✓。

常见丢分：把设计题写成评价旧研究 · 方法不解释为什么合适 · 参与者和 claim 无关 · 人群/人数/抽样含糊 · 问题带引导 · 没写怎么分析 · 结论超出方案能证明的范围。

2025 真题 Q2b 例题：检验 “Marches are often reported on national news channels.” [8 分]

这句 claim 就出自 Source 3 的结论。先拆词再设计：marches = 哪段时间、哪些游行？often = 多频繁才算 often？reported = 画面出现？整段报道？national news channels = 哪几家全国频道？拆不清，阅卷会判你的设计和 claim 对不上。

Level分Mark Scheme（Table D）关键词
47–8Appropriate & fully justified design · wide range of methods and evidence · explanations credible & clearly related to testing the claim
35–6Appropriate & justified · a range of methods · mostly related
23–4Partly justified · mostly descriptive, little explanation
11–2Limited · methods described only, not credible / not related to the claim

方法库（Mark Scheme 原列 7 种，考试挑 3–4 种并逐个 justify）

方法套到本 claim 的写法
Interviews with experts 访谈专家访谈新闻编辑 / 媒体研究者——他们了解频道如何选题报道
Interviews with protestors 访谈抗议者访谈游行参与者——一手证言：自己的游行有没有被报道
Observation 实地观察观察多场不同抗议 / 行动组，记录现场是否有全国频道记者与摄像
Review of secondary sources 查二手文献已有研究、报道与文献提供背景与对照
Internet & media search 互联网与媒体搜索直接检索各全国频道在游行当天的新闻条目（本 claim 的核心证据来源）
Questionnaires / surveys 问卷调查大样本定量数据，如问公众是否在全国新闻里见过该游行
Case studies 案例研究深挖几场具体游行从发生到被报道的全过程

证据库（Mark Scheme 原列，写设计时点名你要收集哪几类）

新闻内容统计（statistics on protests in news content）· 个人证言 / 亲身经历 · 压力团体 / 慈善 / 政府 / 国际组织的材料 · 一手 + 二手数据 · 定量 + 定性数据 · 研究报告 · 专家证言。定量 + 定性、一手 + 二手都沾到，正是 wide range 的证据面。

先定判断标准（本题最容易漏、也最拉分的一步）

The claim would be supported if, over [时间窗，如 3 个月], at least [比例，如超过一半] of the protest marches I track are reported by at least [数量] national news channels within [时限，如游行后 24 小时内]. “Reported” means [定义：出现在该频道当天的新闻节目/官网新闻中，而不只是社交账号提及].

数字阈值是你自己定的操作性定义（operational definition），Mark Scheme 没有给固定数值——关键是提前写明、之后按它判，而不是看完数据再改口径。

三角验证（Triangulation）怎么写

I would cross-check three sources: (1) my own media search / content count of national channels, (2) interviews with protesters and a news editor, and (3) secondary data such as existing media research. If all three point the same way, the conclusion is more reliable; if they disagree, I would report the disagreement rather than claim the claim is simply true.
✅ Level 4 示范（浓缩版，考试可扩写）：“To test whether marches are often reported on national news channels, I would first define ‘often’ as more than half of marches being reported within 24 hours. Over three months I would track every protest march in [范围] and search the main national news channels’ bulletins and websites for reports of each one, recording quantitative data — how many marches, how many reported, by how many channels. This media search directly measures the claim. I would also interview protesters from several marches, who can give first-hand testimony about whether their march was covered, and interview a news editor as an expert on how stories are selected. Finally I would compare my figures with existing secondary research on protest coverage. The claim is supported only if my count passes the threshold AND the interviews and secondary data agree.”

自查：定义 often ✓ 方法 ≥3 种且每种都说了为什么 ✓ 定量+定性 ✓ 判断标准先行 ✓ 三角验证 ✓。

本题专属陷阱：只设计问卷问路人“你觉得游行常上新闻吗”——测的是印象不是事实，与 claim 对不上；只查一场游行就下结论——样本和 2a 的毛病一样；不定义 often，全篇无法判定真假。

7. Q3 分析与评价论证：模板与例题

典型结构：分析（约一半分）+ 评价与判断（约一半分）。最大忌讳是写成两段摘要（summary）——摘要不得评价分。

整体骨架（5 段）

  1. 开头判断句：先表态你更信哪一方，一句话给理由。
  2. 分析 A 方：≥3 个点——主张是什么、理由、证据、结构是否连贯。
  3. 分析 B 方：≥3 个点，同上。
  4. 对比评价：逐项比 reasoning / evidence / tone / bias，哪方更强、为什么。
  5. 结尾判断：必须与开头一致，给出 weigh-up（为什么强的那方“更”强）。

开头：Overall, [A / B]’s argument is more convincing because [一句话核心理由：e.g. its reasoning is consistent and its evidence is more relevant].

分析点：[作者] claims that [主张]. They support this with [理由] and the evidence that “[短引用]”. This is effective / weak because [逻辑或证据质量的解释].

评价点：In contrast, [另一方] … A weakness is [如：moves away from the main claim / relies on emotion], which weakens the argument because [影响].

结尾：Therefore, although [弱方的一个优点，concession], [强方] is stronger overall because [重申核心理由].

评价维度清单（每方至少碰 3 个）

维度看什么
Logic 逻辑理由能不能真正推出结论？有没有谬误、跑题、偷换概念？
Evidence 证据具体吗？相关吗？来源可信吗（机构/数据/亲历）？
Consistency 一致性前后是否一条主线，还是中途转到别的话题？
Tone 语气理性还是煽动？情绪化不自动=弱，但不能代替证据
Bias / Vested interest作者身份会不会影响立场（如家人职业、机构利益）？
Balance 平衡有没有回应反方，还是只讲自己一面？
老师提醒：最大声、最情绪化、最自信的论证不一定最强；有统计数字、有专家出场也不自动可靠——要看证据与结论到底连不连得上。

✅ Q3 范例（Grace vs Seth，hunger / farming 例）

开头判断：“Overall, Seth’s argument is more convincing than Grace’s, because it stays focused and links its evidence to practical solutions, whereas Grace’s argument loses its main line.”

分析 Grace：“Grace supports new technology in farming, giving relevant examples such as self-driving tractors, computers that monitor soil and weather, and seeds bred to survive difficult conditions. These examples make her claim concrete. However, in the second half she moves away from technology to criticising big food companies and rich countries, so her argument becomes less consistent.”

分析 Seth：“Seth consistently opposes industrial farming and proposes native crops with composting, which addresses both hunger and the environment. He supports this with evidence from the UN World Food Programme, a credible international source, and ends with actions individuals can take, such as eating less meat.”

评价 + 结尾：“Grace may also be influenced by her father, who is a farmer, which could shape her positive view of farm technology. Seth’s reasoning is more balanced and his conclusion follows from his evidence. Therefore, although Grace gives some strong technological examples, Seth’s argument is stronger overall.”

拆解：判断先行 ✓ 两方各 ≥3 点 ✓ 引用/具体证据 ✓ 一致性、来源可信度、vested interest 三个评价维度 ✓ 让步（concession）后结论与开头一致 ✓。

常见丢分：只 summarise 不 evaluate · 说“strong/weak”不给理由 · 只谈证据或只谈逻辑一头 · 把情绪化语言当成弱的自动证据 · 两边各打五十大板不做判断 · 开头说 A 强结尾说 B 强 · 引用堆太多、自己的话太少。

2025 真题 Q3 例题：Aput vs Panuk（Source 4）[16 分 = 分析 8 + 评价 8]

原题问法：“Which argument is more convincing, Aput’s or Panuk’s?” 并明确要求：consider both arguments · evaluate their reasoning, evidence and use of language · support your judgement with their words and ideas。漏掉任何一项，Level 就下去了。

✅ Level 4 形状示范（选 Aput 更有说服力；选 Panuk 也可满分，关键是支撑与一致）

开头判断：“Overall, Aput’s argument is more convincing than Panuk’s, because Aput supports his claims with historical examples and research data, while Panuk relies mainly on one personal anecdote.”

分析+评价 Aput：“Aput argues that large-scale change ‘needs a mass movement of people to persuade governments to change laws or invest more money’. His reasoning is clear and logical: individuals can change their own lifestyle, but only collective action changes laws. He supports this with historical evidence — Martin Luther King, Mahatma Gandhi and Greta Thunberg — and with research from 2021 showing protest movements rose from 70 in 2006 to 240 in 2020. His language is measured and respectful. However, the research is not fully referenced, so it cannot be checked, and claims such as powerful people ‘look after their own vested interests’ are value judgements that not everyone would accept.”

分析+评价 Panuk（显性对比）：“Panuk is more balanced in one respect: he concedes ‘You have a point, Aput’ before disagreeing, which Aput does not do. But his evidence is weaker. His main support is his father, a local politician who ‘reads all the letters he receives’ — this is a single anecdote, and his father has a vested interest because he ‘wants their votes’. Panuk gives few examples and no expert evidence, and his language becomes disrespectful when he calls protest ‘selfish’ and says it ‘undermines democratic processes’, which is asserted rather than explained. Unlike Aput, he offers no evidence about impact at a national scale.”

结尾（与开头一致）：“Therefore, although Panuk’s personal-responsibility ideas are practical and his argument is partly balanced, Aput’s argument is stronger overall because its reasoning is clearer and it is supported by a wider range of relevant evidence.”

自查对照 Level 4：both arguments ✓ wide range（logic、历史例、数据、语言、vested interest、balance 六个面）✓ frequent 原文引用 ✓ explicit comparison（“Unlike Aput…”）✓ reasoning 与 evidence 都评了 ✓ judgement 前后一致 ✓。

8. Q4 附录（保险）：行动推荐大作文 [20 分 = 8 + 8 + 4]

2025 真题题面

一个环保行动组想推动公众就全球变暖采取行动，三个备选行动三选一推荐：① Organise a peaceful protest march · ② Write to the government · ③ Promote the issue on social media。要求：state your recommendation · give reasons and evidence · use material in the sources and/or your own ideas · consider different arguments and perspectives。

评分结构（先懂分从哪来）

维度分Level 4（顶档）长什么样

推荐模板（五段）

① Recommendation：I recommend that the group should [三选一], because [一句话核心理由].

② 理由 1 + 证据：Firstly, [规模/速度/成本中的一点]. Evidence from the sources: [引 Sources 1–4 的材料]. This matters because [解释].

③ 理由 2 + 对比另外两个：Compared with [行动 B], my choice [优势], whereas B [局限]. [行动 C] could [它的一个优点，concession], but [为什么仍不如我的选择].

④ 不同视角：From the perspective of [政府/公众/受影响群体], [他们的顾虑或支持]; barriers such as [障碍/vested interests] can be reduced by [办法].

⑤ 结尾判断（与开头一致）：Therefore, [重申选择] is the most effective action because [重申核心理由].

9. 怎么读 Mark Scheme：阅卷规则 + 逐题自查清单

六条通用阅卷规则（Generic Marking Principles，读懂就不做无用功）

  1. Marks are awarded positively（正向给分）：只奖对的、有效的答案；不因错误扣分、不因漏写扣分（no marks deducted for errors or omissions）。含义：不会的题也写下能写的部分，空白才是真 0 分。
  2. Whole marks only：只有整数分，没有半分。含义：差一点到下一 Level，就是整档的差距，所以要把 Level 的关键词全凑齐。
  3. 超出大纲的好答案也给分：valid answers beyond the scope of the mark scheme 也可得 credit。含义：Mark Scheme 的 indicative content 是示例，不是唯一答案清单。
  4. 只甩 key term 不给分：社会科学阅卷原则明确——DO NOT credit answers simply for using a “key term”，必须看得出你真懂。含义：写了 credibility / validity 还要解释它在本题里发生了什么。
  5. 重复不重复给分：同一个点换个说法（mirror statement）不再给分。含义：Q2a 写 4 个“样本小”的变体 = 只有 1 个点；要换标准、换角度。
  6. 自相矛盾不给分：obviously self-contradicting、想把所有可能都覆盖的答案不给 credit。含义：Q3 判断必须一条线到底；Q1(d) 只选一个，别两边讨好。

Levels 怎么定（Levels of Response 通法）

阅卷流程是从最高 Level 往下对，找到和你答案匹配的那一档；档内再看：consistently meets → 档顶 · meets with slight inconsistency → 中上 · just enough on balance → 中下 · borderline → 档底。所以自查顺序也要从 Level 4 的描述往下核对，缺哪条补哪条，而不是数自己写了几个点。

11. 自测（先写再点开答案）

1. “All rich countries waste food, so nobody there cares about hunger.” 这是什么谬误？按四步写。

Overgeneralisation（+ 无证据的动机推断）。四步：引用 “All rich countries…” → 触发词 “All / nobody” → 从未给的证据推到所有国家和所有人 → 需要各国浪费数据与态度调查才成立。

2. 一项研究只采访了 1 位本地食物项目经理，却得出“全球饥饿正在恶化”的结论。写两个不同标准的弱点。

① Representativeness：一个本地项目不能代表全球，结论不能 generalise。② Evidence→Conclusion / Validity：访谈能测本地情况，测不了全球趋势，结论超出了证据范围。注意两者角度不同，不是换说法重复。

3. Claim: “Most teenagers prefer studying online.” 你的研究 aim 和判断标准怎么写？

Aim: “To test whether more than 50% of teenagers aged 13–18 in [范围] prefer online study to classroom study.” 判断标准：问卷中选择 online 的比例 >50% 且访谈/学校数据方向一致，才算支持；样本需跨学校/年龄分层随机，不能只问自己班。

4. Q3 里一方数据很多但结论和数据对不上，另一方数据少但推理严密。结尾判断怎么写才不算“各打五十大板”？

明确选一方并 weigh up：例如 “Although A provides more statistics, its conclusion does not follow from them, so the statistics add length rather than strength. B’s evidence is thinner but every reason connects to its claim, making B more convincing overall.”

5. 把这句改成中立问卷题：“Don't you agree school meals should be free for everyone?”

“To what extent do you agree that school meals should be free for all students? Strongly agree / Agree / Disagree / Strongly disagree” 或 “Should school meals be free for all students? Yes / No / Not sure — please give a reason.”

2025 真题组（protest 卷）

6. 1(b) Source 2 里哪两句是 prediction？任选一句写出 2 分的 (ii) 解释。

两句： “Globalisation means that these issues will affect all countries.” 与 “Protest movements will grow.” 解释满分 = 定义（about what might happen in the future / has not happened yet）+ 指回该句的 will。“Protest movements will grow” is a prediction because “will grow” describes a future increase that has not happened yet.

7. 1(c) 只写了 issue 和 action 两个要素、引了 1 处原文，大概是哪个 Level？怎么补到 Level 3？

Level 1–2（limited / some range）。补：values（cannot ignore）、cause（globalisation）、consequence（affects all countries / time is running out），并把引用加到 4–5 处、每处贴着一个要素写。

8. 2(a) “这项研究发了 100 份问卷，所以样本大、很可靠”——这句为什么拿不到弱点分？正确写法是什么？

因为它只看了发放量，没看实际回收：只有约 20 份寄回，achieved sample 小且回复者可能与未回复者系统性不同 → biased / less accurate。优点那边也只能说 potentially large。发放量 ≠ 样本量。

9. 2(b) 检验 “Marches are often reported on national news channels.” 时，只问卷调查公众“你常在新闻里看到游行吗”够不够？为什么？

不够。那测的是公众的印象（perception），不是报道的事实。可以作辅助的定性/感知证据，但核心必须是直接的内容计数：追踪一段时间内的游行清单，逐家检索全国频道是否在时限内报道，并先定义 often 的阈值，再用访谈与二手研究三角验证。

10. Q3：Panuk 说他父亲会读所有选区来信——把这条证据的优点和缺点各写一句。

优点：这是有亲身经验的 testimony，且他父亲是 local politician，对“来信是否有用”有直接了解。缺点：只是一个人的 anecdote，不具代表性；且他父亲有 vested interest——原文说 he “wants their votes”，所以他说来信有用部分出于争取选票。

12. 通用句式银行（任何题都能借）

功能句式
引证据“This is shown by the evidence that ‘…’.” / “For example, the source states ‘…’.”
解释证据“This shows … because …” / “This matters because it affects …”
让步“Although [对方优点], this is outweighed by [更强理由] because …”
比较“X is more significant than Y because it affects more people / lasts longer / causes further problems.”
判断收尾“Therefore, overall, [判断], mainly because [核心理由].”
限定结论“This evidence only shows [范围], so it cannot prove [更大的主张].”
