二、Q1 (约 14 分)：六大批判性陈述精确辨析与图表趋势因果链

Q1 常失分不是因为看不懂英文，而是“判错类别”或“只写标签不写可核查理由”。请把下表作为考场唯一判定尺：

批判性概念 核心定义与文本信号词 能否被客观独立核查？ (Verification Rule) 考场满分作答公式（缺一不可）
Fact
客观事实		 已发生或现存的可观测状况；含具体年份、地点、测量百分比、法定条款。 可以核查（Verifiable）：可通过官方统计、实验观测或历史记录独立证明真伪。 引用原句 + 点明可借助官方记录/独立观测验证（can be verified objectively through official records / data）。
Opinion
个人观点		 作者基于个人感受、偏好或经验作出的主观解读（常含 I believe, seems, effective, inadequate）。 不可直接证实为真（Unverifiable as fact）：不同人可持有合理分歧，属于个人解读。 引用原句 + 指出这是个人主观诠释，缺乏普遍客观标尺验证（a personal view open to disagreement）。
Prediction
未来预测		 针对尚未发生之事作出的推断（信号词：will, is likely to, projected to, by 2040）。 当下不可核查（Cannot be verified yet）：因为事件尚未发生，即使附有模型数字也仍属预测。 引用原句 + 指出事件指向未来（refers to a future outcome that has not yet occurred），现阶段无法证实。
Value Judgement
价值判断		 基于道德、伦理或社会优先序作出的“好坏/应然”评判（信号词：should, ought to, unfair, immoral, right）。 不可被实证验证对错（Not empirically verifiable）：取决于不同文化或群体的价值观立场。 引用原句 + 指出包含应然/道德评判词，反映伦理优先序而非实证事实（based on moral beliefs rather than objective proof）。
Bias
主观偏见		 刻意只呈现单边有利证据、忽略反例，或使用强烈情绪煽动词（disaster, betrayal, miracle）引导受众。 表现为选择性失衡（Selective / one-sided presentation）：可通过比对缺失的反方数据揭露。 指出具体偏颇措辞或刻意遗漏（omission），说明其如何扭曲读者判断以迎合特定立场。
Vested Interest
既得利益		 作者或资助机构与结论存在直接经济收益、声誉保全、岗位或政治选票挂钩（常见于脚注/作者身份栏）。 客观利益关联可核实：作者头衔/公司业务/资助方直接显示其从某一政策中获益。 直接点名身份与利益绑定（profits financially / gains contracts or votes if this policy is adopted），质疑其中立动机。
📊 Q1 图表趋势提炼与“Local → Global 因果链”高分模板

1. 图表/数据趋势题（2–4 分）三步秒答：① 宏观总趋势（如持续上升/差距扩大）；② 成对精确数字（必须带上单位与年份，如从 2015 年的 24% 升至 2025 年的 41%，而高收入区仅 11%）；③ 异常值或范围限制（如有国家持平或仅统计城市地区）。

2. 因果推理大题（4 分）：避免孤立罗列要点，必须用因果连词（which in turn forces... / exacerbating... / creating a cycle where...）把材料中的个人/地方困境（Local）串联到国家财政与全球宏观后果（National / Global）。

三、Q2A (8 分)：RAVEN 来源评估双面模型（必须兼写 Strengths + Weaknesses）

Q2A 常问：“How well does the evidence in Source 3 support the argument that...?” 或 “Evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of Source 3.” 阅卷红线是双面平衡（Balanced Evaluation）——只骂商业来源有偏见，或只夸学者绝对可信，最高封顶 Level 2 (3–4/8 分)。审题时立即对 Source 3 逐一扫描 RAVEN 五尺，并强制在每条下找出至少 1 个正面价值与 1 个致命局限：

RAVEN 维度 Strengths 抓手（为什么部分可信？） Weaknesses 抓手（受限于何种漏洞/偏见？）
R · Reputation
机构与出版声誉		 发表于知名国际组织（WHO/OECD）、同行评审期刊或主流公共审计机构，经历多轮专家核审。 企业白皮书、倡议博客、商业新闻稿或未注明出版/同行评审流程的自媒体帖子，缺乏外部纠错门槛。
A · Ability to see
观测途径与样本范围		 作者处于一线现场（如社区工程师、多年调研员），或汇报了具体试点项目、最新年份数据。 样本量过小（如仅 1 个小镇或 12 所精选学校）、挑拣样本（cherry-picking）、仅靠二手转述或过时旧数据。
V · Vested Interest
既得利益与资助背景		 明确披露独立科研基金资助，或主动公开原始研究局限与潜在不利费用（透明度加分）。 作者所在公司直接售卖文中推销的技术/服务、政客争取选票，或脚注暗藏高额长期维护/续费合同。
E · Expertise
专业资历与对口度		 拥有相关领域博士学位、多年执业资历或政府专职研究头衔，技术原理或统计口径可信。 跨界评论（如营销总监大谈公共教育评估）、头衔与议题不对口，或仅以普通目击者身份做宏观经济推论。
N · Neutrality
中立度、措辞与指标效度		 措辞克制理性、承认替代解释或列明前提条件，未将局部现象无限放大。 使用绝对化或情绪词（only cure / catastrophic / moral failure）、偷换替代指标（如用“测试完成率”冒充“最终统考成绩”）。

四、Q2B (8 分)：How could you test this claim? 研究检验四大踩分维度

Q2B 要求针对某项具体主张设计实证检验方案。不要空喊“我会查资料和问人”，Level 4 (7–8/8 分) 必须按顺序写满以下 四大踩分维度，且每维写清具体操作对象、测量指标与防偏差机制：

  • 1. Sampling（分层随机抽样与样本量代表性）：按核心混杂因素（城乡区域、家庭收入、基础条件）分层随机抽样（stratified random sampling），写明具体且足够的样本规模（如 20 个匹配社区 / 300 户不同收入家庭），并设置匹配对照组（matched control group），彻底杜绝 Source 3 式的“挑拣富裕样本”。
  • 2. Primary Method（一手研究工具组合 — 三角验证）：采用 混合研究法（methodological triangulation）：① 定量对照实验或纵向追踪（测实施前 baseline pre-test 与实施后 1 年的客观指标变化）；② 匿名标准化问卷（避免诱导性提问 non-leading questions，采用李克特量表测量真实成本与使用率）；③ 对多方利益相关者（居民、基层操作员、独立监督员）做半结构访谈，解释量化数据背后的因果机制。
  • 3. Secondary Cross-check（二手官方与纵贯交叉核验）：将一手结果与独立政府统计局、市政水务/医疗账目、WHO/UNESCO 公开数据库及 3–5 年纵贯追踪数据（longitudinal data）交叉比对，验证短期试点红利是否随时间衰减，区分相关性（correlation）与真实因果（causation）。
  • 4. Reliability Control & Limitations（信效度控制与局限自省）：明确如何控制混杂变量（confounding variables）（如气候年份差异、外部补贴变动）并由无商业利益关联的第三方资助研究；最后主动点出 1–2 项本方案仍难消除的研究局限（如受访者为保留免费补贴而产生社会期许偏误 social desirability bias、长期追踪的样本流失率 attrition、或霍桑效应），并简述匿名核验补救法。

一、Q3 (16 分) 论证实力正面对比 Level 4：戒除“A 一段、B 一段”的六维交锋模板

Q3 提问固定为：“Which argument is more convincing, Person A’s or Person B’s?”。全卷最高频的丢分灾区就是“先总结 A 优缺点，再另起一段总结 B 优缺点”——这种分块写法哪怕双方各自写了 5 条分析，也会因为缺乏持续同维比较被考官封顶在 Level 2 (5–8/16 分)。要拿 Level 4 (13–16/16 分)，必须充当“论证机制裁判”，在同一段落内用 Whereas / Conversely / Unlike 把两人放到下表六维天平上逐一正面对撞：

交锋维度 (D1–D6) 强论证高分特征 (More Convincing) 弱论证漏洞特征 (Less Convincing) Level 4 同维交锋英文句型骨架
D1 · Evidence
实证支撑度		 引用独立大型审计、多年纵贯统计、可核查官方样本数据。 全篇依赖个人情感轶事（one anecdote）、“人尽皆知”无出处数字。 Whereas Writer A substantiates her claim with a 5-year audit across 42 cities, Writer B relies solely on an isolated anecdote and unverified generalisations.
D2 · Reasoning
因果与逻辑谬误		 因果机制严密、每步解释清楚“如何导致结果”。 存在滑坡谬误、非黑即白（false dilemma）、相关当因果或隐含未证实假设（unstated assumption）。 Writer A establishes a clear causal mechanism linking..., whereas Writer B rests on the unstated assumption that...
D3 · Balance
视角平衡与偏见		 主动承认自身主张的短期局限并给出反驳或缓释方案（concession）。 单边一刀切，完全回避执行代价与不利反证。 Crucially, Writer A demonstrates balanced reasoning by conceding..., in stark contrast to Writer B’s one-sided dismissal of...
D4 · Tone
语调与情绪煽动		 冷静克制、学术客观，依靠事实而非情绪施压。 充斥恐吓、道德绑架或极端标签词（catastrophic, betrayal, medieval）。 Writer A maintains an objective, measured tone appropriate to policy debate, unlike Writer B’s alarmist rhetoric such as “...”.
D5 · Reliability
来源权威与立场		 独立研究机构或跨界无盈利关联学者，头衔与议题高度匹配。 存在明显商业顾问/选票利益冲突（vested interest），样本刻意挑拣。 Writer A’s independent institutional role enhances credibility, whereas Writer B’s former industry affiliation introduces potential vested interest.
D6 · Conclusion
局部 vs 过度外推		 结论设有适用前提与边界条件（qualifying language）。 将特例无限外推为普遍规律（always / every city / overnight）。 While Writer A draws a nuanced, context-bound conclusion, Writer B commits empirical overgeneralisation by claiming...

二、红笔实战对照：为什么这篇 Q3 只能拿 6/16 (Level 2)，而另一篇拿到 15/16 (Level 4)？

❌ 低分反面示范：块状分述 + 个人立场站队（仅对比表层“谁有数字”） Level 2 · 6 / 16 Marks

In Source 4, Professor Al-Mansoor argues for fixing water pipes and tiered pricing. She says pipes lose a lot of water and gives a World Bank report about 42 cities. She also cares about poor people getting free basic water. However, digging up streets takes a long time and causes traffic.

On the other hand, Councillor Thorne wants desalination plants. He talks about a hospital shutting down and says desalination guarantees water forever. He sounds angry and cares about factories. Overall, I agree with Professor Al-Mansoor because saving water is better for the environment and cheaper for citizens.

🔴 考官红笔诊断（三大致命伤锁死 Level 2）

① 块状分述无对比：第一段只写 A，第二段只写 B，从未在同一标准下交锋；② 内容复述替分析：只概括两人说了什么（says / wants），未拆解逻辑漏洞或隐含假设；③ 个人站队代替论证审计：末尾写 “I agree... because saving water is better”，偏离“比较谁的论证更有说服力”。

✅ 满分正面示范：逐维正面交锋 + 揭露隐含假设 + 客观裁决 Level 4 · 15 / 16 Marks

Overall, Professor Al-Mansoor’s argument is significantly more convincing than Councillor Thorne’s because it is anchored in independent comparative evidence, balanced by explicit concessions, and free from emotive overstatement. First, regarding evidential authority, Al-Mansoor cites a 2024 World Bank/ADB five-year audit across 42 municipalities showing leak repairs cut losses by 28% at one-third the lifecycle cost of desalination; by contrast, Thorne offers zero verifiable financial or hydrological data, relying instead on a single emotive hospital-shutoff anecdote and the sweeping claim that desalination makes conservation “obsolete”. Second, in terms of balance and logical rigour, Al-Mansoor strengthens her credibility by conceding street-digging disruption and proposing lifeline allowances for low-income households, whereas Thorne’s argument rests on the unstated assumption that fossil-fuel power costs and marine brine pollution carry negligible long-term risk. Finally, while Thorne’s alarmist rhetoric (“criminal negligence”, “third-world thirst”) and former consultancy ties indicate promotional bias, Al-Mansoor maintains a measured, context-specific conclusion, making her case far stronger even though her repair timeline is slower.

三、Q4 (24 分) 压轴政策决策大作文：五维权衡矩阵与 4 段式高分架构

Q4 要求从题干提供的 3 个备选行动方案中推荐 1 项并论证为何最佳（单题占 24/70 分 = 34.3%）。死穴是“全篇只赞美首选方案、对另两项一笔带过”，这会被卡死在 Level 2 (7–12/24 分)。必须用下表 五维权衡标尺 贯穿四段写作：

Q4 五维权衡标尺 必考自问问题（考场 2 分钟草稿打分） 4 段式满分骨架 & 双边让步防护句式
1. Cost & Feasibility
成本与落地可行性		 初始基建费 + 长期运营维修费能否由地方财政持续承担？技术与施工人员是否本地可及？ 段 1（定标立论）：明确推荐 Option X，并亮出 3 条核心评估尺（cost sustainability, equity, long-term effectiveness）。

段 2（深证首选 · 挂钩跨源证据）：调用 Sources 1–4 实证，展开首选方案在经济、社会与治理维度的优势。

段 3（正面对照淘汰另 2 项备选）：必须逐一点名另外两个落选方案！使用对比转折句式证明为何它们在成本/持久度/覆盖面上较弱。

段 4（双边让步防护 + 配套缓释）：Admittedly, Option X carries short-term drawbacks such as...; however, these can be effectively mitigated by combining... with...（坦承 1–2 项弱点并给出分阶段补救措施，直接封顶 Level 4/5）。
2. Short vs Long-term
短期救急 vs 长期治本		 它是立刻缓解燃眉之急但易反弹，还是需 2–5 年见效却能彻底根治结构病灶？

3. 展开 Question 2(b) (8 marks) Level 4 Mark Scheme + 研究设计四要素英文满分范文

Q2(b) Model Answer (8/8):
To rigorously test whether desalination harms coastal ecosystems and fails to lower bills for low-income households, I would conduct a three-year mixed-methods comparative study combining environmental sampling, household bill audits, and stakeholder interviews. First, for sampling, I would select a stratified random sample of 400 households across Port Solara’s low-income northern wards and affluent coastal suburbs, paired with a socio-economically matched coastal control city without desalination, to eliminate Source 3’s cherry-picking bias.

Second, to measure affordability, I would compare audited municipal water bills and private-tanker receipts at baseline and again in Year 3 once commercial desalination tariffs take effect, alongside anonymous non-leading questionnaires on water-cut frequency and affordability stress. Q3 Model Essay (16/16):
Overall, Professor Nadia Al-Mansoor’s argument (Writer A) is substantially more convincing than Councillor Victor Thorne’s (Writer B) because Al-Mansoor grounds her case in independent multi-city evidence, acknowledges practical trade-offs, and maintains a measured tone, whereas Thorne relies on emotional anecdotes, false dilemmas, and unsubstantiated assumptions.

5. 展开 Question 4 (24 marks) Level 4/5 Mark Scheme + 四段五维决策英文满分范文

Q4 Model Essay (23–24/24):
To secure Port Solara’s urban water supply over the next five years, the municipal government should prioritise Option 2: Smart Pipe Rehabilitation paired with Tiered Urban Water Pricing. Assessing all three proposals against lifecycle affordability, long-term reliability, social equity, and environmental sustainability demonstrates that Option 2 outperforms both mega-scale desalination and rainwater subsidies, provided its short-term construction disruption is systematically managed.

Option 2 delivers the strongest structural value and directly targets the city’s root crisis. Source 1 and Source 2 reveal that Port Solara loses water not because reservoirs are empty, but because underfunded 1970s pipes leak roughly 36% of treated supply before reaching homes. As evidenced in Source 4, the 42-city World Bank/ADB audit proves sensor-guided leak rehabilitation recovers 28% of supply within three years at USD 0.35 per cubic metre—matching Singapore and Tokyo’s 14% benchmark (Source 1). Pairing upgrades with a free 50-litre daily lifeline eliminates low-income families’ dependence on predatory water tankers (Mateo’s 20% income loss in Source 2) while charging higher tariffs to luxury estates using private lawns.

By contrast, Option 1 (Mega-Scale Desalination) is financially and ecologically unsustainable. Although Source 3 claims delivery within fourteen months, desalination costs USD 1.15 per cubic metre—over triple Option 2 (Source 1)—and locks taxpayers into a 15-year commercial tariff once AquaNova’s Year 1–2 discount expires (Source 3 footnote). Worse still, high-pressure desalination still fails low-income wards if piped water continues leaking out of corroded 1970s mains, while marine brine discharge threatens coastal ecosystems. Admittedly, Option 2 carries two notable limitations: temporary traffic disruption from street excavations and the three-year lag before full leak recovery takes effect, during which vulnerable wards still face summer shortages. However, the city can mitigate both drawbacks by scheduling modular sensor-guided repairs at night along worst-leaking trunks first, and deploying a targeted 10% share of budget toward municipal emergency standpipe tanks and school rainwater harvesting in high-density northern wards during the transition. By pairing Option 2’s durable infrastructure with focused transitional relief, Port Solara achieves equitable, fiscally sustainable water security.

一、10 秒自诊：Component 1 最常卡在 Level 2 的五大症状与手术式修复

把自己的模考或课堂简答/长文对照下表做 4 步自查，先定位卡档症状，再录入下方诊疗台生成 AI 精批清单：

题号 常见低分症状（自查信号） 官方档位天花板 手术式提分修复动作（立即增分 +4 至 +8 分）
Q1(b) 只写出“Fact / Prediction”标签，未抄原句或仅写“因为它是真的/未来的”。 最多拿 2 / 4 分 固定句式：原句双引号 + 能否被独立客观核查（verify / cannot be verified yet / based on moral values）。
Q2(a) 看到商业高管来源便全篇批“有偏见不可信”，未提任何专业或时效亮点。 封顶 Level 2 (3–4/8) 强制首段用 On the one hand... 写清作者 PhD 资历、最新年份与具体试点数字，再用 However... 痛击利益与样本漏洞。
Q2(b) 只写“做问卷和查谷歌”，缺样本量、对照组、二手核验与局限反思。 封顶 Level 2 (3–4/8) 严格套用 4 要素清单：分层样本量 + 对照前后测/匿名问卷+访谈 + 官方纵贯比对 + 1 条混杂/社会期许局限。
Q3 第一段写完 Writer A，第二段写完 Writer B，末尾写“I agree with A because...”。 封顶 Level 2 (5–8/16) 撕掉两段分述！改为按 Evidence / Balance & Assumptions / Tone & Vested Interest 分段，每段必用 Whereas 同屏交锋并评估论证机制。
Q4 90% 篇幅夸首选方案，其余两个选项只写一句“too expensive / bad”，且美化首选无弱点。 封顶 Level 2–3 (10–14/24) 独立辟出第 3 段逐一对比淘汰另 2 个备选方案（挂钩材料数据），并在第 4 段坦承首选 1–2 个短期短板并附缓释措施。

Q1-01 · Fact 核查基准Q1 概念
What makes a statement a verifiable Fact in Q1(b), and how do you justify it in English?
如何用英文标准句式证明某句话是 Fact？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Quote the sentence with concrete figures/dates, then state: “This is a fact because it can be objectively and independently verified against official records or empirical measurement.”
Q1-02 · Prediction vs FactQ1 概念
Even if a source states “By 2040, shortages will rise by 51%”, why is it still a Prediction rather than a Fact?
为什么带具体百分比的未来预测仍不能算 Fact？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Because it projects an outcome that has not yet occurred; future projections (will / by 2040) cannot be verified as true today regardless of model precision.
Q1-03 · Value JudgementQ1 概念
How do you spot and explain a Value Judgement instantly?
一秒锁定“价值判断”的标志词与解释法？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Look for moral/prescriptive cues (should / ought to / unfair / basic right). Explain that it reflects an ethical or value-based stance rather than a testable empirical claim.
Q1-04 · Vested Interest vs BiasQ1 概念
What is the exact distinction between Bias and Vested Interest?
Bias（偏见）与 Vested Interest（既得利益）有何本质区别？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Bias is one-sided presentation/emotive framing in the text; Vested Interest is the author’s or sponsor’s personal, financial, or political stake/gain driving that slant.
Q1-05 · Causal Chain 连词Q1 趋势
Which sentence frame turns isolated bullet points into a 4-mark Local → Global causal chain in Q1(d)?
Q1(d) 拿满因果分的高级连接骨架？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Use multi-step links: “Locally, X forces families to..., which in turn disrupts..., ultimately exacerbating national/global...”（串联微观困境与宏观后果）。
Q2-01 · RAVEN 五维口诀Q2A 来源
What does every letter in RAVEN stand for when evaluating Source 3?
RAVEN 来源可信度评估模型的 5 个完整英文词？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Reputation（声誉） · Ability to see（观测视角与样本） · Vested interest（利益关联） · Expertise（专业资历） · Neutrality（中立与语调）。
Q2-02 · Cherry-Picked SampleQ2A 来源
How do you diagnose a trial that only surveyed resort managers or electrified pilot schools?
批驳“挑拣最有利试点或人群”的高分英文术语？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Cherry-picked / unrepresentative sample (selection bias)：所得高满意度无法外推至缺乏基建或低收入社区（lacks generalisability）。
Q2-03 · Proxy Measure 偷换Q2A 来源
What is wrong when a source uses “hotel manager satisfaction” to prove “universal city affordability”?
用“度假酒店满意度”替代“市民可负担性”犯了什么指标错误？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Weak operationalisation / invalid proxy indicator：衡量指标与核心待证主张脱节（does not measure low-income household bills）。
Q2-04 · Stratified & TriangulationQ2B 研究
Name the two core methodological phrases that upgrade Q2(b) into Level 4 (7–8/8).
Q2(b) 研究设计最核心的两个专业方法术语？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
① Stratified random sampling with a matched control group（按收入/地域分层对照）；② Methodological triangulation（定量考分/账单 + 定性访谈 + 官方二手数据）。
Q2-05 · Confounding & Social BiasQ2B 研究
Which two realistic limitations should you acknowledge at the end of Q2(b)?
Q2(b) 结尾必须主动反思哪两类真实研究局限？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
① Confounding variables（季节降雨/洋流/通胀等外部混杂干扰因果）；② Social desirability bias（受访者迎合资助方而不讲真话，需匿名账目核查）。
Q3-01 · Sustained ComparisonQ3 交锋
Which linking words guarantee point-by-point clash inside every Q3 paragraph?
确保 Q3 每段都在“同屏对比”而不是“分述 A/B”的关键衔接词？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
句中强制并置：Whereas Writer A..., Writer B... / Conversely / In stark contrast / Unlike Writer B’s reliance on..., Writer A substantiates...
Q3-02 · Unstated AssumptionQ3 谬误
How do you define and expose an Unstated Assumption in Q3?
如何在 Q3 犀利揭穿作者隐藏的“未明说前提假设”？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Point to a hidden condition the argument takes for granted: “Writer B rests on the unstated assumption that energy prices and marine brine discharge impose zero long-term costs.”
Q3-03 · False DilemmaQ3 谬误
What logical fallacy occurs when a writer claims “Either build desalination or drive all factories away”?
“不选我这招就一定完蛋”的二选一恐吓叫什么逻辑谬误？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
False dilemma / false dichotomy (虚假二分法)：人为抹杀中间折中方案（如阶梯水价保留基础工业配额、分期修管道）。
Q3-04 · Slippery Slope & OverstatementQ3 谬误
How do you critique claims like “Every metropolis knows X solves shortages forever”?
如何精准点评“所有大都市 / 永远彻底解决”类绝对化断语？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Critique it as sweeping empirical overgeneralisation / unverified absolutist claim（过度外推），并比对另一位作者的限定性结论（context-bound conclusion）。
Q3-05 · Concession 平衡力Q3 交锋
Why does conceding a limitation in your own argument make a Q3 writer more convincing?
为什么主动承认自身方案局限反而让 Q3 作者更有说服力？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Explicit concession and counter-mitigation signals intellectual honesty, balance, and anticipation of real-world obstacles—far stronger than dogmatic denial.
Q4-01 · Rejected Options 铁律Q4 决策
Why is ignoring or briefly dismissing the other two Q4 options fatal to Level 4/5 (19–24/24)?
为什么 Q4 不深入反驳另外两项落选方案会痛失 10 分以上？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Q4 explicitly assesses weighing different options. Without comparative criteria proving why both alternatives underperform on cost, equity, or durability, marks stay capped at Level 2 (7–12).
Q4-02 · Net Benefit 对比句型Q4 句型
What transition smoothly pivots from praising your chosen option to rejecting the other two?
Q4 从第 2 段推荐方案切入第 3 段驳斥另两项方案的满分过渡句？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
“By contrast, Option 1 proves fiscally unsustainable because...; similarly, although Option 3 offers..., it fails under prolonged...”（双线并驳）。
Q4-03 · Mitigation Shield 句型Q4 句型
How should Paragraph 4 acknowledge your chosen policy’s drawback without contradicting your recommendation?
Q4 第 4 段怎么做到“既承认缺点又不推翻自己结论”？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Use the Concession + Mitigation Shield：“Admittedly, Option 2 entails short-term disruption/time lag; however, this can be effectively mitigated by phasing night works and providing interim relief.”
Q4-04 · Lifecycle vs Upfront CostQ4 权衡
Why is distinguishing upfront capital cost from 5–15 year lifecycle operating cost crucial in Q4?
在 Q4 成本权衡时，为什么要拆开“初期补贴”与“长期全周期运营费”？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Commercial pitches often mask recurring fees with Year 1 discounts; comparing lifecycle cost per unit exposes hidden long-term fiscal traps and locks down Economic depth.
Q4-05 · Cross-Source SynthesisQ4 证据
How do you avoid losing AO1 evidence marks in Q4 even when writing your own policy evaluation?
如何在 Q4 写出“跨材料综合论证”而不是脱离资料空谈？
🙈 点击揭晓答案
Weave concrete data and stakeholders across all four sources (e.g. Source 1 lifecycle cost + Source 2 ward impact + Source 3 footnote trap + Source 4 multi-city audit).
