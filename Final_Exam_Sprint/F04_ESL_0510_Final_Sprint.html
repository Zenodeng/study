4.1 考纲锁定 · Paper 1 Reading (Ex 1–4) 与 Paper 2 Listening (40 分) 排雷总图

Paper 1 Reading Ex 1–4 Strategies (30 Marks) & Paper 2 Listening Distractor Radar (40 Marks)
2024–2027 统一试卷 · 不含 Speaking Reading 30 分 (≤45 min) · Listening 40 分 (~50 min)

一、期末范围锁定与 Paper 1 / Paper 2 双线配时铁律

本冲刺页严格锁定 ESL 0510（2024–2027 新考纲 · 取消 Core / Extended 分级） 笔试与听力双卷：Paper 1 Reading & Writing（2 小时 · 满分 60 分，占 0510 总评 70%）与 Paper 2 Listening（约 50 分钟 · 满分 40 分，占 0510 总评 30%）；明确不含 Paper 3 Speaking 口语。

⏱️ Paper 1 考场 120 分钟铁律：45 分钟阅读硬止损线

前 45 分钟必须结束阅读（Ex 1: 10 min · Ex 2: 12 min · Ex 3: 10 min · Ex 4: 13 min）；若某道 1 分阅读小题卡壳超过 90 秒，立刻标星跳过！把足额 70 分钟均分给各占 15 分的 Ex 5（35 min） 与 Ex 6（35 min），最后留 5 分钟扫雷查语法与词数。

二、Paper 1 Reading（Ex 1–4 · 30 分）考前速记攻略

题型与分值 高分核心操作流程 考官高频扣分陷阱与红线
Ex 1 Short Answer
8 分（≤10m）		 先圈疑问词（Why / How / What two...）与定位词，回原文锁定区间，做同义替换或去修饰提取核心短语。多问或列举两点时用分号隔开；注意数字后的货币/时间/人数单位。 反矛盾规则（Anti-contradiction）：若问 why/how 时贪图省事整句照抄，带入了无关主语、错误时间或否定修饰，即使答案句在里面也会被整题扣 0 分！
Ex 2 Matching
9 分（≤12m）		 先读全部选项/题干抽取 2 字核心概念短码（如“初疑/经费/意外收获”），再按 A → B → C → D 单遍扫读文章各段落收网，省下 40% 回找时间；最后核对每段出现次数通常在 2–3 次左右。 “同词原词假匹配”陷阱：题干写 cost，干扰段往往原封不动出现 cost（如 cost far less than feared），而真答案段却改写为 steep expenses / out of reach。
Ex 3 Note-Making
7 分（≤10m）		 严格围绕双标题从原文提炼核心名词短语或动名词短语，砍掉冠词/空洞修饰语与完整主谓结构；每条横线只放 1 个独立要点，遇到 and / as well as 连接的双要素必须拆成两行写。 ① 超行/一格塞两点（其余要点顺延失效）；② 漏并列点；③ 重复已有内容（横线前或 Heading 已印出的词不可再占格）；④ 照抄整句导致混入干扰。
Ex 4 Multiple-Choice
6 分（≤13m）		 2024+ 新增题型（替代旧 Summary）。先看题干锁定段落与转折词（However / Yet / Despite），自行预判作者立场与情感光谱（如 cautiously optimistic / sceptical / ambivalent），再用排除法淘汰选项。 必背五大干扰项排雷标签（见下方详表）：Too Extreme · Out of Scope · Twisted Logic · True-but-Wrong-Part · Opposite。
💣 Ex 4 五大常见干扰项排雷速查矩阵
  • 1. Too Extreme（过度绝对）：选项混入 always / completely / impossible / only / never 等极端词，而原文只是谨慎局部讨论（to some extent / rarely）。
  • 2. Out of Scope（无中生有）：选项听起来符合生活常识或“很合理”，但原文根本未提及该对比或原因，属于考生自我脑补。
  • 3. Twisted Logic（因果倒置 / 张冠李戴）：把原文 A 句的事实强行嫁接给 B 句当原因，或悄悄对调主语（如把“观众惊讶”改成“导演惊讶”）。
  • 4. True-but-Wrong-Part（真话答非所问）：选项陈述的内容在原文别的段落确实出现过，但并没有回答当前题干所问的具体限定（如问 initial reaction 却答成 final result）。
  • 5. Opposite（语义反转）：漏看否定前缀/半否定词（hardly / reluctant / far from convinced），或把“别人曾经以为但被作者反驳的旧观点”当成作者立场。

三、Paper 2 Listening（2024+ 全客观 40 分 Ex 1–5）攻克指南

新考纲听力 彻底取消手写填空拼写题，5 个部分共 40 题全部为客观选择题或配对题，每段音频均完整播放两遍。考场黄金节拍：听前 15–30 秒圈出题干限定词并划掉选项相同背景词（只看差异点）→ 第 1 遍用斜线排除显性错误、暂定候选并盯防后半句反转 → 第 2 遍专查 Actually / Wait / However 后是否推翻前文。

听力板块与分值 题型形态 核心考查点与高分破题策略
Ex 1 (Q1–Q8 · 8分) 8 段短对话/通知 · 4 选 1（常配图文） 高频埋伏数字价格时间与自我推翻修正；听到第一个数字先别下笔，等说话人确认最终决定。
Ex 2 (Q9–Q18 · 10分) 5 段短对话 · 每段 2 道 3 选 1 典型“一实一虚”：第 1 题问事实/计划原因，第 2 题考说话人情绪感受或最终妥协方案。
Ex 3 (Q19–Q26 · 8分) 1 段长独白 · 8 道句子完成 3 选 1 题序严格与时间线同步！听到下一题主干关键词时若上题还没定，立刻放弃纠结切入下一题。
Ex 4 (Q27–Q32 · 6分) 6 位说话人配 8 条观点 (A–H，多 2 项) 听前先把 A–H 压缩为 3 字中文态度标签；听第 1 遍先记每人主旨大意再对表，严防原词诱饵！
Ex 5 (Q33–Q40 · 8分) 1 段深度访谈 · 8 道 3 选 1 A* 拉分战场：专考隐含态度、双方共识/分歧，以及“客套认可但实质拒绝”的委婉表达。
听力三大干扰排雷口诀：重头戏永远在“推翻词、转折词、修正数字”之后！

① Self-correction（自我推翻修正）：Actually... / Wait, sorry... / On second thoughts... / Oh, hang on, I meant... —— 前半句 100% 是诱饵；
② Negation & Contrast（转折否定排除）：however / although / instead / rather than / apart from / rule out / far from —— 留意先扬后抑或否定旧方案；
③ Numbers / Dates / Units（数据时间单位陷阱）：分清 original vs discounted（原价 vs 折后）、helpers arrive vs doors open（工作人员入场 vs 对外开放）、15 vs 50、adult vs student。

4.2 Paper 1 Writing：四大文体模板 · Band 9 句式升级 · Top 10 语法诊所

Ex 5 Email & Ex 6 Article / Review / Report Register Control, Complex Engines & Model Comparisons
Ex 5 (15分) + Ex 6 (15分) = Paper 1 半壁江山 各 120–160 词 · Content 6 分 + Language 9 分

一、Ex 5 & Ex 6 四大文体黄金结构与语域（Register）四象限

Ex 5（Informal Email）与 Ex 6（Article / Review / Formal Report）各自要求 120–160 词（最稳字数区间 135–155 词），评分均拆分为 Content（6 分：要点覆盖 + 细节拓展 + 文体读者意识） 与 Language（9 分：准确性 + 句式词伙多样性 + 衔接语域）。写作失分的第一大杀手就是语域错位（Register Mismatch）——例如 Email 写出公文腔，或者 Report 写出 don't / kids / !。

图 4.2：Writing Ex 5 与 Ex 6 四大文体的语域刻度、分段骨架与高频扣分禁区对照。
✉️ Ex 5 Informal Email 100% 稳拿 Content 6/6 三步法
  • 1. 三点全覆 + 各自深拓 1–2 句：题目必定给出 3 个 bullet points（如 what happened / how you felt / why your friend should join），必须每点独立成群，并补上具体时间、人物细节或画面感，不可一句带过！
  • 2. 口语亲切感标配：开篇用 How are things going? / Guess what! / I'm writing to tell you all about...；自然融入缩写（I've / wasn't / you'd）与短语动词（pick up / turn out / cheer us on）；结尾用 Catch up soon, / Write back when you get a chance, + 名字。

二、Band 9 高级句式升级引擎（4 大通用高分模块）

在一篇 140 词左右的作文中，稳妥嵌入以下 2–3 种不同结构的复杂句，即可打破“主谓宾简单句堆砌”的 Band 6 天花板，冲刺 Language 8–9/9：

升格引擎类型 Band 9 黄金模板句式 考场易错警示
1. 倒装句
Negative Inversion		 Not only did the workshop boost my confidence, but it also sparked my interest in design.
Rarely have students experienced such engaging lessons.		 Not only / Rarely / Seldom 放句首时，前半句必须部分倒装（助动词 did / have / can 提前到主语前）！
2. 非限制性定语从句
Non-Defining Clause		 The canteen seating was overcrowded, which frequently discouraged students from staying for lunch. 前面逗号不可省，且引导词绝不能用 that，指代前面整件事必须用 which！
3. 分词结构作状语
Participle Clauses		 Having analysed the student survey, our committee identified two urgent issues.
Designed to cut screen fatigue, the new schedule includes outdoor breaks.		 防悬垂分词：句首分词动作的发出者或承受者，必须与逗号后面的主句主语完全一致！
4. 让步状语从句
Concessive Clauses		 Although the equipment proved costly initially, it has significantly reduced daily waste.
Despite initial doubts, the scheme turned out remarkably popular.		 用了 Although / While / Even though 后面严禁再加 but；Despite 是介词，只能接名词或动名词！

三、中国考生高频语法 TOP 10 防错诊所（考前 5 分钟逐行扫描清单）

编号与病灶 典型错句 (Redline) Band 9 修正 (Fix) 排雷口诀
① 双连词叠用 Although it rained, but we stayed. Although it rained, we stayed. Although/Because 与 but/so 永不共存。
② 逗号拼接跑句 The hall was noisy, I couldn't hear. The hall was noisy, so I could not hear. 两套主谓绝不能只用逗号连接，改分号或加连词。
③ 悬垂分词错位 Entering the gym, the noise shocked me. Entering the gym, I was shocked by the noise. 逗号后的主语必须能发出句首分词动作。
④ There be 双谓语 There are many students join the club. Many students join the club. / There will be... 不可写 There are... do 或 There will have。
⑤ 不可数名词复数 She gave me many advices and equipments. She offered valuable advice and equipment. advice/information/equipment/homework/research 绝不加 -s。
⑥ 介词 to 与 suggest I look forward to see you. / suggest him to go. I look forward to seeing you. / suggest that he go. look forward to 后接 V-ing；suggest 无 sb to do。
⑦ 时态全篇漂移 Yesterday we visited the lab and see robots. Yesterday we visited the lab and saw robots. 过去游记/经历通篇锚定过去时，切忌突然滑回现在时。
⑧ 长主语主谓不一致 The number of books are limited. The number of books is limited. 剥离 of 修饰短语找中心词；动名词作主语用单数。
⑨ 被动语态与 -ed/-ing The fair will hold soon; I am interesting. The fair will be held soon; I am interested. 物作主语检查 be + 过去分词；人感受用 -ed。
⑩ 定从重复与零冠词 The app which I used it / The technology The app that I used / Technology 定从不留多余代词 it/them；泛指抽象概念不加 the。

对照 1A · Ex 5 Informal Email（推荐一次周末科学工作坊 · 约 145 词） Band 9 示范 · 15/15 (C6 + L9)

Hey Sam! How are things going? Guess what—I've just got back from a Saturday robotics workshop at our town library, and I couldn't wait to fill you in!

To start with, an engineer taught us how to code miniature sensors using recycled cardboard frames. After two hours of trial and error, my partner and I actually built a mini car that dodged obstacles on its own! What amazed me most wasn't the coding itself, though, but watching total strangers team up and cheer whenever a model crossed the finish line.

Honestly, you'd have a blast at next month's session. Not only do you pick up practical soldering skills without needing any experience, but it's also a brilliant way to unwind after mock exams. Fancy signing up together next Saturday?

Catch up soon,
Alex

对照 1B · Ex 5 同题 Band 6 对比版（公文死板语域 + 细节干瘪 · 约 122 词） Band 6 警示 · 10/15 (C4 + L6)

Dear Sam,

I am writing to inform you about a robotics workshop that I attended on Saturday. First, an engineer taught us to make a small car. My partner and I built it in two hours and it moved nicely.

Second, the workshop was very good and everyone was very happy. Although the coding was difficult, but I learned many knowledges and felt surprised.

Finally, I suggest you to join the workshop next month because it is very useful for students. Please reply to my email soon.

Yours sincerely,
Alex

考官批注：1B 出现公文开篇（Dear Sam / I am writing to inform / Yours sincerely）、完全没有缩略词、反复使用空洞 very good/happy/useful，且混入 Although... but、knowledges、suggest you to join 等系统性病句，Language 被锁在 5–6 分；要点虽触及但缺乏具体画面感，Content 仅拿 4 分。

对照 2A · Ex 6 Formal Report（校园图书馆现状与改进报告 · 约 149 词） Band 9 示范 · 15/15 (C6 + L9)

Introduction
The aim of this report is to evaluate our school library facilities and suggest improvements, based on a survey of eighty Year 10 students.

Current Strengths
It was widely observed that the quiet study zone and helpful staff remain major attractions. Having expanded its fiction collection last term, the library has recorded a noticeable rise in lunchtime borrowing.

Areas of Concern
Despite these positive features, overcrowding occurs before afternoon lessons. Several computers run outdated software, which frequently delays students completing coursework. Moreover, poor ventilation in the reading corner causes discomfort during warm weather.

Recommendations
It is strongly recommended that ten additional study desks be installed along the south window and that existing computers receive urgent software updates. Implementing these measures would ensure a far more productive learning environment.

对照 2B · Ex 6 同题 Band 6 对比版（缺小标题 + 语域崩塌 + 病句连连 · 约 126 词） Band 6 警示 · 9/15 (C4 + L5)

Hi Principal, I want to tell you about our school library. Kids don't always feel comfortable there, so I think we need changes!

On the good side, there are many students love the new storybooks. The librarians are kind, they always help us find books quickly.

However, the library doesn't have enough chairs, it is too crowded at noon. Also, the computers are very slow and the air is hot, which make students tired.

In conclusion, the school should buy new computers and add more desks, so everyone can to study better in the future.

考官批注：2B 未分设小标题导致文体特征缺失；出现 Hi Principal / Kids / don't / doesn't / ! 严重违背 Formal Report 零缩写与客观原则；夹杂两处逗号拼接（kind, they / chairs, it）、There are... love 双谓语及 can to study 情态动词错误。

4.3 听读写三线浓缩综合模拟实战 (Reading + Listening + Writing)

Integrated Mini-Mock: Original 4-Para Passage (Ex 1–4), Two Trap-Rich Listening Drills & Writing Tasks

第一线 · Paper 1 Reading 综合精读选段（覆盖 Ex 1 / Ex 2 / Ex 3 / Ex 4）

The Rise of Youth Repair Cafés: Fixing More Than Broken Gadgets

Paragraph A When seventeen-year-old Nadia Chen noticed piles of discarded toasters and tangled headphones outside her apartment block, she decided to launch a monthly Youth Repair Café in the town library. Initially, several local electrical shopkeepers were sceptical, fearing that free repairs would steal away their paying customers. Yet within two months, those very shop owners began donating boxes of spare switches and sending trainee technicians along to help. Nadia also noticed that teenagers were hesitant to attend the opening session—not because of any entrance charge, as the service was completely free, but out of embarrassment at appearing unable to afford brand-new replacements.

Paragraph B To run smoothly, Saturday sessions follow a strict routine. Arriving items are sorted into mechanical or electrical faults and logged onto a shared digital fault tracker. Crucially, volunteers insist that owners remain seated beside the workbench throughout the repair rather than simply dropping damaged goods off and wandering away. Equipment costs were covered at first by a modest town-council micro-grant for soldering irons and precision screwdrivers, supplemented by an honesty jar where satisfied visitors drop spare coins to replace worn-out cables.

Paragraph C Retired electronics engineer Dr Patel, who mentors the student team, believes early newspaper coverage missed the heart of the project. Journalists praised the café almost exclusively for keeping plastic and metal out of landfill, whereas Dr Patel maintains its greater achievement is restoring teenagers' confidence with practical tools and bridging the gap between generations. Even so, one stubborn obstacle limits what the café can accept: strict public-liability insurance rules forbid volunteers from opening sealed lithium-battery devices such as smartphones or wireless earbuds.

Paragraph D Nadia readily admits that community repair tables will never match the speed or low unit cost of factory mass production. Nevertheless, she feels quietly optimistic about scaling the idea next term. Alongside adding weekday drop-in hours dedicated to bicycle maintenance, she plans to publish an open-access illustrated handbook so neighbouring schools can set up their own cafés without repeating her early administrative mistakes.

✍️ Ex 1 Short Answer 精练（作答后点开下方 Mark Scheme 核对反矛盾红线）

Q1 (1 mark): Why were some teenagers reluctant to bring broken items to the first Repair Café session?

Q2 (2 marks): Give two sources of funding that paid for the café's tools and replacement supplies.

📋 查看 Ex 1 官方 Mark Scheme 踩分词与 Anti-contradiction 扣分警示

Q1 (1 mark) Accepted: They felt embarrassed about seeming unable to afford new items / replacements.
Rejected (0 分)：若照抄 "not because of any entrance charge" 却漏掉 embarrassment，或误答 because it wasn't free，直接 0 分。

Q2 (2 marks) Accepted (any two, 1m each): ① a town-council micro-grant；② an honesty jar / voluntary visitor donations。
答题规范：写两点务必分号隔开或分行标注，不可写 shop owners donated spare switches（Para A 谈的是配件支持，题干问的是 paid for tools/supplies 的资金来源）。

第二线 · Paper 2 Listening 模拟文本剧本实战（专攻自我推翻 · 转折否定 · 数字陷阱）

（考场练习提示：模拟音频只听两遍——请按顺序精读以下两段高密度剧本，抓取 Self-correction 与 Contrast 信号词后再点选！）

🎧 Listening Transcript Drill 1 · 校园环保市集摊位通知与费用变更（模拟 Ex 1 / Ex 2）

Organiser: "Hi Mia, thanks for calling about Saturday's Eco-Fair stall. Last spring a single student table cost 18 pounds, and although the committee initially discussed raising it to 22 pounds this term, we decided to keep the early-bird student rate at 15 pounds—wait, sorry, 15 pounds was only for bookings confirmed before Wednesday; from Thursday onwards it rises to 20 pounds. Today is Thursday, isn't it? So it'll be 20 pounds unless you split a table with the Debate Club—oh, hang on, I just checked my email and the Debate Club pulled out ten minutes ago, so a 20-pound single table is what's left. Also, we'd originally planned to host the fair in the Main Hall from 9:30, however because of urgent roof repairs we've shifted everything to the Courtyard Canopy. Student helpers still report at 9:30 to set up, though visitors won't be admitted until 10:15."

Listening Drill 1 · Q7 1 mark · Price Self-correction
How much will Mia have to pay for her Eco-Fair stall?
Mia 最终需要为她的环保市集摊位支付多少钱？
Listening Drill 1 · Q8 1 mark · Venue & Time Contrast
Where and at what time will the Eco-Fair open to visitors?
环保市集最终将在何地、几点正式对访客开放？
🎧 查看 Listening Drill 1 干扰链路完整拆解
  • 数字连环排雷：18 镑（Last spring 过时价）→ 22 镑（initially discussed 被否决）→ 15 镑（wait, sorry... before Wednesday 过期早鸟价）→ 20 镑（周四起标准价，且 oh, hang on 排除了拼摊可能）。
  • 时间地点双维核对：originally planned... Main Hall from 9:30 在 however 后被屋顶维修推翻为 Courtyard Canopy，且题干问的是 open to visitors（10:15），不是 student helpers report（9:30）。
🎧 Listening Transcript Drill 2 · 夜班面包师纪录片拍摄访谈（模拟 Ex 5 深度态度题）

Interviewer: "Sam, viewers loved your short documentary on night-shift bakers. Was convincing a bakery to let you film the toughest hurdle?"
Sam: "Everyone assumes that, and I'd braced myself for weeks of rejections. Surprisingly, three family bakeries welcomed me straight away. What actually drove me up the wall wasn't even getting up at two in the morning—a loud alarm sorted that out—it was stopping my camera lens from misting over every time we stepped from the freezing street into the steamy oven room!"
Interviewer: "And why is there no background music at all?"
Sam: "Originally I asked a friend to compose a gentle piano score, and he sent a lovely track. Yet while editing the rough cut, I noticed the piano drowned out the natural rhythm of clattering trays and sizzling dough. Rather than enhancing the mood, the music made the kitchen feel artificial, so I removed the entire soundtrack."

Listening Drill 2 · Q9 1 mark · Negation Focus
What caused Sam the greatest difficulty during the filming process?
Sam 在拍摄过程中遇到的最大困难是什么？
Listening Drill 2 · Q10 1 mark · Contrast & Reason
Why did Sam decide to cut the piano music from the final film?
为什么 Sam 最终决定从成片中删掉钢琴配乐？
🎧 查看 Listening Drill 2 否定结构与同义改写解析
  • 高频否定句框：What actually drove me up the wall wasn't X — it was Y!（口语习语 drive sb up the wall = 让某人抓狂），misting over（镜头起雾）同义改写为 condensation。
  • 态度反转信号：Originally... Yet while editing... Rather than enhancing..., it made the kitchen feel artificial —— 删音乐并非音乐不好听，而是破坏了纪录片的真实感（realism）。

📝 Writing Task 1 · Ex 5 Informal Email (15 marks · 120–160 words · 35 min)

You recently helped organise a weekend book-swap event at your school. Write an email to your cousin telling them about it. In your email you should:

  • explain how you prepared for the event
  • describe an unexpected problem that happened on the day and how you solved it
  • say why your cousin should organise a similar event at their school.
📊 查看 Ex 5 官方 Content (6分) + Language (9分) 评分要点与高分提纲

Content 6/6 必须落地三层细节：① 准备阶段（如收集旧书、按学科分类贴标签、设计海报）；② 突发状况与化解（如早晨下起大雨户外摆摊泡汤，急忙和门卫协调搬进走廊或借遮阳棚）；③ 劝说表亲（如既能免费淘到好书又环保省钱、还能认识同好）。

Language 8–9/9 亮点加分项：必须使用缩略词（I've / wasn't / you'd）+ 地道感叹（Believe it or not, / What saved the day was...）+ 1 个自然复合句（如 Not only did over eighty students swap novels, but we also raised money for charity.）。

Score Band Content (0–6 分) 判分标准 Language (0–9 分) 判分标准

G04 · to 介词与 suggestGrammar

🙈 点击揭晓答案
