6.1 正式报告 Formal Report 黄金规范与三大铁律

Formal Report Structure: Mandatory Subheadings, Objective Passive Tone, Zero Contractions & Actionable Proposals
2024–2027 新考纲 Ex 6 15 Marks (Content 6 + Language 9) · 120–160 Words

6.1.1 为什么 Report 绝不能像 Email 或 Article 那样写？

很多考生失分不是因为语法错，而是文体错位（Register Mismatch）：把写给校长或主办方的 Report 写成跟同学聊天的语气。正式报告的核心交际目的，是基于调查或实地观察，向上级或决策者（Principal / Headteacher / Event Organiser / Committee）客观呈报现状并给出可落地建议。

⚖️ 三大文体核心分水岭（Ex 5 Email vs Ex 6 Article vs Ex 6 Report）
  • Ex 5 Informal Email（写给朋友）：亲切自然，必须使用缩略词（I'm, don't）与短语动词，不加小标题。
  • Ex 6 Article（写给校刊同学）：半正式活力互动，可用吸睛标题、反问句、you/we 及适度缩略词。
  • Ex 6 Formal Report（写给校长/机构）：正式、客观、去个人情感化（Formal, Objective & Impersonal）——用事实和数据说话，不用反问和感叹，必须分栏列小标题！

6.1.2 正式报告不可违背的三大格式与语域铁律

  1. 铁律一：必须使用清晰小标题分段（Mandatory Subheadings）
    典型四段式小标题组合：Introduction → Current Strengths（或 Positive Aspects）→ Areas of Concern（或 Issues Encountered）→ Recommendations（或 Conclusion and Recommendations）。小标题各自独立成行，考官一秒就能确认你的文体格式达标！
  2. 铁律二：严禁缩略词、感叹号与口语词，多用被动语态与客观汇报句
    • 绝对禁区：don't / doesn't / didn't / can't / won't / I'm / it's 全部展开为 do not / does not / did not / cannot / will not / I am / it is！严禁感叹号 !、反问句（Isn't that awful?）及口语词（kids, guys, awesome, cool, lots of, stuff, things）。
    • 高频客观被动句框：A survey was conducted among... ｜ It was observed that... ｜ Several students expressed concern regarding... ｜ A significant majority of respondents praised...
  3. 铁律三：即使题干未明确写“提建议”，结尾也必须给 1–2 条具操作性的方案
    例如题目只说 “Write a report about the recent museum trip”，也绝不要只停在“缺点”，必须以 Recommendations 收尾：It is strongly recommended that... / Implementing these measures would ensure...

6.1.3 口语低分表达 → 正式 Report 语域即时转换表

❌ 违规口语 / 缩略表达（失分） ✅ Band 9 正式客观转换 语域升级原理
Lots of kids don't like the canteen food. A considerable number of students expressed dissatisfaction with the canteen menu. 去除 lots of / kids / don't，改用量化主语 + 名词化短语
I think we shouldn't waste time waiting in line! It was noted that prolonged queues caused unnecessary delays. 去掉主观 I think 与缩略/感叹号，改用形式主语被动汇报
The trip was super awesome and cool. Overall, the excursion was regarded as highly educational and engaging. 用客观被动评价 was regarded as 替代夸张口语词
You should buy some new computers for us. It is strongly recommended that outdated desktop hardware be replaced promptly. 用建议从句（虚拟语气原形 be replaced）礼貌提出可执行措施

6.2 冲刺 Language 8–9/9 必考“六大复杂句型升格引擎”

Six High-Scoring Complex Sentence Engines (Universal Across Ex 5 & Ex 6 Writing)
Language 9 分核心壁垒 全书写作通用升格公式

为什么同样写满 145 词、要点齐全，有人 Language 只拿 5–6 分，有人却稳拿 8–9 分？剑桥评分标准明确区分：Band 5–6 依赖简单句与 and / but / so / because 并列连缀；Band 8–9 能够自然精准地驾驭多样化复杂句式与高级衔接。下面这 六大升格引擎，在一篇 Ex 6 中只需自然嵌入 3–4 个不同类型，即可显著拉升得分档位：

Engine 1 · 非限制性定语从句（Non-Defining Relative Clause）

公式：主句, which not only [动词短语 A], but also [动词短语 B].

Band 5 原句：Guest speakers gave clear talks. This helped students understand physics and want to study engineering.

Band 9 升格：Guest speakers delivered clear demonstrations, which not only clarified complex concepts but also inspired several students to consider engineering careers.

⚠️ 避坑红线：非限制性定从前面的逗号不可省，且引导词绝不能用 that 代替 which！

Engine 2 · 分词短语作状语（Participle Clauses: Doing / Having Done / Done）

公式：Having done X, [主语] concluded that... ｜ Designed to do X, [名词] proved...

Band 5 原句：We analysed all the survey forms. Then our committee realised that the seating was too cramped.

Band 9 升格（主动完成）：Having analysed the survey feedback, our student committee concluded that cramped seating hinders group revision.

Band 9 升格（被动用途）：Designed to encourage practical engagement, the robotics stalls proved exceptionally popular among participants.

⚠️ 避坑红线（防悬垂分词）：分词的逻辑主语必须等于主句主语！错句：Having analysed the feedback, it became clear...（it 不能分析反馈！必须改主语为 our committee / we）。

Engine 3 · 让步状语高阶转换（Advanced Concession Structures）

公式：Although S + V, S + V ｜ Despite the fact that S + V, S + V ｜ Much as S + V, S + V

Band 5 原句：The printed schedule looked very nice, but many rooms changed without telling us.

Band 9 升格：Although the printed programme looked impressive, several workshop rooms were relocated without prior notice.（或 Despite the fact that the programme appeared well organised, several rooms were relocated unexpectedly.）

⚠️ 避坑红线：中文思维极易误写 Although..., but...（英文绝对禁止连用！）；用 Despite / In spite of 后只能接名词或动名词，接完整句子必须加 the fact that！

Engine 4 · 否定词开头部分倒装（Negative Inversion）

公式：Not only + 助动词/情态动词/be + 主语 + 动词, but + 主语 + also... ｜ Rarely + have/has + 主语 + 过去分词...

Band 5 原句：The old computers are slow and there are not enough power sockets either.

Band 9 升格：Not only are the existing desktop computers outdated, but electrical sockets are also scarce.

Band 9 升格（频率/罕见强调）：Rarely have our students experienced such prolonged queues at entrance desks.

⚠️ 避坑红线：只有前半句倒装，but also 后恢复正常陈述语序！实义动词一般现在/过去时必须借用 do / does / did（如 Not only does it reduce waste, but it also lowers costs）。

Engine 5 · 第二类虚拟与省略 If 条件倒装（Second & Inverted Conditionals）

公式：Were + 主语 + to + V原形, 主语 + would + V原形 ｜ Should + 主语 + V原形, 主语/V-ing + would...

Band 5 原句：If the school builds more soundproof study rooms, senior students will feel much happier.

Band 9 升格：Were additional soundproof study pods to be introduced, senior students would benefit considerably.

Band 9 升格（应急建议）：Should schedule changes occur, sending instant mobile alerts to school coordinators would prevent delays.

⚠️ 避坑红线：用了 Were... to... 倒装后，主句情态动词必须回退过去式（would / could / might），不可混用 will！

Engine 6 · 形式主语、What 名词性裂句与客观被动汇报（Anticipatory 'It' & 'What' Cleft）

公式：It is worth noting that... ｜ It is widely acknowledged that... ｜ What concerned respondents most was + 名词/that从句

Band 5 原句：Most students worried most about the noisy study hall.

Band 9 升格：It is worth noting that background noise remains a primary concern. ／ What concerned respondents most was the excessive noise inside the study hall.

⚠️ 避坑红线：What + 谓语... 作主语时，后面系动词通常用单数 is / was；It is worth noting that... 中 worth 后只接主动形式 noting（勿写 worth to note）。

6.3 2 套完整 Report 精批范文（Band 9 140–155 词 vs Band 5 失分版）

2 Complete Annotated Report Models (Library Renovation Survey & Science Week Visit) + Register Diagnosis
精确词数核验 (140–155w) 完整植入六大句型引擎

下面两套 Report 均先给出 Band 9 满分版（正文与含小标题均严格控制在 140–155 words，标注色块：蓝色 = Content 客观调研发现与可落地建议 ｜ 绿色 = Language 正式语域与六大复杂句型引擎），随后对照同题目的 Band 5 口语化失分版（红色波浪线 = 语域违规与低分硬伤）。

🏛️ Topic 1 · Band 9 范文（向校长汇报校园图书馆改造调研）｜ 正文 146 words（含小标题 153 words） Band 9 · 15/15 (C6 + L9)

Task: Your principal wants to upgrade the school library and asked you to survey students and write a report outlining what works well, what needs improvement, and your recommendations.

Introduction

The aim of this report is to evaluate the school library and present recommendations for its upcoming renovation, based on a survey of 120 students.

Current Strengths

It was observed that the majority of respondents value the library's calm atmosphere and helpful staff. Furthermore, the recent addition of contemporary fiction has boosted borrowing rates, which demonstrates strong pupil interest in independent reading.

Areas of Concern

Despite these positive aspects, several pressing issues were identified. Not only are the existing desktop computers outdated, but electrical sockets are also scarce. Having analysed the survey feedback, our student committee concluded that cramped seating and poor lighting severely hinder effective group revision during examination periods.

Recommendations

It is strongly recommended that the school replace old hardware and install individual desk lamps. Were additional soundproof study pods to be introduced, senior students would benefit considerably. Implementing these measures would transform the facility into a modern learning hub.

查看考官评分拆解（Topic 1 · 六大引擎定位核验）

[格式与语域] 四大清晰小标题齐全；全文 0 个缩略词、0 个感叹号、0 个主观第一人称 I（仅用客观团队 our student committee 与被动语态）。

[引擎精准植入] ① Engine 1 非限制定从：..., which demonstrates strong pupil interest...；② Engine 2 分词状语（主语一致无悬垂）：Having analysed the survey feedback, our student committee concluded...；③ Engine 3 让步：Despite these positive aspects...；④ Engine 4 否定倒装：Not only are the existing desktop computers outdated, but...；⑤ Engine 5 省略 if 虚拟倒装：Were additional soundproof study pods to be introduced...；⑥ Engine 6 形式主语：It was observed that... / It is strongly recommended that...（虚拟原形 replace / install）。

🚨 Topic 1 · Band 5 口语化失分版红笔批注（同题反面教材） Band 5 · 7/15 (C3 + L4)

Hi Principal, I'm writing to tell you about our school library survey. Most kids love the quiet space and nice librarians, and they like the new storybooks too. However, lots of things aren't good enough! The computers are super slow and we can't find plugs for our laptops. Also, the chairs are too small and the lights are dark when we study for exams. I think you should buy cool new PCs and make more study rooms soon!

🩺 红笔批改清单（为什么直降两档？）

① 无小标题 + 误用信件称呼 Hi Principal,（Report 不写 Dear/Hi）；② 混入 I'm, aren't, can't 缩略词与两处感叹号 !；③ 口语词扎堆：kids, lots of things, super, cool；④ 全篇都是 and 并列简单句与主观 I think，建议缺乏具体操作性（如未提及台灯、隔音学习舱）。

🔬 Topic 2 · Band 9 范文（向活动主办方汇报科学周参观报告）｜ 正文 143 words（含小标题 149 words） Band 9 · 15/15 (C6 + L9)

Task: You recently accompanied a group of students to a regional Science Week exhibition. Write a report for the event organiser evaluating the positive aspects, the problems encountered, and suggesting improvements for next year.

Introduction

This report outlines student feedback following last week's regional Science Week exhibition and suggests practical improvements for future events.

Positive Aspects

Overall, the exhibition was regarded as highly educational. Designed to encourage practical engagement, the robotics and renewable-energy stalls proved exceptionally popular among young participants. Moreover, guest speakers delivered clear demonstrations, which not only clarified complex concepts but also inspired several students to consider engineering careers.

Issues Encountered

Nevertheless, certain organisational weaknesses were noted. Rarely have our students experienced such prolonged queues at entrance desks, largely owing to an inefficient manual registration system. Although the printed programme looked impressive, several workshop rooms were relocated without prior notice, causing unnecessary confusion.

Recommendations

It is therefore recommended that organisers introduce online QR-code registration next year. Should schedule changes occur, sending instant mobile alerts to school coordinators would prevent delays. Adopting these straightforward steps would ensure a far smoother visitor experience.

查看考官评分拆解（Topic 2 · Content 6/6 + Language 9/9）

[Content 6/6] 精准覆写三大命题任务：① 亮点（机器人与新能源展台动手参与强 + 嘉宾演示清晰并激发工程志向）；② 问题（人工签到造成入口长排长队 + 未提前通知更换教室导致混乱）；③ 来年改进（二维码线上签到 + 即时手机日程变更通知）。

[Language 9/9] 过去分词开头（Designed to encourage...）、非限制性定从（which not only... but also...）、否定副词倒装（Rarely have our students experienced...）、Although 让步 + 现在分词结果状语（causing unnecessary confusion）、省略 if 条件倒装（Should schedule changes occur...），无一处语法或语域瑕疵！

🚨 Topic 2 · Band 5 游记流水账失分版红笔批注（同题反面教材） Band 5 · 7/15 (C3 + L4)

Last Tuesday, we went to the Science Week by school bus. It's a really cool place and we had lots of fun! We saw robots and listened to speakers and they were awesome. But we waited forever at the door because the system wasn't good. Although our teachers had the paper timetable, but some rooms changed and guys got lost. Next year, don't make us wait so long and tell us when rooms move.

🩺 红笔批改清单（游记化 + 语法硬伤）

① 写成了个人日记/游记（went by school bus, had lots of fun），缺少报告小标题；② 出现 It's, wasn't, don't 缩略词与 cool, awesome, guys, waited forever 口语词；③ 致命语法错误：Although..., but... 连用；④ 结尾用祈使句命令主办方（don't make us wait），极其不礼貌！

Section B · 6 道 Band 5 → Band 9 句型合并升格书面特训

请依次用指定的 复杂句型引擎（Engines 1–6）将两句零碎简单的 Band 5 句子合并升格为一句 Band 9 正式句（可复制到上方“M06 交互写作台”或草稿本练笔，完成后展开核对评分细则与标准答案）。

Upgrade Drill 1 · Engine 1 非限制性定语从句[2 marks]
Original Band 5: The school replaced the old fluorescent bulbs with LED lights. This lowered electricity bills and reduced eye strain in classrooms.
Required Engine: 合并为一句，使用 ..., which not only..., but also...
将“更换 LED 灯”与“降低电费、减轻眼睛疲劳”两句用非限制性定从合并为一句。
查看评分标准与 Band 9 满分参考句

[M1] 正确使用逗号 + which 指代前面整件事（不可用 that，不可漏逗号）。
[M1] 平行结构 not only lowered..., but also reduced... 时态一致。

Band 9 参考句：The school replaced the old fluorescent bulbs with LED lights, which not only lowered electricity bills, but also reduced eye strain in classrooms.

Upgrade Drill 2 · Engine 2 分词短语作状语[2 marks]
Original Band 5: The student council reviewed 150 questionnaires. After that, the council proposed extending library hours until 6 p.m.
Required Engine: 使用完成时分词短语 Having + 过去分词..., ... 开头（确保无悬垂分词！）
用 Having... 分词结构合并两句，注意保持主句主语为动作执行者。
查看评分标准与 Band 9 满分参考句

[M1] 正确写出完成分词短语 Having reviewed 150 questionnaires,。
[M1] 主句主语明确锁定为 the student council（严禁写成 it was proposed... 导致悬垂分词！）。

Band 9 参考句：Having reviewed 150 questionnaires, the student council proposed extending library hours until 6 p.m.

Upgrade Drill 3 · Engine 3 高阶让步状语转换[2 marks]
Original Band 5: The new online booking portal looks modern. However, many parents still struggle to navigate its menu.
Required Engine: 使用 Despite the fact that... 或 Although... 合并（禁止出现 but）
用高阶让步句型把“界面看似现代”与“家长仍难以操作菜单”合二为一。
查看评分标准与 Band 9 满分参考句

[M1] Despite the fact that 或 Although 引导让步从句准确无误。
[M1] 主句前不加 but / however，用逗号直接连接主句。

Band 9 参考句：Despite the fact that the new online booking portal looks modern, many parents still struggle to navigate its menu.（亦可用 Although... 替代 Despite the fact that。）

Upgrade Drill 4 · Engine 4 否定词开头部分倒装[2 marks]
Original Band 5: The outdoor seating area lacks shelter from rain. It also lacks sufficient rubbish bins.
Required Engine: 使用 Not only + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词原形, but... also...
将户外座位区“既缺避雨棚、又缺垃圾桶”合并为否定词部分倒装高分句。
查看评分标准与 Band 9 满分参考句

[M1] 前半句正确借用助动词并倒装：Not only does the outdoor seating area lack...（动词 lack 必须回原形！）。
[M1] 后半句保持正常陈述语序：but it also lacks sufficient rubbish bins.

Band 9 参考句：Not only does the outdoor seating area lack shelter from rain, but it also lacks sufficient rubbish bins.

Upgrade Drill 5 · Engine 5 省略 If 的虚拟条件倒装[2 marks]
Original Band 5: If the school installs covered bicycle racks, more pupils will cycle safely to campus.
Required Engine: 改写为 Were the school to..., ... would... 正式建议句
把普通真实条件句升格为 Report 结尾专用的 Were... to... 礼貌虚拟建议句。
查看评分标准与 Band 9 满分参考句

[M1] 省略 if 并提前 Were，搭配不定式 to install。
[M1] 主句助动词同步使用虚拟过去式 would cycle（可加 safely 或 more confidently）。

Band 9 参考句：Were the school to install covered bicycle racks, more pupils would cycle safely to campus.

Upgrade Drill 6 · Engine 6 形式主语 / What 裂句客观汇报[2 marks]
Original Band 5: I noticed that the lack of quiet revision rooms bothered Year 11 students the most.
Required Engine: 去掉主观 I noticed，分别用 It is worth noting that... 或 What bothered... most was... 改写
去除第一人称主观表述，改用形式主语或 What 裂句升级为客观报告腔。
查看评分标准与 Band 9 满分参考句

[M1] 成功剔除主观 I noticed，切换为客观结构。
[M1] 准确使用 It is worth noting that... 或 What bothered... most was...（单数系动词 was）。

Band 9 参考句（任选其一）：
• What bothered Year 11 students most was the lack of quiet revision rooms.
• It is worth noting that the shortage of quiet revision rooms concerned Year 11 students most.
