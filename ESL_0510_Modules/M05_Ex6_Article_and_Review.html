5.1 校刊文章 Article（半正式互动语域）完整通关法

School Magazine Article: Catchy Titles, 3-Option Hook Openings, Dual-Paragraph Body & Reader Call-to-Action
2024–2027 新考纲 Ex 6 15 Marks (Content 6 + Language 9) · 120–160 Words

5.1.1 Ex 6 考纲铁律与 Article 语域（Register）精准定位

在 2024–2027 新考纲中，Paper 1 Exercise 6 不再区分 Core 与 Extended，满分统一为 15 分（Content 6 分 + Language 9 分），官方目标词数为 120–160 words。Ex 6 常考三大文体：Article（校刊/青年杂志文章）、Review（书评/影评/场馆活动评价） 与 Report（正式调研报告）。

🎯 语域定位：为什么 Article 既不能写成聊天短信，也不能写成枯燥报告？

Article 的预设读者是同龄学生或校刊读者（fellow students / school newsletter readers），因此语域必须是半正式互动感（Semi-formal, lively & engaging）：
• 与 Ex 5 Email 的区别：可以适度使用常见缩略词（如 don't, it's, you'll），但禁止过度随意的网络缩写与俚语（如 lol, gonna, wanna, btw, awesome guys），且结构必须有清晰论点推进而非家常闲聊。
• 与 Ex 6 Report 的区别：绝不用 Introduction / Recommendations 等生硬公文小标题，而是依靠吸睛主标题 + 设问/画面开头 + 直接呼应读者（you / we）来抓住阅读兴趣。

5.1.2 吸睛标题（Catchy Title）避坑与三式升级法

阅卷考官看一篇 Article 的前 3 秒，最先扫到的就是标题。写成 An Article About Sleep 或把题目抄一遍，会立刻暴露平庸的读者意识。高分标题控制在 5–10 词，不加 Title: 标签，优先使用以下三式：

低分平淡标题（忌用） 高分升级招式 Band 9 吸睛标题示范 提分效果
Article about teenage sleep 式 1：比喻 + 设问悬念型 Running on Empty? Why Teens Deserve More Sleep 用汽车缺油比喻缺觉，立即引发学生共鸣
Should we ban phones on Friday? 式 2：短句对比 + 冒号点题 Screens Off, Minds On: The Case for Phone-Free Fridays 节奏干脆利落，鲜明亮出立场与话题关键词
Building an outdoor gym at school 式 3：动词押韵 + 问号挑衅 Sweat, Smile, Succeed: An Outdoor Gym for Break Time? 三连动词有力抓耳，问号邀请读者参与讨论

5.1.3 首段“三选一 Hook 开头公式”（约 25–30 词）

首段唯一任务是两句话内勾住读者 + 点出核心议题（Thesis），切忌写 “I am writing this article to tell you my opinion about...”（那是信件或报告腔！）。考试直接套用下面三种 Hook 之一：

Hook A · 设问直呼读者（最稳万能式）

Have you ever noticed how quiet our courtyard becomes when everyone is glued to a glowing screen?（紧接过渡句交代背景与争议）

Hook B · 反常识 / 校园痛点画面代入

Picture spending twenty minutes between double maths and history lifting weights in the fresh air instead of slouching on a bench.

Hook C · 惊人现象 / 对比统计引出

Did you know that nearly half of our classmates skip breakfast just to grab ten extra minutes of sleep? It is time to rethink our morning routine.

5.1.4 主体双段论证结构与强有力结尾呼吁

  • 单边倡议型题目（如支持某活动/项目）：
    Para 2（约 45 词） 阐述第一大核心收益 + 具体校园细节（如 between double science lessons, during stressful mock weeks）；
    Para 3（约 45 词） 阐述第二层价值，或预判反对意见并反驳（Of course, critics question... Yet in reality...）。
  • 利弊讨论型题目（For & Against / Give your views）：
    Para 2（约 45 词） 肯定正面价值（On the bright side... Furthermore...）；
    Para 3（约 45 词） 转折讨论担忧并给出折中解决（Admittedly, some students worry that... However, simple rules such as... can resolve this easily.）。
  • Para 4 互动结尾（约 20–25 词）：用凝练金句总结 + 行动呼吁 / 留白反问，把思考交还给校园读者！例：Why not give it a fair shot this term? / After all, small daily changes can turn an ordinary campus into a thriving community.
文章功能位置 校刊读者意识高频表达库（Semi-Formal Reader Engagement）
首段拉近读者 Have you ever wondered why...? ｜ Look around our canteen at lunchtime... ｜ Let us be honest: none of us enjoys...
主体让步与反驳 Admittedly, some argue that... However,... ｜ Critics may point out..., yet... ｜ While cost is a valid concern,...
结尾行动呼吁 Why not step outside and try it yourself? ｜ It is high time we took action. ｜ Could this be the change our school needs?

5.2 书评 / 影评 / 活动与场馆评价 Review 完整通关法

Review Writing: Zero-Spoiler Brief Context, Evaluative Commentary, Balanced Criticism & Audience Verdict
2024–2027 新考纲 Ex 6 15 Marks · Avoid Plot-Summary Trap!

5.2.1 Review 最大失分黑洞：流水账剧情复述（No Spoilers!）

⚠️ 考官头号扣分警告：写成了“剧情梗概”或“一日游流水账”

中国考生写 Review 最常踩的坑，就是花 80–100 词复述电影从头到尾发生了什么（First the hero goes to Mars, then a storm hits, then he repairs the ship and returns home safely...），或者按时间表记录参观过程（At 9 am we arrived, at 10 am we saw robots, at 12 pm we ate lunch...）。
铁律：Review 的核心动词不是“发生了什么 (happened)”，而是“你如何评价它 (evaluated)”！
• 背景概括严格限制在 2 句话（25–30 词）：点明对象名称、类型/主创或地点、核心看点，严禁剧透结局（No spoilers!）。
• 把其余 75% 以上篇幅留给多维亮点分析 + 一条温和客观的不足 + 精准目标受众推荐。

5.2.2 万能 Review 四段平衡框架（影评 / 书评 / 场馆 / 餐厅 / App 通杀）

段落与词数 核心写作任务 适用维度举例（影评 / 书评 / 场馆餐厅）
Title + Para 1
(约 25–30 词)		 短评标题 + 零剧透速写 交代作品/地点名称、主创或位置、题材氛围（Set aboard a damaged station... / Tucked beside the central library...）。
Para 2 · 优点亮点
(约 45–50 词)		 评估性褒奖（2 个具体方面） 电影看 cinematography, score, pacing, acting；书籍看 vivid world-building, suspense, relatable characters；场馆/餐厅看 hands-on exhibits, atmosphere, value for money。
Para 3 · 温和保留
(约 35–40 词)		 平衡批判（1 条建设性不足） 用 That said / My only minor reservation is that... 客观指出 1 处小瑕疵（节奏拖沓、周末拥挤、价格偏贵、部分套路化），体现真实判别力！
Para 4 · 受众结论
(约 25–30 词)		 总评 + 精准受众推荐 禁止空喊 Everyone should go! 改为分众推荐：Fans of X will adore it, though younger viewers / those seeking Y might...

5.3 4 篇 Band 9 精批示范范文（135–152 词）与中低分雷区对照

4 Fully Annotated Band 9 Models (2 Articles + 2 Reviews) & Band 5 Pitfall Diagnosis
精确词数核验 红蓝双色标注解构

以下 4 篇范文均严格对照 2024–2027 新考纲 120–160 词区间精修，每篇正文与含标题词数均精确控制在 135–152 words 之间。批注色标说明：蓝色高亮 = Content 论点与具体校园/评估细节 ｜ 绿色高亮 = Language 高分句型、互动语域与精准评价词伙。

📰 Model 1 · Article（校园无手机日利弊讨论）｜ 正文 138 words（含标题 145 words） Band 9 · 15/15 (C6 + L9)

Task: Your school is considering introducing a 'Phone-Free Friday' policy. Write an article for the school magazine giving your views.

Screens Off, Minds On? Reclaiming Our Fridays

Have you ever noticed how quiet our courtyard becomes when everyone is glued to a glowing screen? With our school considering a Phone-Free Friday trial, students are fiercely divided.

On the bright side, ditching smartphones for one day could transform break times. Instead of scrolling mindlessly, classmates would actually talk, share jokes, or join quick basketball games. Furthermore, teachers report that fewer buzzing notifications lead to sharper focus during afternoon lessons.

Admittedly, some students worry about losing contact with parents or missing study apps. However, urgent messages can easily pass through the school office, and paper planners still work reliably for just six hours!

Ultimately, one phone-free day will not cut us off from the modern world; it might simply remind us how much fun real-world company can be. Why not give it a fair shot this term?

查看考官逐段红笔拆解（Model 1 · Content 6/6 + Language 9/9）

[Content 6/6] 完美覆盖利弊双面：Para 2 写出课间真实社交 + 下午课堂专注度提升两大具体收益；Para 3 正面回应“联系家长”与“学习软件”两大顾虑，并给出办公室转达 + 纸质日程表两条可行解决方案。

[Language 9/9] ① 标题采用 Screens Off, Minds On? 韵律对比；② 首句设问 + glued to a glowing screen 画面感极强；③ 使用 ditching, mindlessly, buzzing notifications, give it a fair shot 等自然地道表达；④ 分号衔接对照，结尾感叹与反问呼应校刊读者意识。

🏋️ Model 2 · Article（校园课间户外健身区倡议）｜ 正文 137 words（含标题 146 words） Band 9 · 15/15 (C6 + L9)

Task: Some students think the school should build an outdoor fitness zone in the playground. Write an article for your school newsletter sharing your opinion.

Sweat, Smile, Succeed: An Outdoor Gym for Break Time?

Picture spending twenty minutes between double maths and history lifting weights in the fresh air instead of slouching on a bench. Building an outdoor fitness zone on the back lawn could turn that vision into reality.

Regular movement during breaks brings undeniable rewards. Research consistently shows that brief bursts of exercise sharpen memory and lift mood, helping stressed exam candidates return to class energised rather than sluggish. Unlike indoor sports halls, open-air equipment stays freely accessible to everyone without booking hassle.

Of course, critics rightly question the cost and weather. Yet sturdy, low-maintenance stations need minimal upkeep, and sheltered canopies would protect users from sudden rain. Clear safety signs would also prevent careless injuries.

Investing in student wellbeing is never wasted money. Should we not transform that underused patch of grass into a healthier, happier campus hub?

查看考官逐段红笔拆解（Model 2 · Content 6/6 + Language 9/9）

[Content 6/6] 不仅论证运动对考生记忆力、情绪与免预约便利性的好处，更主动预判成本、雨天与受伤三大反对点并逐一破解，说服力极强。

[Language 9/9] ① 首句 Picture spending... instead of slouching... 动词画面对比鲜明；② 分词短语 helping stressed exam candidates return... energised rather than sluggish 结构精准；③ 结尾否定疑问句 Should we not transform... 兼顾半正式礼貌与号召力。

🎬 Model 3 · Review（科幻电影影评 Horizon Drift）｜ 正文 139 words（含标题 145 words） Band 9 · 15/15 (C6 + L9)

Task: You recently watched a new science-fiction film. Write a review for your school magazine, saying what you liked and disliked and who would enjoy it.

Horizon Drift: Stunning Spectacle, Familiar Plot

Directed by Elena Vance, Horizon Drift is a gripping sci-fi thriller set aboard a damaged research station orbiting Mars. When a solar storm severs Earth contact, commander Maya Lin must race against dwindling oxygen to save her crew.

The film's greatest triumph lies in its breathtaking cinematography and pulse-pounding sound design. Every silent spacewalk feels genuinely tense, keeping viewers on the edge of their seats without cheap explosions. Lead actor Nadia Chen also delivers a remarkably nuanced performance.

My only minor reservation is that the middle act feels overly predictable, as supporting characters repeat clichés from older space dramas. Tighter dialogue could easily have elevated the script further.

Overall, Horizon Drift is thoroughly enjoyable and well worth watching on a big screen. Fans of atmospheric space adventures will love it, though viewers seeking shocking twists might feel mildly underwhelmed.

查看考官逐段红笔拆解（Model 3 · Content 6/6 + Language 9/9）

[Content 6/6] 零剧透满分范本！首段仅用 2 句交代设定与危机；第 2 段精准评估视听与演技；第 3 段诚恳指出中段套路化与台词松散；第 4 段按两类不同观众口味精准推荐。

[Language 9/9] 过去分词开头 Directed by Elena Vance；高阶评估词 gripping, dwindling, breathtaking, nuanced, overly predictable, mildly underwhelmed 密集自然。

🔬 Model 4 · Review（新开青少年科学馆体验评价）｜ 正文 137 words（含标题 143 words） Band 9 · 15/15 (C6 + L9)

Task: A new science museum for teenagers has recently opened in your town. Write a review for a local youth magazine.

SparkLab Youth Centre: Science Comes Alive!

Tucked beside the central library, the newly opened SparkLab Interactive Science Centre promises teenagers hands-on discovery rather than dusty glass cabinets. Covering three lively floors, it explores robotics, renewable energy, and virtual space travel.

From the moment you step inside, the atmosphere feels vibrant and welcoming. The standout attraction is undoubtedly the climate-engineering simulator, which lets visitors test flood barriers in real time while friendly student guides explain the physics clearly. Every exhibit encourages curiosity rather than passive reading.

That said, the experience is not completely flawless. On Saturday afternoons, popular stations develop frustratingly long queues, and the upstairs café charges surprisingly steep prices for basic snacks.

Nevertheless, SparkLab thoroughly lives up to the hype. If you enjoy solving practical puzzles or want an inspiring weekend outing with friends, do not miss it, provided you arrive early!

查看考官逐段红笔拆解（Model 4 · Content 6/6 + Language 9/9）

[Content 6/6] 未写一句“我们先去一楼又去二楼”的流水账，而是紧扣“互动感”、“防洪模拟器亮点”、“学生讲解员”以及“周六排队 + 咖啡厅偏贵”的真实观察展开，结尾给出错峰实用建议。

[Language 9/9] Tucked beside... 与 Covering three lively floors... 双分词开头；非限制性定语从句 which lets visitors...；地道评价词伙 dusty glass cabinets, vibrant, standout attraction, steep prices, lives up to the hype。

🚨 中低分误区诊断对照 · Review 典型失分版（为什么以下片段只能拿 Band 5？） Band 5 · 7/15 (C3 + L4)

My Film Review

I am going to write a review about a very good sci-fi movie called Horizon Drift. At the beginning, the astronauts go to Mars and then a big storm comes and breaks the radio. After that, Maya repairs the oxygen tank and finally everyone flies back to Earth happily. The pictures are very nice and the music is very exciting, but some parts are a little bit boring and the actors are ok. In my opinion, everyone should watch it because it is really awesome!!!

🩺 四大扣分病因与一键抢救方案
  • 病因 1（标题与开篇无效）：My Film Review + I am going to write... 毫无杂志感 → 改成有观点对比的短标题，首句直接切入背景。
  • 病因 2（剧透流水账）：用 At the beginning... After that... finally everyone flies back happily 把结局全剧透光，且毫无评价词 → 只留前 2 句悬念设定，把篇幅让给评估！
  • 病因 3（低阶词扎堆）：连用 very good / very nice / very exciting / boring / ok / awesome!!! → 替换为 breathtaking / pulse-pounding / overly predictable / nuanced。
  • 病因 4（结尾空泛 + 多感叹号）：everyone should watch it 缺乏受众针对性且 !!! 显得稚嫩 → 改用 Fans of X will love it, though Y might...

Ex 6 Task 1 · Article 改写与限时作文（Casual Fridays）[15 marks]
Prompt: Your school is thinking about letting students wear casual clothes instead of school uniform every Friday. Write an article for the school magazine giving your views. (120–160 words)

Step 1 升格热身（试在草稿纸或下方写作台改写以下 3 处低分草稿句）：
(1) 平庸标题： Should We Have Casual Fridays? → 试改为设问+立场型吸睛标题；
(2) 平庸开头： I want to tell you about wearing casual clothes on Fridays. → 试改为设问 Hook + 校园画面；
(3) 干瘪论点： Choosing our own clothes is good for students. → 试升级为包含具体益处 + 非限制性定语从句的完整论证。
先完成 3 句升格练习，再使用上方“M05 交互写作台”限时 25 分钟写出完整 135–152 词校刊文章，然后对照参考范文与评分细则。

参考范文 · Article（正文 135 words ｜ 含标题 143 words） Band 9 · 15/15

Ditching the Tie? Why Casual Fridays Make Sense

Should self-expression end at the school gate? Every morning, hundreds of students rush into identical blazers, yet a growing student campaign now proposes introducing Casual Fridays next term.

Allowing sensible everyday clothes once a week offers real advantages. Firstly, comfortable outfits help teenagers feel relaxed during demanding science labs and creative workshops. Secondly, choosing appropriate clothing teaches independent judgement, which is an essential skill before workplace dress codes arrive.

Naturally, some parents fear fashion competition or messy appearances. However, setting clear guidelines, such as banning offensive slogans and beachwear, prevents distraction while keeping expenses low. Wearing what we already own costs nothing extra.

Uniforms undeniably build school pride, yet flexibility fosters trust. If we want responsible young adults who respect campus rules, surely letting us pick our Friday hoodies is a smart place to start?

[Content 6/6] 清晰表态并兼顾正反：正面写出实验/艺术课舒适度 + 独立着装判断力；反面回应攀比与着装散漫顾虑，并提出“禁印字标语/沙滩装 + 零额外花费”方案。
[Language 9/9] 动名词作主语（Allowing... / Choosing... / Wearing...）、非限制性定从（which is an essential skill...）、让步对比（Uniforms undeniably build..., yet flexibility fosters...）与结尾反问一气呵成。

Ex 6 Task 2 · Review 改写与限时作文（The Page and Bean）[15 marks]
Prompt: A new bookshop café for teenagers has opened near your school. Write a review for the school magazine, describing what makes it appealing, one aspect that could be improved, and who would enjoy visiting it. (120–160 words)

Step 1 去平庸改写热身（试升级以下 3 处平淡表达）：
(1) It is a nice new café with books and good drinks. → 用分词修饰 + 高阶对比结构点明特色；
(2) The upstairs room was very hot and small. → 替换为精准客观的批评名词与形容词（如 cramped, ventilation）；
(3) All students should go there after school. → 改为特定受众 + 条件限定推荐句（用 provided...）。
先完成 3 句评价词升级，再计时撰写 135–152 词完整 Review 并使用上方写作台自检，最后核对满分示范。

[升格参考]
(1) Combining sunlit wooden booths with shelves of graphic novels and study guides, it offers a refreshing alternative to noisy fast-food chains.
(2) My one disappointment was the cramped upstairs corner, where poor ventilation made afternoon studying uncomfortable during busy hours.
(3) Book lovers and diligent students will adore it, provided they grab a downstairs seat early.

参考范文 · Review（正文 136 words ｜ 含标题 146 words） Band 9 · 15/15

The Page and Bean: A Cosy Haven with Minor Flaws

Located near our school, The Page and Bean is a brand-new bookshop café designed specifically for teenage readers. Combining sunlit wooden booths with shelves of graphic novels and study guides, it offers a refreshing alternative to noisy fast-food chains.

Its strongest asset is undoubtedly the calm, welcoming ambience. Soft acoustic music plays gently, power sockets sit beside every table, and friendly staff never rush customers lingering over a single drink. Their homemade berry smoothies are genuinely delicious and reasonably priced.

My one disappointment was the cramped upstairs corner, where poor ventilation made afternoon studying uncomfortable during busy hours. Adding extra fans would resolve this effortlessly.

Overall, The Page and Bean is a delightful spot for quiet revision or relaxed weekend chats. Book lovers and diligent students will adore it, provided they grab a downstairs seat early.

[Content 6/6] 完整回应题干 3 要素：① 吸引力（安静氛围、桌边插座、友善不催单、性价比饮品）；② 改进点（楼上角落闷热拥挤 + 建议加风扇）；③ 推荐人群（爱书者与认真复习的学生，提醒早到占楼下座位）。
[Language 9/9] 分词短语（Located near... / Combining...）、非限制性定从（where poor ventilation...）、条件连词（provided they grab...）与精准评价词 ambience, cramped, refreshing alternative, reasonably priced 协同拿满 9/9。
