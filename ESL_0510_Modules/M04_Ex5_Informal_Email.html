4.1 Ex 5 非正式邮件：15 分双轨评分拆解、120–160 词铁律与语域把控

2024–2027 Untiered Paper 1 · Exercise 5 Informal Email (15 Marks: Content 6 + Language 9)
Paper 1 · Ex 5 (15 分) 严格词数：120–160 words 建议考场配时：30–35 分钟
📏 2024–2027 新考纲铁律：为什么 120–160 词与“双轨评分”是提分关键？

在 2024–2027 新版 0510 / 0511 试卷中，取消了旧版 Core (100–150 词) 与 Extended (150–200 词) 的字数差异，全体考生在 Ex 5（非正式邮件）统一要求 120–160 words，总分 15 分 = Content 内容 6 分 + Language 语言 9 分。虽然考官不会一到第 161 词就一刀切，但低于 120 词通常意味着 3 条要点没展开（Content 直接掉至 3–4 分）；而超过 160 词容易在考场超时、逻辑散漫并拉高语法出错率。最稳健的冲刺区间是 135–150 words！

Content (6 分) + Language (9 分) 官方评分维度拆解

评分轨道 考官高分标准 (Band 9: 5–6 / 8–9) 低分失分病症 (Band 5 及以下) 考场破局公式
Content · 内容分
满分 6 分		 ① 紧扣全部 3 条 Bullet Points，不漏要点、不偏题；
② 每条要点均有细节、原因或感受延展；
③ 有真实的读者意识（Audience & Purpose）：像真的在写信给老友。 		把 3 个要点当成问答提纲，每点只回 1 句干瘪陈述；或者前 50 词全是无关寒暄，第 3 点只剩一句话草草收场。 1 Point = 1 Mini-Story / Reason + Feeling（每条要点写“发生了什么 + 具体小细节 + 你的情绪反应”）。
Language · 语言分
满分 9 分		 ① 语域精准（Informal Register）：自然亲切、善用缩略词；
② 地道词伙：短语动词（pick up, turn out）与适度习语（over the moon）；
③ 句式多样且准确：感叹句、反问句、定语从句与让步转折自然融合。 		像写商业公函或正式报告（I am writing to inform you... / Yours faithfully），或者反复堆砌 very good / very happy / very tired。 全篇至少配置：3+ 个自然缩略词 + 2 个短语动词 + 1 句感叹或亲切提问 + 2 个以上复合句。
Ex 5 要点扩写核心引擎：1 Point = Mini-Story / Concrete Reason + Personal Feeling
平淡一句 ❌ → 场景动作 + 具体名词/时间 + 情绪共鸣 ✅
例如要点要求 say what challenged you most：
❌ 干瘪流水账 (9 词)：I felt nervous when I played music for people.
✅ 满分扩写 (20 词)：What surprised me most was how nerve-racking presenting our track to thirty strangers felt, yet everyone cheered us on!

Informal Register（非正式友爱语域）红黑榜对照

很多中国考生受“写议论文/Report 要高大上”思维影响，给好朋友写信却用成客服公文；另一些同学则走向另一个极端——把手机短信缩写（u, thx, gonna, lol）写进考卷。记住：Informal Email = Warm, Chatty & Standard English（亲切口语化，但拼写语法依然规范标准）：

功能部位 ❌ 过分生硬正式（掉 Language 分） ❌ 过度短信俚语（扣规范分） ✅ Band 9 自然友爱语域（满分示范）
称呼与开篇 Dear Friend, I am writing to inform you about an event. Yo bro!! sup, u won't believe dis lol Hi Maya, How are things going? Guess what! I've just got back from...
缩略词规范 全程写死 I am / I have / did not / would not 滥用 wanna / gonna / kinda / ain't 自然使用标准缩略：I'm, I've, wasn't, you'd, let's, don't
承接过渡 Furthermore, / Secondly, / In conclusion, btw / tbh / anywayz Believe it or not, / What surprised me most was... / Anyway,
情感升级 It was very good and I was very happy. It was sick / lit / crazy af! I was over the moon! / It was worth every penny / lifted my mood.
结尾与落款 I look forward to your reply. Yours faithfully, cya l8r! Let me know what you think! Catch up soon, / Write back soon,

真题级对比 Set 1 · 推荐周末电子音乐与编程创意工作坊

📋 Prompt 1 审题框（120–160 words）

You recently attended a weekend digital music and coding workshop. Write an email to your penfriend Maya about it. In your email, you should:

  • say what the workshop involved and what you created
  • explain what surprised you or challenged you most
  • suggest why she should try a similar workshop together next time.
✅ Version A · Band 9 满分示范（实测 146 words · 黄金区间） 预估得分：14–15 / 15（Content 6 + Language 8–9）

Hi Maya,

How are things going? Guess what! I've just got back from a weekend digital music workshop at our town's youth hub, and I couldn't wait to tell you all about it!

On Saturday morning, a sound producer showed us how to mix drum loops with live keyboard tracks using simple coding software. By Sunday afternoon, my partner and I had actually produced our own upbeat two-minute dance track! What surprised me most wasn't the tech side, though. It was how nerve-racking presenting our track to thirty strangers felt, yet everyone cheered us on!

Honestly, you'd be over the moon if you tried one next month. Not only do you pick up real editing skills without needing any experience, but it's also a brilliant way to recharge your batteries after mock exams. Are you free to sign up together in October?

Catch up soon,
Leo

⚠️ Version B · Band 5 流水账对照（实测 105 words · 未达 120 词底线） 预估得分：8 / 15（Content 4 + Language 4）

Dear Maya,

I am writing to inform you about a digital music workshop that I attended last weekend. First, the teacher taught us to use a computer program to make music. I made a short song with another student.

Second, I was surprised because there were many people in the room and I felt nervous when I played my song. However, the workshop was very good and I felt very happy at the end.

Finally, I recommend that you join a similar workshop next month because it is very useful for learning new things. Please tell me if you want to go.

Yours faithfully,
Leo

🔍 考官红笔逐条批注：为什么 Version A 拿 15 分而 Version B 只有 8 分？
  • 词数与 Content 饱满度：Version A 共 146 词，用 drum loops / two-minute dance track / thirty strangers / cheered us on 把经历与感受写活；Version B 只有 105 词（低于 120 词红线），3 个要点全部停留在“提纲一句话”，缺少画面与真情实感。
  • 语域灾难（Register Clash）：Version B 出现 I am writing to inform you / First, Second, Finally / Yours faithfully，完全是正式商业公函或议论文的语气，与“写给笔友”严重违背；Version A 用 Guess what! / I've / wasn't / you'd / Catch up soon 营造出地道朋友聊天的温度。
  • 词汇与句式落差：Version B 连续重复三次 very + 简单词（very good / very happy / very useful）；Version A 使用了短语动词 pick up、sign up、习语 over the moon、recharge your batteries，以及 Not only do you... 倒装和 What surprised me most was... 主语从句！

真题级对比 Set 2 · 向好友道歉失约并提议新计划

📋 Prompt 2 审题框（120–160 words）

You had arranged to meet your friend Alex on Saturday afternoon, but you had to cancel at the last minute. Write an email to Alex. In your email, you should:

  • apologise and explain why you had to cancel
  • say how you felt about missing your plans together
  • suggest a detailed new plan for next weekend to make up for it.
✅ Version A · Band 9 满分示范（实测 143 words · 黄金区间） 预估得分：15 / 15（Content 6 + Language 9）

Hey Alex,

I'm terribly sorry for cancelling our cinema trip at the last minute on Saturday! My younger brother took a nasty tumble off his bike that morning, and my parents had to rush him to the local clinic while I stayed behind to look after our dog. Luckily, he only bruised his elbow.

I felt awful about letting you down, especially since we'd both been looking forward to watching that new sci-fi thriller for weeks. Believe it or not, I spent the whole afternoon staring at my phone wishing I were eating popcorn with you instead!

Anyway, let me make it up to you this coming Saturday. How about grabbing lunch at that cosy noodle place near the station before catching the three o'clock screening? My treat for the snacks! Let me know if that works for you.

Write back soon,
Chloe

⚠️ Version B · Band 5 流水账对照（实测 100 words · 未达 120 词底线） 预估得分：7–8 / 15（Content 4 + Language 3–4）

Dear Alex,

I am very sorry that I could not meet you on Saturday to watch the film. My brother fell off his bicycle in the morning and went to the hospital, so I had to stay at home.

I was very sad that I missed the movie because I wanted to see it. I hope you are not very angry with me about this bad situation.

Next weekend I suggest we go to the cinema on Saturday afternoon and have lunch together before the film starts. Please reply to my email soon to confirm your schedule.

Best regards,
Chloe

🚫 踩坑警醒：道歉信最忌“公关腔”与“空洞计划”

Version B 用 confirm your schedule 和 Best regards 像在给客户发商务邮件，显得冷漠生分，而且 Bullet 3（"suggest a detailed new plan"）没有时间点、没有具体餐厅和补偿心意；Version A 巧妙运用了 let you down（让你失望）、make it up to you（补偿你）、wishing I were...（虚拟语气表达懊恼）以及具体的 cosy noodle place near the station + 3 o'clock screening + My treat for the snacks!，情绪真挚、细节满分，143 词一气呵成！

4.3 语域诊断刷题与 Ex 5 考场仿真写作实操

🎯 第一部分：Ex 5 语域诊断与句型升级互动单选（Questions 1–6）
Ex 5 诊断 · Q1 1 mark · Register Check
1. Which opening sentence is MOST suitable for an informal email to a close friend about joining a robotics club?
以下哪句开头最适合用于给好朋友写非正式邮件（讲述参加机器人社团）？
🔍 查看解析：三种语域高下对比
  • A 错（过分正式）：I am writing... in order to inform you 是正式信函/投诉信套话。
  • B 对（黄金语域）：自然热情、自带悬念钩子（You'll never guess...）+ 规范英文。
  • C 错（过度随意）：IGCSE 虽考 Informal Register，但仍严格考核标准拼写与标点，严禁 u、asap、lol 或连续感叹号。
Ex 5 诊断 · Q2 1 mark · Content Development
2. Bullet point: "describe the best part of the camping trip." Which response scores highest on Content & Language?
针对要点“描述露营中最棒的部分”，哪一句在内容展开与语言上得分最高？
🔍 查看解析：Content 扩写三要素
得分规律 A 只交待了事实骨架；B 把非正式邮件写成了生态调查报告；只有 C 实现了“画面细节 + 个人感受”双落地。
Ex 5 诊断 · Q3 1 mark · Lexical Upgrade
3. How can you best upgrade the flat Band 5 sentence: "I was very tired after the hike, but it was very good"?
如何把干瘪的 Band 5 句子 "I was very tired after the hike, but it was very good" 升级为地道高分表达？
🔍 查看解析：告别 very 的非正式高频替换
用短语动词 worn out / knackered（注意 worn out 更稳妥通用）代替 very tired，用 worth every penny / worth every step 代替 very good，能立即提升 Language 分。
Ex 5 诊断 · Q4 1 mark · Sign-off Convention
4. Which closing and sign-off pairing violates the tone of an Informal Email to a friend?
以下哪组结尾句与落款搭配违背了写给朋友的非正式邮件语域？
🔍 查看解析：Ex 5 收尾黄金百搭句
考试时闭眼选用 A 或 B 即可稳稳得分：Write back soon, / Take care, / Best wishes, / Catch up soon,；而 Yours sincerely / faithfully 留给极少数正式信函或完全不用于 2024+ Ex 5。
Ex 5 诊断 · Q5 1 mark · Word Count Diagnosis
5. A student finishes her Ex 5 email in 104 words and hits all three bullet points briefly. What should she do during editing?
一名考生写完 Ex 5 后发现全篇只有 104 词，但 3 条要点都简单点到了。修改时她最应该怎么做？
🔍 查看解析：如何科学凑够黄金 140 词不注水
不要在开头和结尾硬塞“最近你家小狗好不好”，而是向主体段要字数：给每个要点补一句 “具体怎么发生的 + 当时心情 + 对朋友说的一句互动话”，轻轻松松多出 35 个高含金量词。
Ex 5 诊断 · Q6 1 mark · Natural Transition
6. Which linker creates the most natural conversational shift between describing a chaotic moment and offering advice?
在描述完一场手忙脚乱的小插曲后，用哪个过渡句引出给朋友的建议最自然流畅？
🔍 查看解析：非正式邮件的润滑剂
Anyway,、Speaking of which,、On top of that,、Believe it or not, 是 Ex 5 替代生硬 Furthermore / In conclusion 的四大神器。

✍️ 第二部分：2 道全真考场仿真 Ex 5 写作任务（请在上方交互写作台试写后对照范文！）

📝 仿真写作任务 A · International Food Festival Volunteering (120–160 words)

Task Prompt: Last Sunday you helped out as a volunteer at an international food festival in your town. Write an email to your penfriend Sam about the experience. In your email, you should:

  • say what tasks you helped with at the festival
  • describe an unexpected problem that happened and how you solved it
  • explain why Sam would enjoy volunteering at a similar event next year.

动笔前 60 秒审题清单：① 主时态 = 一般过去时（前两点）+ 一般现在/将来条件句（第3点）；② 想好一个生动小危机（如刮风吹跑菜单/洒了酱汁/停电）；③ 在上方“M04 交互写作台”写够 135–150 词 再点开参考范文！

⭐ 展开自评核对表 + 参考 Band 9 范文（实测 145 words）
考场自评 5 项必勾清单（全勾稳拿 13–15/15）：
✅ 词数严格在 120–160 之间？ ✅ 三个要点各自至少 2 句（含画面+感受）？ ✅ 至少 3 个自然缩略词（如 I'd, won't, you'd）？ ✅ 至少 2 个短语动词/习语（如 hand out, run a stall, lift your mood）？ ✅ 结尾有面向朋友的问句与友好落款？
✅ Task A · 参考 Band 9 满分范文（实测 145 words） Content 6/6 · Language 9/9

Hi Sam,

How's everything going? You won't believe how hectic yet brilliant my Sunday was! I volunteered at our town's first International Food Festival!

I'd expected to spend the day handing out leaflets, so I was thrilled when the organiser asked me to run the Thai dessert stall with two classmates. Things went smoothly until a sudden gust of wind blew away half our menu cards and knocked over a stack of paper cups! Instead of panicking, we borrowed heavy stones from a nearby planter to weigh down the posters and turned the mess into a joke with queuing customers.

Honestly, you'd love volunteering at something like this next summer. Not only do you pick up teamwork skills and sample amazing street food for free, but the buzzing atmosphere also lifts your mood for days. Would you fancy teaming up next year?

Take care,
Hannah

📝 仿真写作任务 B · Joining a New Sports Club & Inviting a Friend (120–160 words)

Task Prompt: After moving to a new neighbourhood, you recently joined a local sports club. Write an email to your old friend Noah about it. In your email, you should:

  • describe what the club and its facilities are like
  • say what you enjoy most about going there and anything you miss from before
  • suggest what you and Noah can do together when he visits next month.

动笔前 60 秒审题清单：① 第 2 个要点包含两个小分支（最喜欢什么 + 怀念以前的什么），千万不能漏掉“miss from before”！② 第 3 点必须给出可落地的周末游玩组合。

⭐ 展开自评核对表 + 参考 Band 9 范文（实测 138 words）
审题易漏项检查： Bullet 2 是典型“双问要点”——只写喜欢新俱乐部、忘写怀念旧日时光（miss），Content 会被直接扣掉 1 分！
✅ Task B · 参考 Band 9 满分范文（实测 138 words） Content 6/6 · Language 8–9/9

Hey Noah,

Long time no speak! Now that we've finally unpacked in our new neighbourhood, I've joined a lively community sports club down the road, and it's already my favourite weekend hangout.

The place looks bright and modern, featuring an indoor climbing wall and floodlit outdoor badminton courts that stay open late. What I love most is how welcoming our coach is, although I definitely miss our noisy five-a-side matches after school! Still, having friendly teammates nearby has helped me settle in way faster than I expected.

Since you're visiting next month, why don't we book the climbing wall on Saturday morning and grab huge burgers at the club café afterwards? I've already got a guest pass lined up for you, so don't forget your trainers! Let me know which weekend suits you best.

See you soon,
Marcus

抛弃单调的 I suggest we go...，改用 Would you fancy teaming up? 或 How about grabbing lunch before...?，问号互动感拉满。

开头收尾 #04Sign-off
Write back soon, / Catch up soon, / Take care, / Best wishes,

先加 1 句期待回复（Let me know if that works for you! / Drop me a line soon!），再配 Catch up soon, 或 Take care, + 名字（切勿写 Yours faithfully）。

自然过渡 #08Emphasis Frame
What surprised / impressed / helped me most was...

用 What 引导的主语从句 既口语自然又结构高级（如 What I love most is how welcoming our coach is），比干写 I love our coach most 加分显著。
