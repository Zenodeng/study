1.1 2024–2027 新考纲全景：0510 vs 0511 与 120 分钟配时

2024–2027 Untiered Syllabus Architecture, Weightings & Paper 1 Time Allocation
2024–2027 统一试卷 · 已取消 Core/Extended 分级 Paper 1 · 2 小时 / 60 分 (Ex 1–6)

一、2024–2027 新考纲重大变革与 0510 / 0511 精准对比

从 2024 年考季起，Cambridge IGCSE English as a Second Language（0510 与 0511）正式进入同卷评估时代（Untiered Syllabus）：全面取消原有的 Core 与 Extended 分级试卷，所有考生统一作答同一套难度渐进的试卷，最终成绩覆盖完整等级 A*–G（部分地区采用 9–1 分制）。两套代码考题内容、时长与卷面满分完全一致，唯一区别在于 Paper 3 Speaking（口语）是否计入总分权重。

考卷 (Component) 考查题型与时长 卷面满分 0510 (Speaking Endorsement) 0511 (Count-in Speaking)
Paper 1: Reading & Writing Ex 1–4 阅读 (30 分) + Ex 5–6 写作 (30 分) · 2 hours 60 marks 70% 总评权重 50% 总评权重
Paper 2: Listening Ex 1–5 全部为客观选择题 · 约 50 min（含涂卡/检查） 40 marks 30% 总评权重 25% 总评权重
Paper 3: Speaking Part 1 访谈 + Part 2 短讲 + Part 3 讨论 · 约 10–15 min 40 marks 0%（独立等级认证 Grade 1–5）
Grade 1 最高，Grade 5 最低		 25% 直接计入 A*–G 总评
🔍 0510 vs 0511 选考避坑与核心差异

1. 0510 口语不是“免考”：考生仍必须参加 Paper 3，证书上会单独印出 Speaking Endorsement Grade 1–5（注意：剑桥规则中 1 级最高，5 级最低，未达 5 级标准则无等级认证），但它不拖累也不拉升你的 Paper 1+2 书面总等级。

2. 0511 三卷合力冲刺：口语直接占总成绩 25%，Paper 1 降至 50%、Paper 2 降至 25%。对口语流畅自然、但长篇写作容易拼写或语法失分的考生，0511 往往更容易稳住 A / A*；反之，若口语临场容易紧张卡顿，0510 把 70% 权重锁定在可反复打磨的 Paper 1 上更具确定性。

二、Paper 1（120 分钟 / 60 分）黄金配时表与“卡题止损铁律”

Paper 1 既考快速精准阅读，又考两篇高质量实用文体写作。整卷 60 分中，Ex 1–4 阅读占 30 分，Ex 5–6 写作占 30 分。很多考生失利并非英语水平不够，而是前四道阅读耗时超过 70 分钟，导致最后只剩 20 分钟仓促完成占分一半的写作。

⏱️ 考场铁律：45 分钟阅读硬止损线 (The 90-Second Stop-Loss Rule)

阅读前四题单题仅值 1 分。若你在 Ex 1–4 任意一道 1 分题上纠结超过 90 秒 仍无把握，立即在题号旁标记星号 ★ 并果断跳过！第 45 分钟铃响（或自备指针表走到 45 分点）必须强制转入 Ex 5 写作——用 1 分换回写作从容列提纲、打磨高级句型与查错的 35 分钟，是冲刺 A* 的核心节奏控制术。

1.2 Ex 1 简答题满分 4 步定位法与反冗余铁律

Exercise 1 (8 Marks): 4-Step Precision Scanning, Lift-Trimming & Contradiction Defence
Paper 1 Ex 1 · 8 Marks Q1–5 (各 1 分) + Q6 (3 分)

一、Ex 1 满分四步定位流水线

Exercise 1 通常基于一篇约 400–450 词的说明文、报道或博客文章，设 6 道简答题（Q1–Q5 各 1 分，最后一道 Q6 通常要求 Give three details / reasons 占 3 分，共 8 分）。它不考你能否写出优美的长难句，只考你能否在最短时间内精准锁定唯一事实并干净作答。

  1. 第一步 · 圈出疑问词与双限定词（Lock Wh-word & Limiters）：动笔读文前先扫全部 6 题，圈出提问方向（How / Why / What / When / Where / Who）以及隐蔽限定词：Apart from X（除了 X 之外）、originally / currently（过去还是现在）、Give three details（必须 3 个不同要点）。
  2. 第二步 · 按自然段顺序往下扫读（Sequential Paragraph Scan）：剑桥 Ex 1 的出题顺序几乎 100% 与文章从头到尾的段落顺序一致。Q1 落在第 1–2 段，Q2 紧接其后，Q6 往往在倒数 1–2 段。找完 Q2 后直接从该句往下扫 Q3，绝不从头盲找！
  3. 第三步 · 极简精准抽词法则（Lift-and-Trim Rule）：Ex 1 不要求写完整主谓宾句子，写单词或核心短语即可拿满分！当原文是复杂长句时，把修饰性从句和转折废话削掉，仅保留回答疑问词的最小精确单元。
  4. 第四步 · 反冗余与反矛盾终检（Contradiction Check）：紧盯是否漏了单位（metres / 1998 / 4.30 pm）、是否误抄了题干 Apart from... 里已经排除的项目、是否带进了无关甚至自相矛盾的词句。
Ex 1 满分答题公式（极简精准抽词法则）：
Target Fact Phrase (+ Required Unit / Verb) − Irrelevant Clause = 100% Mark Scheme Safe
核心原理：剑桥官方 Mark Scheme（评分细则）明确执行 “Contradiction / Over-copying Penalty”——只要你在正确答案后面多抄了错误、无关或含糊的干扰信息，使考官无法判断你是否真的理解，整道题直接判 0 分！

二、Ex 1 五大常见失分雷区对照表

失分雷区 考场常见错误示范 Mark Scheme 判分逻辑与正确改写
1. 贪多整句照抄（带出冗余/矛盾） 问 What time does the ferry leave on Sundays?
写 At 7.30 am on weekdays or 9.15 am on Sundays.		 把平日期干扰信息 7.30 am 一并抄入，考官判定考生未能甄别限定词 Sunday → 0 分。
正确：9.15 am
2. 漏写单位、时段或关键修饰词 问 How long is the wooden bridge?
写 450		 缺乏计量单位，450 可能是米、厘米或步数 → 0 分。
正确：450 metres（或 450m）
3. 中了 Apart from 排已知陷阱 问 Apart from free entry, why did families visit?
写 Because entry was free and there were craft stalls.		 题干 Apart from free entry 已明确排除“免费”，若再写它凑数或替代新点 → 0 分。
正确：craft stalls
4. 误把否定前提/昔日观念当答案 原文：Many assumed the cave collapsed due to heavy rain, yet geologists proved tree roots cracked the roof.
问 Why did the cave collapse? → 答 heavy rain		 忽略转折词 yet，把被推翻的猜测当成事实 → 0 分。
正确：tree roots (cracked the roof)
5. 多分题不分点或“一意拆两点” 问 Give three benefits of cycling to school. [3]
写 It saves money, costs less and keeps you fit.		 saves money 与 costs less 语义完全重合，考官仅记 1 分！→ 共得 2/3 分。
正确：分三行用分号列 3 个互不重叠的独立要点。
💡 Worked Example · 亲历考官视角对比“整句照抄” vs “极简精准抽词”

原文片段：“Although the community bakery originally opened solely to train unemployed teenagers in 2018, rising flour costs nearly forced it to close last winter; thankfully, a crowd-funded grant from local residents kept the ovens running.”

题干：Apart from training young people, how did the bakery survive last winter? [1]

  • ✗ 危险写法（0 分）：“It opened to train unemployed teenagers and rising flour costs nearly closed it, then residents gave a grant.”（混入题干已排除的培训目的及无关背景，冗长且焦点模糊）
  • ✓ 满分写法（1 分）：(a) crowd-funded grant (from local residents) —— 6 个词内直击核心方式，零风险！

Passage A + B · 各 ~430 词仿真文本 12 题 Mark Scheme + 6 道交互单选

一、全真阅读模拟 Passage A（~430 词 · 8 分）

建议用时：10 分钟内完成 Q1–Q6。答题前先划出疑问词与限定词，作答尽量压缩至最短精确短语，随后展开官方标准 Mark Scheme 逐词核对。

Night Markets of Penang: Where Heritage Meets Midnight Appetites

A As dusk settles over George Town on Malaysia’s Penang Island, quiet side streets undergo a sudden transformation. Folding tables clatter open, strings of warm bulbs flicker into life, and the sharp scent of toasted spices drifts above the pavement as hungry families stroll out from nearby blocks. Known locally as pasar malam, Penang’s roving night markets do not remain in one fixed spot all week; instead, each neighbourhood hosts its stalls on a designated evening, ensuring that even suburban residents far from the city centre can walk to a bustling market without boarding a bus.

B Historians trace the island’s nocturnal trading culture back more than a century, when harbour dockworkers and tin-mine labourers finished late shifts after sunset and needed inexpensive hot meals. Today, tourists often assume that colourful lanterns and pop music are the main attraction for visitors. Yet a recent municipal survey revealed a different priority: over seventy percent of overseas guests attend primarily to sample regional dishes cooked openly in front of them, rather than to buy souvenirs or clothing.

C Among the busiest stalls is one operated by 68-year-old Lim Wei Kang, who fries flat rice noodles over charcoal flames. While gas burners would speed up service and lower his fuel bills, Lim refuses to abandon hardwood charcoal, insisting that only glowing embers create the subtle smoky aroma regulars recognise instantly. His daughter, Mei Ling, initially doubted whether young shoppers would appreciate such old-fashioned methods; however, long queues every Friday evening convinced her to join the family stall full-time after leaving her office job in Kuala Lumpur.

D Keeping an open-air street market safe and spotless is no simple matter. Until 2021, overflowing litter bins and blocked traffic lanes regularly drew complaints from nearby apartment blocks, especially during humid weekend rushes. To solve these tensions, the local council introduced a strict charter requiring every vendor to bring portable grease traps, pack away waste before midnight, and leave a two-metre emergency walkway down the centre of every road.

E Beyond lively late-night dining, planners now see these markets as vital social glue across the island’s diverse neighbourhoods. First-time entrepreneurs can test new recipes at a fraction of the cost of renting a permanent café, turning wooden pushcarts into stepping stones for family businesses. Retired artisans teach teenagers how to fold traditional banana-leaf parcels, and neighbours from different cultural backgrounds gather around shared plastic stools to swap stories long after formal shops have pulled down their shutters.

Passage A · Q1[1 mark]
Why do Penang’s night markets move to a different neighbourhood each evening?
【审题提示】定位第 A 段末句，提取最终让郊区居民受益的结果，不要照抄“一周不固定在一个地方”的同义重复。
查看 Mark Scheme 得分短语与拒分警告

Accept (1) (so) suburban residents can walk to a market / people far from the city centre can reach one without taking a bus / locals don’t have to board a bus to attend.

Reject "they do not remain in one fixed spot all week"（只重述了题干现象，未回答 Why 目的）。

解析：第 A 段结尾 ensuring that... 后面即为原因与目的；提取“郊区居民不用乘公交就能步行到达”即可拿 1 分。
Passage A · Q2[1 mark]
Who originally depended on late-night street food in Penang over a hundred years ago? Give one group or both.
【审题提示】圈出疑问词 Who 与时间限定 over a hundred years ago，顺读至第 B 段首句。
查看 Mark Scheme 得分短语与拒分警告

Accept (1) (harbour) dockworkers / (tin-mine) labourers（写出任意一类或两类均得 1 分）。

Reject "tourists / overseas guests"（这是 Today 的人群，违背百年前的时间限定）。

解析：more than a century 同义改写题干的 over a hundred years ago；锁定主语群体名称即可，不必把“下晚班需要热饭”也抄进去。
Passage A · Q3[1 mark]
According to the municipal survey, what is the main reason international visitors go to the night markets?
【审题提示】留意第 B 段转折词 Yet，切勿被前面的“人们常以为（assume）”误导！
查看 Mark Scheme 得分短语与拒分警告

Accept (1) to try / sample regional / local dishes (cooked in front of them) / for the local food.

Reject "colourful lanterns and pop music"（这是前面被推翻的错误假设）；"to buy souvenirs or clothing"（原文用 rather than 明确排除）。

解析：典型的 assume ... Yet ... rather than ... 双重否定排雷题，答案精准落在 primarily to sample regional dishes...。
Passage A · Q4[1 mark]
Why does Lim Wei Kang prefer using hardwood charcoal instead of gas burners?
【审题提示】顺读至第 C 段，区分“gas 的优点（快且省钱）”和“charcoal 不可替代的优势”。
查看 Mark Scheme 得分短语与拒分警告

Accept (1) it creates a (subtle) smoky aroma / flavour.

Reject "it speeds up service and lowers fuel bills"（张冠李戴，这是 gas burners 的优点）。

解析：只需 4 个词 (creates a) smoky aroma。若顺手连带前面 While gas burners... 一整句照抄，会直接触发矛盾扣分！
Passage A · Q5[1 mark]
Apart from using portable grease traps and clearing waste before midnight, what rule must stallholders follow under the new council charter?
【审题提示】圈出 Apart from...！绝对不能再抄“隔油器”或“午夜前清垃圾”，还要注意数字与单位完整！
查看 Mark Scheme 得分短语与拒分警告

Accept (1) leave a two-metre / 2m emergency walkway (down the centre of every road).

Reject "leave a two walkway"（漏掉单位 metre/m 扣分）；或误答已排除的 "portable grease traps / pack away waste"。

解析：第 D 段最后一句列了三项规定，前两项已被题干 Apart from 锁定排除，必须精准保留 two-metre 单位。
Passage A · Q6[3 marks]
Apart from providing lively late-night food, how do Penang’s night markets benefit the community today? Give three details.
【审题提示】3 分多分题！先圈出 Apart from providing lively late-night food，再从第 E 段抽出 3 个互相独立的社区益处分点列写。
查看 Mark Scheme 得分短语（3 个独立得分点）

1 mark each (max 3)

  1. (first-time) entrepreneurs / new businesses can test recipes cheaply / at low cost（新创业者低成本试菜，比租永久咖啡馆便宜）
  2. retired artisans teach teenagers traditional skills / how to fold banana-leaf parcels（退休手艺人向青少年传授传统包装手艺）
  3. neighbours from different cultures gather / socialise / share stories（不同文化背景的邻居聚会交流）

Reject 把“test new recipes”和“cheaper than renting a café”拆成两点（两者共同构成第 1 点，拆开只计 1 分）。

二、全真阅读模拟 Passage B（~435 词 · 8 分）

建议用时：10 分钟内完成 Q7–Q12。注意科研说明文里的专业对比细节与转折限定。

Acoustic Ecology: Listening to the Pulse of Vanishing Forests

A Walk into a tropical rainforest at dawn and you enter a wall of sound before you spot a single animal. Frogs chirp in rhythmic choruses, cicadas buzz like miniature saws, and gibbons broadcast echoing calls across the canopy. Traditionally, wildlife biologists measured an ecosystem’s health by counting visible creatures along muddy trails or examining images from motion-triggered cameras. Yet dense foliage hides most nocturnal species, meaning visual surveys often miss up to half the animals actually living in the trees.

B Over the past decade, a growing branch of science called acoustic ecology has offered a quieter alternative. Instead of sending large teams trampling through fragile undergrowth with heavy equipment, conservationists strap weatherproof audio boxes—no larger than a paperback novel—onto high branches. Powered by compact lithium batteries, each recorder wakes automatically for one minute in every ten, capturing high-frequency bat clicks as well as deep elephant rumbles for up to three consecutive months before anyone needs to return along muddy tracks to retrieve the memory card.

C Analysing thousands of hours of raw forest audio used to require teams of trained graduate students wearing headphones for weeks on end. Today, machine-learning software scans a whole month of recordings in under twenty minutes. Rather than listening only for one famous bird, the computer draws a visual "soundscape" showing how low, middle, and high frequencies are shared. In an untouched forest, every creature occupies its own acoustic bandwidth so signals never collide; when logging or road noise intrudes, empty silent gaps appear on the chart almost immediately.

D Acoustic monitoring does not merely catalogue disappearing wildlife; it can stop illegal damage while it is still happening. In parts of Borneo, solar-assisted microphones mounted high above the canopy connect continuously to rural mobile networks. When an algorithm recognises the unmistakable metallic whine of a chainsaw—distinguishing it cleanly from thunder or falling branches—it sends a GPS alert straight to park rangers’ phones within ninety seconds, allowing nearby patrols to intercept loggers before rare hardwood trees are felled.

E Nevertheless, sound scientists warn that microphones cannot solve every conservation headache on their own. Heavy monsoon rain drumming on metal casings still obscures quiet amphibian calls during stormy months, and local communities must trust how acoustic data is used, since residents worry recordings might accidentally invade village privacy. Even so, by turning every forest into a continuous live broadcast, acoustic ecology lets protectors hear trouble long before trees vanish from satellite maps.

Passage B · Q7[1 mark]
Why are traditional visual surveys often inaccurate in dense rainforests?
【审题提示】顺读至第 A 段结尾，区分“过去如何调查”与“为什么漏算动物”。
查看 Mark Scheme 得分短语与拒分警告

Accept (1) dense foliage / thick leaves hide(s) (most nocturnal) species / animals.

Reject "counting visible creatures along muddy trails"（这是调查方法，不是不准确的原因）。

解析：第 A 段末句 dense foliage hides most nocturnal species 直接给出因果。
Passage B · Q8[1 mark]
How long can an audio recorder operate in the forest before its memory card has to be collected?
【审题提示】注意第 B 段有多个时间/比例数字（one minute in every ten vs three months），圈准 before its memory card has to be collected！
查看 Mark Scheme 得分短语与拒分警告
